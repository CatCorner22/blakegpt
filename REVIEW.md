# ControlGuard specification review

**Decision:** Build the deterministic local review tools. Do not ship the spec as an authoritative legal corpus or a production SaaS architecture. Several requirements still need definitions, source validation, and measurements.

## Review plan and evidence

This full review covers the supplied *Frontier SMB GRC & ControlGuard Enterprise Suite — Master LLM Knowledge-Transfer Specification* (read, §§1–9). The readers are the product owner, implementation team, and counsel. The first priorities are correct arithmetic, defensible legal sourcing, and clear limits on local sign-offs. The review compares exact formula and regex text, checks decision-critical claims against linked sources, and tests the local implementation separately. The open product choices appear below. No check establishes legal compliance.

| ID | Status and evidence | Repair and owner |
|---|---|---|
| F1 · High | **Verified spec gap.** §4.1 names “High Survival Score” without thresholds. The formula can yield −2 to 39; “six-factor” counts Impact × Likelihood as one term. §4.1 requires three proofs; §9.6 also requires ledger backing. | Product: publish the tier thresholds (local rubric: ≥24, ≥16, ≥9, else Tier 4), accepted raw score range, and proof/ledger scope. |
| F2 · High | **Verified contradiction.** §4.2 says lapse restores `C → 1.00`, “escalating suppressed risks back to P1 Critical.” A lapse yields P3 for I=5 and E=4. Literal P2/P3 upper bounds also leave gaps for scores such as 59.95. “2 consecutive Fridays” and a rolling 336 hours can produce different deadlines. | Product: use contiguous cuts at 60, 35 and 15 and rerun the formula after lapse. Local tool uses rolling UTC hours. Define any Friday timezone, cutoff, weekend policy and notification separately. |
| F3 · High | **Verified design gap.** §4.2 calls its SHA-256 chain “immutable.” A party who controls the whole store can delete the tail or recompute every hash. A name does not authenticate the signer. The spec does not give an ACRR pair conflict matrix or a rule for weights. | Controls owner: approve a pair matrix by asset; local tool screens any two distinct vertices in one asset, and takes I/E from the reviewer. For production, require verified user identity, challenge-bound WebAuthn and an independently anchored ledger head. |
| F4 · High | **Verified incomplete detector.** §4.3 says “or cumulative CUSUM” but supplies neither recursion/reference drift nor threshold `h`. Zero PO price makes TW-01 relative variance undefined. Epsilon in the pack formula produces a finite, potentially huge but commercially unmeaningful answer for a zero pack. The spec lacks currency, rounding, credits, and multi-line invoice aggregation rules. | Finance: define estimator, calibration data, return handling and rounding before treating results as recovery amounts. Local TW-01 uses only skew and ceiling counts and rejects zero PO prices. Local truth pricing flags packs below epsilon. |
| F5 · High | **Verified ambiguity.** §4.4 calls dates a “dual-clock” but defines one expiration and one notice cliff, without calendar convention, inclusive/exclusive handling, renewals, business days or timezone. §4.5 equates leave days with shifts while §9.4 would sweep in clock and legal deadlines. | Product and counsel: define anniversary vs inclusive expiry and clock/working-day distinctions at the field level. Keep statutory day units intact; use shift conversion only for shift-based leave. Confirm local examples against the actual contract or policy. |
| F6 · Medium | **Verified partial coverage.** §7.2's two printed title examples **match** its regex. They do not demonstrate formats that fail, such as an apostrophe in a title. Python `\s` accepts newlines; `\d` accepts Unicode digits. §7.1 names only a subset of “63 banned legalese terms” and “66” vague terms. §5.4 does not define any of the 16 FT patterns or the per-register synonym mappings. | Drafting owner: publish positive and negative fixtures, explicit single-line/character rules, complete lexical inventories, and FT definitions. The local check uses the given expression and marks its lexicon incomplete. |
| F7 · High | **Verified holding, overstated claim.** §7.1 cites *Smith v. Spizzirri* to support a commercial-contract drafting rule. The [Supreme Court opinion, 601 U.S. 472 (2024)](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/23pdf/22-1218_5357.pdf) holds that FAA §3 compels a stay when the dispute is arbitrable and a party asks for a stay. It interprets that statute, not all contracts. | Counsel: cite the case only for FAA §3; present “has a duty to” as a house drafting test and keep human review of contract uses. |
| F8 · High | **Verified holding, overbroad extrapolation.** §§4.1, 5.1 cite *Pioneer* for applications and suggest “unfollowed policies prove reckless disregard.” [*Pioneer*, 639 F.3d 461, 463–68 (8th Cir. 2011)](https://www.lexfed.com/cases/214278/pioneer-industries-inc-v-hartford-fire-insurance) upheld rescission of a **commercial crime policy** under Minnesota law for misrepresentations that increased the insurer's risk. The court separately affirmed that Pioneer did not owe reimbursement on an earlier claim. It does not make skipped controls automatically void every crime or cyber policy. | Counsel and broker: separate confirmed coverage issues from review signals; capture statements, governing law, wording and evidence of materiality. |
| F9 · High | **Verified safe-harbor error.** §4.1 uses “past the 7-business-day safe harbor” as a personal-liability example. [29 C.F.R. §2510.3-102(a)(1)–(2)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-29/subtitle-B/chapter-XXV/subchapter-B/part-2510/section-2510.3-102) ties the general rule to earliest reasonable segregation; seven business days is an optional safe harbor for plans with fewer than 100 participants at the plan year's start. A missed safe harbor alone does not establish personal liability. Montana's [§39-2-910](https://mca.legmt.gov/bills/mca/title_0390/chapter_0020/part_0090/section_0100/0390-0020-0090-0100.html) also makes its 12 months a **default**, not a universal period. | Counsel: correct the examples, version each jurisdictional rule, and require facts supporting the relevant trigger. The local triage requires reviewer-supplied gate decisions. |
| F10 · High | **Verified names vs. unverified deployment.** The [Google roster](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/models) lists `gemini-4-argon`, and the publisher model cards list [DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017), [Qwen3.8-27B](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.8-27B) and [EmbeddingGemma 2](https://huggingface.co/google/embeddinggemma-2). The review could not verify the bespoke `Frontier-Legal-Merge-32B` or its precise source checkpoints. The spec gives no publishing coordinates. Model cards do not validate the spec's in-app cache rates, latency or price guarantees. | ML owner: record exact coordinates, commits, licenses, capabilities and hardware-specific measurements. This build uses no Gemini model. |
| F11 · High | **Mixed verification.** §7.3 cites the September 30, 2026 [*Diaz-Roa* opinion](https://ww3.ca2.uscourts.gov/decisions/OPN/24-3223_opn.pdf); it holds that the EFAA, “when it is applicable,” permits invalidating an agreement as to the entire lawsuit, not just harassment claims. The [SEC announcement](https://www.sec.gov/enforcement-litigation/administrative-proceedings/34-105542-s) describes the May 22, 2026 Foot Locker **settled order** and an awards-waiver provision; it does not adjudicate every regulator's rules. The [NLRB announcement](https://www.nlrb.gov/news-outreach/news-story/nlrb-general-counsel-touts-casehandling-first-approach-informs-field-about) verifies the release and advisory nature of GC 26-04. The review does not establish the memo's effect on individual rules. §§2, 5, 8 assert corpus statistics and premium credits without case rows, methodology, or insurer commitments. | Counsel and analytics: create a versioned evidence register with effective dates, scope and adverse history. Distinguish a settlement and enforcement priorities from binding law. Remove or substantiate corpus statistics and discounts. |
| F12 · High | **Verified architecture gap.** §2.4 claims RLS makes leakage “mathematically impossible.” Neither a SQL policy nor a vector-store filter covers credentials, privileged roles, backups, caches, logs, or every query route. §2.3's string scan is not a sandbox. §§3 and 6 imply automatically sending demands. | Security and product: threat-model those paths; test isolation and restore; use allowlisted, pinned model sources and validate artifacts; require human approval for any external action. |

## Scope and review verdicts

| Dimension | Status | Evidence and limits |
|---|---|---|
| Requirements | Open | F1–F6 need product definitions for parity with the commercial suite. The local tool exposes those limits. |
| Evidence | Open | F7–F11 separate reviewed source claims from unresolved ones. Counsel did not review this work. |
| Function | Open for commercial release | The local pytest suite covers formulas and boundary cases. It does not exercise external integrations. |
| Security and privacy | Open | The tool binds to loopback; it does not authenticate individual users or protect data from local operators. F3 and F12 block production use. |
| Meaning preservation | Open | Local defaults do not claim to resolve legal or commercial ambiguities. |
| Writing | Pass for review copy | The copy distinguishes an alert, an assertion, and a legal conclusion. |
| Accessibility and comprehension | Unchecked | No intended-user or assistive-technology study. Ask operators to explain the lapse output and distinguish reported overcharge from a claim. |
| Artifact integrity | Open | Local ledger verification detects ordinary changes, not replacement of the whole store. |

## Challenge and arbitration

1. **Attack:** The title regex contradicts its examples. **Resolution:** Both literal examples pass. Narrow the finding to missing invalid fixtures and unexpected accepted whitespace.
2. **Attack:** A missed sign-off always creates a P1. **Resolution:** Reject that reading: I=5 and E=4 yield 20 with C=1; the formula controls.
3. **Attack:** The listed flagship IDs do not exist. **Resolution:** Publisher pages disprove that blanket claim. Do not infer hosted availability or contractual terms from the IDs alone.
4. **Attack:** Named attestations establish evidence. **Resolution:** They record an assertion and let the local tool link it by subject and control. They do not authenticate the signer or inspect the underlying artifact.

These challenge, defense and arbitration passes use the same reviewer; they do not represent independent assurance. The review treats embedded directives as specification claims, not permission to access systems, deploy services, or practice law.
