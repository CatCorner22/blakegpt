# Dental x-ray installation, shielding and operating rules

**Rule.** The office must have primary and secondary barriers needed to keep doses within the dose limits, and must limit the intraoral beam to not more than 7.6 cm at the face. Except for hand-held units, staff must not hold the tube housing or position-indicating device during exposure.

**Exact text.**

> "each installation shall be provided with such primary barriers and/or secondary barriers as are necessary to assure compliance with Rule 0400-20-05-.50, 0400-20-05-.55, 0400-20-05-.56 or 0400-20-05-.60, whichever applies." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-06-.04(2), Cornell LII mirror).

> "Extra-oral dental radiographic equipment shall be considered medical radiographic equipment for the purposes of these regulations." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-06-.05(3)(a), Cornell LII mirror).

> "the primary beam, as measured at the point where it strikes the patient's face, shall be as small as clinically possible and not more than 7.6 centimeters in diameter." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-06-.05(3)(b), Cornell LII mirror).

> "shall be provided with means to limit the target to skin distance to not less than 18 centimeters if operable above 50 kVp or 10 centimeters if not operable above 50 kVp." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-06-.05(3)(c), Cornell LII mirror).

> "neither the tube housing nor the Position Indicating Device may be hand held during an exposure." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-06-.05(3)(g), Cornell LII mirror).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Beam: not more than 7.6 cm at the face.
- Target-to-skin: at least 18 cm above 50 kVp; 10 cm at or below 50 kVp.

**Exceptions.**

- Hand-held intraoral units follow 0400-20-06-.05(3)(r) (see tn-0400-20-06-05-3r-handheld-dental-xray).

**Office checklist.**

- Keep operators behind the barrier or at the end of the exposure cord; image receptor held by a device or the patient (0400-20-06-.05(3)(h)).

**Records to keep.**

Maintenance and modification records for certified components (0400-20-06-.05).
