# Nitrous oxide: administration, supervision and equipment

**Rule.** Only a dentist or a certified hygienist under direct supervision may administer nitrous oxide, and the dentist must be on the premises the whole time it is in use. An authorized person must constantly monitor each patient, and the equipment must deliver at least 30% oxygen and have a scavenger system.

**Exact text.**

> "The administering or supervising dentist must be on the premises at all times that nitrous oxide is in use." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.07(4)(a), Cornell LII mirror).

> "An authorized person must constantly monitor each patient receiving nitrous oxide." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.07(4)(b), Cornell LII mirror).

> "an oxygen concentration of no less than thirty percent (30%) can be administered" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.07(4)(d), Cornell LII mirror).

> "must be equipped with a scavenger system." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.07(4)(e), Cornell LII mirror).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Oxygen: not less than 30% (0460-02-.07(4)(d)).

**Exceptions.**

None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- Before each nitrous day, confirm scavenger works and the dentist is on site; log equipment checks monthly.

No retention rule in this source.
