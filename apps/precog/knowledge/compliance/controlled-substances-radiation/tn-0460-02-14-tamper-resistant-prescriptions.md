# Tamper-resistant paper prescriptions

**Rule.** A dentist must issue every handwritten, typed or computer-generated prescription on tamper-resistant paper with all 3 required feature types.

**Exact text.**

> "A prescriber shall ensure that all handwritten, typed, or computer-generated prescription orders are issued on tamper-resistant prescriptions." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.14(3)(a), Cornell LII mirror).

> "Either a void or illegal pantograph or a watermark designed to prevent copying;" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.14(3)(a)1., Cornell LII mirror).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- 3 feature types: copy prevention, erasure/modification prevention, counterfeit prevention (0460-02-.14(3)(a)1.-3.).

**Exceptions.**

- Fax and electronic prescriptions do not need tamper-resistant paper but must be documented in the patient record (0460-02-.14(3)).

**Office checklist.**

- Order pads only from a vendor certifying all 3 features; lock pad stock.

**Records to keep.**

No retention rule in this source.
