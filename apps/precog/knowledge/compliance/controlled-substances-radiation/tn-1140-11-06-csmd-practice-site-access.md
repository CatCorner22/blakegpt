# Practice site must provide CSMD electronic access

**Rule.** The office must provide electronic access to the CSMD at all times when a prescriber provides care to a patient who may receive a controlled substance.

**Exact text.**

> "Each person or entity operating a practice site where a controlled substance is prescribed or dispensed to a human patient shall provide for electronic access to the database at all times when a prescriber or dispenser provides healthcare services to a human patient potentially receiving a controlled substance." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 1140-11-.06(1), Cornell LII mirror).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Civil penalty up to $100 per day for a continued pattern of not providing access (1140-11-.06; also T.C.A. § 53-10-310).

**Exceptions.**

None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- Confirm on each clinic day that an operatory computer can reach the CSMD.

**Records to keep.**

No retention rule in this source.
