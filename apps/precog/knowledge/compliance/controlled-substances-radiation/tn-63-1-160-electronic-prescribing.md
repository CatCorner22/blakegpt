# Electronic prescribing of Schedule II-V controlled substances

**Rule.** A dentist must issue each Schedule II-V controlled-substance prescription as an electronic prescription to the pharmacy, unless an exception in § 63-1-160(d) applies.

**Exact text.**

> "on or after January 1, 2021, any prescription for a Schedule II, III, IV, or V controlled substance issued by a prescriber who is authorized by law to prescribe the drug must be issued as an electronic prescription from the person issuing the prescription to a pharmacy." (T.C.A. § 63-1-160(c), Justia unofficial mirror).

> "Issued by a health care prescriber who issues fifty (50) or fewer prescriptions for Schedule II controlled substances per year" (T.C.A. § 63-1-160(d)(11), Justia unofficial mirror).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Effective on or after 2021-01-01.
- Low-volume exception: 50 or fewer Schedule II prescriptions per year.

**Exceptions.**

- Listed in § 63-1-160(d) include technological or electrical failure, out-of-state pharmacy, prescriber and dispenser the same entity, waiver, impractical delay harming the patient, and the 50-or-fewer Schedule II exception.

**Office checklist.**

- Keep the e-prescribing system EPCS-enabled; log every paper exception with its reason.

No retention rule in this source.
