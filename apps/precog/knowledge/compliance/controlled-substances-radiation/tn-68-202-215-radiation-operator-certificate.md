# Medical radiation operators must hold a current certificate

**Rule.** A dentist must not permit an employee without a current certificate from the appropriate board to operate medical radiation equipment. An operator awaiting examination may operate for not more than 1 year.

**Exact text.**

> "If any licensee of a professional board permits an employee to operate or supervise the operation of medical radiation equipment by an operator who does not possess a current certificate issued by the appropriate board or agency, then the appropriate professional licensing board shall consider such action to be grounds for a finding of unprofessional conduct" (T.C.A. § 68-202-215(c)(1), Justia unofficial mirror).

> "shall be permitted to operate medical radiation machines for a period of not to exceed one (1) year." (T.C.A. § 68-202-215(d)(1), Justia unofficial mirror).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Awaiting-examination period: not more than 1 year.

None stated in the source.
