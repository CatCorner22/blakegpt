# Required contents of a controlled-substance prescription (added item)

**Rule.** A dentist must date and sign each controlled-substance prescription on the day issued and must include the patient full name and address, drug name, strength, dosage form, quantity, directions, and the dentist name, address and DEA number.

**Exact text.**

> "All prescriptions for controlled substances shall be dated as of, and signed on, the day when issued and shall bear the full name and address of the patient, the drug name, strength, dosage form, quantity prescribed, directions for use, and the name, address and registration number of the practitioner." (21 CFR 1306.05(a)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Signed and dated the same day issued.

**Exceptions.**

None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- Spot-check 5 paper or fax prescriptions per quarter for every element; dentist corrects.

**Records to keep.**

No retention rule in this source.
