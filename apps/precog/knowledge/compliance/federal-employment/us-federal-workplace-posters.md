# Federal workplace notice review

**Rule.** The practice must identify and display the federal notices required by its actual coverage. The office manager must keep required notices accessible to employees and, where required, applicants.

**Exact text.**
> "where employees can readily observe them" (DOL FirstStep Poster Advisor, introductory guidance).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- The cited final Federal Register rule sets the EEOC posting penalty maximum at $698 per violation effective September 30, 2025; it does not establish later adjustments (90 FR 46766, dates and calculation).
- Check the FMLA coverage card before selecting that notice; do not assume all DOL notices apply to every practice (DOL poster requirements guidance).

**Exceptions.**
- The DOL poster pages and EEOC poster page are guidance, not statutes. Each underlying law has its own coverage and posting rules; this is a selection checklist, not a blanket size exemption.

**Office checklist.**
- At onboarding, workplace moves, and policy reviews, use the DOL Poster Advisor to screen FLSA, FMLA, EPPA, and USERRA notices; review the EEOC Know Your Rights notice. Refer OSHA posting to the workplace-safety section. Owner dentist signs off on the notice inventory.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.

**Related.** us-fmla-employer-coverage-50, us-eppa-polygraph-ban, us-userra-reemployment.

**Not verified.** Later inflation adjustments to the EEOC penalty were not established by the assigned final-rule source; do not treat its amount as a guarantee of the current maximum.
