# Count all compensable work time

**Rule.** The practice must count work it permits, including after-shift tasks. The practice must count compensable rest breaks, workday travel, and required job training.

**Exact text.**
> "Work not requested but suffered or permitted is work time." (29 CFR 785.11).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Short rest periods from 5 minutes to about 20 minutes count as hours worked (29 CFR 785.18).
- A bona fide unpaid meal ordinarily lasts at least 30 minutes and requires complete relief from duties; shorter meals can qualify under special conditions (29 CFR 785.19(a)).

**Exceptions.**
- Training can be excluded only when attendance is outside regular hours, voluntary, unrelated to the job, and involves no productive work (29 CFR 785.27). The de minimis rule is narrow; it does not permit arbitrary exclusion of regular work (29 CFR 785.47).

**Office checklist.**
- At every payroll, reconcile opening, sterilization, closing, interrupted meals, training, and between-site travel; owner dentist signs off on corrections.
