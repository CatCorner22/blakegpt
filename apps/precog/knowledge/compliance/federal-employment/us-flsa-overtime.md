# Overtime pay

**Rule.** The practice must pay covered nonexempt employees overtime for hours worked above the weekly limit. The practice must use the regular rate, not automatically the base hourly rate.

**Exact text.**
> "at a rate not less than one and one-half times the regular rate at which he is employed" (29 U.S.C. 207(a)(1)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Over 40 hours in a workweek triggers overtime at the statutory multiplier quoted above (29 U.S.C. 207(a)(1); DOL Overtime Pay guidance, opening paragraphs).

**Exceptions.**
- Employees meeting an exemption require a separate analysis; a job title or salary alone does not establish an exemption.

**Office checklist.**
- At every payroll, review weekly time and regular-rate inputs before paying overtime; owner dentist signs off. DOL Overtime Pay material is guidance, not law.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
