# Wage and hour records

**Rule.** The practice must keep required employee, hours, wage, and payroll records. The practice must keep them safe, accessible, and available for authorized inspection.

**Exact text.**
> "all basic time and earning cards or sheets" (29 CFR 516.6(a)(1)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Keep payroll records for at least 3 years from the last entry (29 CFR 516.5(a)).
- Keep basic time and earnings records for at least 2 years from the last entry; keep other supporting calculation records for the specified period (29 CFR 516.6(a), (c)).

**Exceptions.**
- Record requirements vary for exempt employees; do not assume the nonexempt record list applies identically to every classification (29 CFR 516.2(a)).

**Office checklist.**
- At every payroll, check daily and weekly hours, pay rates, additions, deductions, totals, and pay dates; owner dentist signs off. DOL Fact Sheet guidance is not law.

**Records to keep.** Keep employee identification, hours, pay calculations, and payroll records for the periods above; maintain inspection access (29 CFR 516.2(a); 516.5; 516.6; 516.7).
