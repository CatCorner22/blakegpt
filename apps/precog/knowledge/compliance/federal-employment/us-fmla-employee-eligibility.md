# Employee eligibility for FMLA

**Rule.** The practice must assess employee eligibility separately from employer coverage when leave begins.

**Exact text.**
> "at least 1,250 hours of service during the 12-month period immediately preceding the commencement of the leave" (29 CFR 825.110(a)(2)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- At least 12 months employed, at least 1,250 hours of service in the preceding 12 months, and a worksite with at least 50 employees within 75 miles are required (29 CFR 825.110(a)).

**Exceptions.**
- The employment months need not be consecutive; rules for breaks in service and military service can affect the calculation (29 CFR 825.110(b), (c)).

**Office checklist.**
- When leave is requested or a qualifying need becomes known, check service, hours, and worksite count; owner dentist signs off.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
