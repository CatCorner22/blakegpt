# FMLA employer coverage

**Rule.** The owner dentist must assess private-employer FMLA coverage separately from employee eligibility.

**Exact text.**
> "50 or more employees for each working day during each of 20 or more calendar workweeks in the current or preceding calendar year" (29 U.S.C. 2611(4)(A)(i)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- 50 employees on each working day during 20 calendar workweeks in the current or preceding calendar year (29 U.S.C. 2611(4)(A)(i)).

**Exceptions.**
- Public agencies and schools have separate coverage rules; do not apply the private-practice test to them (29 CFR 825.104(a)).

**Office checklist.**
- Review headcounts at year-end and staffing changes; owner dentist signs off before treating the practice as outside FMLA.
- Keep the coverage analysis and supporting payroll or request records as office practice, subject to applicable retention and litigation holds.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
