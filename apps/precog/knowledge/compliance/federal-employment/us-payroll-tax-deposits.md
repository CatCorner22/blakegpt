# Payroll tax deposit schedules

**Rule.** The practice must determine its deposit schedule using the applicable lookback period and monitor large daily tax accumulations. The practice must not confuse depositing tax with filing a return.

**Exact text.**
> "the aggregate amount of employment taxes reported for the lookback period is $50,000 or less" (26 CFR 31.6302-1(b)(2)(i)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Ordinary monthly status applies at $50,000 or less in lookback tax; above $50,000 ordinarily means semiweekly status (26 CFR 31.6302-1(b)(2)-(3)).
- For ordinary quarterly filers, the lookback is the 12-month period ending the preceding June 30 (26 CFR 31.6302-1(b)(4)(i)).
- Monthly deposits are due by the 15th of the following month; semiweekly deposits follow the Wednesday/Friday schedule tied to payday (26 CFR 31.6302-1(c)(1)-(2)).
- For semiweekly depositors, Wednesday through Friday paydays ordinarily require deposit by the following Wednesday; Saturday through Tuesday paydays ordinarily require deposit by the following Friday (26 CFR 31.6302-1(c)(2)(i)).
- Accumulating $100,000 or more within a deposit period triggers deposit by the next banking day and changes a monthly depositor's status for the remainder of that calendar year and the following calendar year (26 CFR 31.6302-1(b)(2)(ii), (c)(3)).

**Exceptions.**
- New employers, annual filers, small liabilities, holidays, and banking-day rules require separate application of the regulation; payroll frequency alone does not choose the schedule (26 CFR 31.6302-1(b), (c), (f)).

**Office checklist.**
- At year-end, approve payroll's lookback calculation; every payday monitor accrued tax, due dates, and electronic confirmations; owner dentist signs off. IRS materials are guidance, not law.

**Records to keep.** Keep deposit calculations, payment confirmations, returns, and supporting employment-tax records for at least 4 years as IRS guidance directs (IRS Publication 15, Recordkeeping).
