# Impressions to the lab and prostheses from the lab

**Rule.** Staff must rinse and disinfect contaminated impressions and ship them in a sealed case labeled "Biohazard", marked with the biohazard symbol, or in a red container. Staff must wash, rinse, disinfect and rinse again every prosthetic appliance from the lab before placing it in the mouth.

**Exact text.**

> "Items such as impressions contaminated with blood or saliva must be thoroughly rinsed, disinfected, placed in, and transported to the dental laboratory in an appropriate case containment device that is properly sealed and labeled "Biohazard"" (0460-01-.11(9)).

> "Oral prosthetic appliances received from a dental laboratory must be washed with soap or a detergent and water, rinsed well, appropriately disinfected, and rinsed well again before the prosthetic appliance is placed in the patient's mouth." (0460-01-.11(10)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Every outgoing impression and every incoming appliance; no numeric thresholds.

**Exceptions.**

- None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- Keep labeled biohazard lab bags or red containers at the lab station.
- Add an "incoming case disinfected" initial box to the lab log.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
