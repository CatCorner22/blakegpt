# Gloves, masks, eye protection and hand hygiene for clinical staff

**Rule.** Clinical staff must wear gloves, a surgical mask and eye protection with shields for intra-oral work when contact with blood or saliva is imminent, must change gloves between patients, and must not wash or reuse gloves. Clinical staff must follow current CDC hand hygiene guidelines, including soap and water when hands are visibly soiled.

**Exact text.**

> "Surgical or examination gloves, surgical masks, and eye protection with eye shields shall be worn by all dentists, dental hygienists and dental assistants while performing, or assisting in the performance of, any intra-oral dental procedure on a patient in which contact with blood and/or saliva is imminent in accordance with CDC recommendations." (0460-01-.11(11)).

> "Surgical or examination gloves must be changed between patients. Gloves are never to be washed and reused." (0460-01-.11(11)).

> "All dentists, dental hygienists, and dental assistants shall follow hand hygiene guidelines in accordance with current CDC recommendations." (0460-01-.11(12)).

> "Use alcohol-based hand rubs for routine decontamination of hands for all clinical indications, except as provided in subparagraph (a)." (0460-01-.11(12)(b)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Torn or punctured gloves: replace "immediately" after rewashing hands (0460-01-.11(11)).
- Soap and water: visibly dirty hands, proteinaceous material, blood or body fluids, before eating, after restroom (0460-01-.11(12)(a)).

**Exceptions.**

- Alcohol rub is the default except in the soap-and-water cases in (12)(a).

**Office checklist.**

- Stock gloves in all sizes, masks, eyewear with side shields, alcohol rub at each chair.
- Observe hand hygiene for 1 clinician per week using the CDC checklist; the infection prevention coordinator signs.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
