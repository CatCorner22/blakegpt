# EPA dental amalgam rule: who must install an amalgam separator and by when

**Rule.** A dental office that discharges amalgam wastewater to a POTW must run a 95% ISO 11143 or ANSI/ADA 108 amalgam separator, inspect it per the manual, repair or replace it within 10 business days of a malfunction, and must not flush waste amalgam or use oxidizing or acidic line cleaners outside pH 6 to 8.

**Exact text.**

> "No later than July 14, 2020, any existing source subject to this part must achieve the following pretreatment standards" (40 CFR 441.30).

> "As of July 14, 2017, any new source subject to this part must comply with the requirements of § 441.30(a) and (b)" (40 CFR 441.40).

> "until the existing separator is replaced as described in paragraph (a)(1)(v) of this section or until June 14, 2027, whichever is sooner." (40 CFR 441.30(a)(1)(iii)).

> "as soon as possible, but no later than 10 business days after the malfunction is discovered" (40 CFR 441.30(a)(1)(v)).

> "must not be cleaned with oxidizing or acidic cleaners, including but not limited to bleach, chlorine, iodine and peroxide that have a pH lower than 6 or greater than 8." (40 CFR 441.30(b)(2)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Existing sources: comply by July 14, 2020 (441.30). New sources (first discharge after July 14, 2017): comply from day 1 (441.20(i), 441.40).
- Separator at least 95% removal efficiency (441.30(a)(1)(i)).
- Separators installed before June 14, 2017 are grandfathered until replaced or June 14, 2027, whichever is sooner (441.30(a)(1)(iii)).
- Repair or replace within 10 business days (441.30(a)(1)(v)).
- Line cleaners: pH 6 to 8 only (441.30(b)(2)).

**Exceptions.**

- Offices practicing only oral pathology, oral and maxillofacial radiology, oral and maxillofacial surgery, orthodontics, periodontics or prosthodontics (441.10(c)).
- Mobile units (441.10(d)).
- Offices that send no amalgam wastewater to a POTW (441.10(e)).
- Offices that do not place amalgam and remove it only in limited emergencies, after certifying to the Control Authority (441.10(f)).

**Office checklist.**

- Check the separator install date; if before June 14, 2017, plan replacement by June 14, 2027.
- Inspect on the manual schedule; log it.
- Check every waterline and vacuum-line cleaner label for pH 6 to 8.

**Records to keep.** See us-40-cfr-441-amalgam-reporting-records (3 years for inspection logs; One-Time Compliance Report for life of ownership).
