# Safe injection practices and single-use devices (CDC guidance)

**Rule.** Staff must not use a needle, syringe or anesthetic cartridge for more than 1 patient, and must enter any vial with a new needle and new syringe. Staff must clean and heat-sterilize the metal cartridge syringe between patients.

**Exact text.**

> "Do not use needles or syringes* for more than one patient (this includes manufactured prefilled syringes and other devices such as insulin pens)." (2016 Summary, Safe Injection item 3).

> "Medication containers (single and multidose vials, ampules, and bags) are entered with a new needle and new syringe, even when obtaining additional doses for the same patient." (2016 Summary, Safe Injection item 4).

> "Do not combine the leftover contents of single-use vials for later use." (2016 Summary, Safe Injection item 7).

> "When using a dental cartridge syringe, needles and anesthetic cartridges are considered single-use disposable items and should be used for one patient only and then discarded." (CDC Best Practices for Safe Injections, Recommendations).

> "Single-use devices are discarded after one use and not used for more than one patient" (2016 Summary, Appendix A, II.6.A).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- 1 patient per needle, syringe and cartridge.

**Exceptions.**

- CDC gives no rule on wiping the cartridge diaphragm with alcohol; follow the manufacturer ("CDC does not provide a specific recommendation").

**Office checklist.**

- Keep multidose vials (if any) out of operatories.
- Include safe injection steps in annual IPC training.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
