# OSHA: sharps containers and regulated waste containers

**Rule.** Staff must discard contaminated sharps immediately or as soon as feasible into closable, puncture-resistant, leakproof, labeled containers kept upright near the point of use and must not let them overfill. The employer must dispose of regulated waste under federal, state and local rules.

**Exact text.**

> "Contaminated sharps shall be discarded immediately or as soon as feasible in containers that are: ( i ) Closable; ( ii ) Puncture resistant; ( iii ) Leakproof on sides and bottom; and ( iv ) Labeled or color-coded" (29 CFR 1910.1030(d)(4)(iii)(A)(1)).

> "Replaced routinely and not be allowed to overfill." (29 CFR 1910.1030(d)(4)(iii)(A)(2)(iii)).

> "Disposal of all regulated waste shall be in accordance with applicable regulations of the United States, States and Territories, and political subdivisions of States and Territories." (29 CFR 1910.1030(d)(4)(iii)(C)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Sharps: immediately or as soon as feasible.
- Close containers immediately before removal; secondary container if leakage is possible ((d)(4)(iii)(A)(3)).

**Exceptions.**

- None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- 1 sharps container in each operatory and the sterilization area, upright, below the fill line.
- Swap containers at the fill line; never empty by hand.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this paragraph.
