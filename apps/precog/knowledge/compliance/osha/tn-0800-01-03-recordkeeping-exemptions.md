# Tennessee dental and small-employer recordkeeping exemptions

**Rule.** The employer may rely on the dental-industry partial exemption from routine injury and illness logs unless instructed in writing to keep records. The employer must still report severe events under Tennessee's reporting rule.

**Exact text.**
> "6212 Offices of Dentists" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-03-.02(4), Appendix A).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Small-company exemption: 10 or fewer employees at all times in the prior calendar year, using the entire company's peak employment (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-03-.02(2)(a)1, (b)).
- Dental-industry exemption: NAICS 6212, by establishment, subject to written requests (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-03-.02(3)-(4)).

**Exceptions.**
- Written TOSHA/BLS requests can require records despite partial exemption. Non-exempt establishments require their own coverage analysis (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-03-.02(3)).

**Office checklist.**
- Practice workflow: Amanda: retain the practice's classification and any agency request; do not confuse log exemption with reporting exemption. Owner dentist: approve classification at organizational changes.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule for the exemption determination in this source.
