# Tennessee state plan and adopted standards

**Rule.** The employer must provide employment free from recognized hazards causing or likely to cause death or serious injury or harm and comply with applicable Tennessee safety standards. The employer must use Tennessee's adoption rule and exceptions rather than assume every current federal amendment applies.

**Exact text.**
> "as of January 1, 2022 except as provided in Rule 0800-01-01-.07 of this chapter." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-01-.06(2)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Tennessee's mirror adopts federal general-industry standards as of January 1, 2022, subject to exceptions; the adoption-rule history lists an amendment effective May 18, 2023 (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-01-.06(2), history).

**Exceptions.**
- Coverage exclusions appear in -.05(2); specific standards prevail over differing general standards for the same condition under -.05(3)(a). Tennessee excludes specified federal provisions and substitutes air-contaminant standards under -.07.
