# Hepatitis B vaccination and declination

**Rule.** The employer must offer hepatitis B vaccination to occupationally exposed employees at no cost after required training. The employer must obtain the mandatory signed declination from employees who decline and offer vaccination if they later accept while still covered.

**Exact text.**
> "I have been given the opportunity to be vaccinated with hepatitis B vaccine, at no charge to myself." (29 CFR 1910.1030, Appendix A).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Offer vaccination within 10 working days of initial assignment, after required training (29 CFR 1910.1030(f)(2)(i)).

**Exceptions.**
- Prior complete series, demonstrated immunity, or medical contraindication excuse the initial offer requirement. Prescreening cannot be a prerequisite (29 CFR 1910.1030(f)(2)(i)-(ii)).

**Office checklist.**
- Practice workflow: Amanda: arrange the offer and track acceptance or signed Appendix A declination at assignment. Owner dentist: confirm access to a licensed healthcare professional and no-cost service.

**Records to keep.** Keep vaccination status, dates, and related medical records confidential for employment plus 30 years (29 CFR 1910.1030(h)(1)(ii)-(iv)).
