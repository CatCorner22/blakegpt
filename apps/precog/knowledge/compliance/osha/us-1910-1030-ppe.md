# Bloodborne pathogen PPE

**Rule.** The employer must provide appropriate PPE at no employee cost and ensure its use. The employer must clean, launder, repair, replace, and dispose of required PPE at no employee cost.

**Exact text.**
> "All personal protective equipment shall be removed prior to leaving the work area." (29 CFR 1910.1030(d)(3)(vii)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Remove penetrated garments immediately or as soon as feasible; remove all PPE before leaving the work area (29 CFR 1910.1030(d)(3)(vi)-(vii)).

**Exceptions.**
- A temporary, brief professional-judgment refusal is limited to rare and extraordinary circumstances described in the rule; investigate and document it. Do not wash or decontaminate disposable gloves for reuse (29 CFR 1910.1030(d)(3)(ii), (ix)(B)).

**Office checklist.**
- Practice workflow: Amanda: stock correct sizes and allergy alternatives; arrange employer-paid laundering. Owner dentist: confirm selection protects against anticipated splashes and investigate any exceptional refusal.

**Records to keep.** Document exceptional PPE non-use; no retention period stated (29 CFR 1910.1030(d)(3)(ii)).
