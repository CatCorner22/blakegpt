# Conditional federal sharps injury log

**Rule.** The employer must keep a confidential sharps injury log if required to maintain an occupational injury and illness log under federal recordkeeping rules. The employer must record the device type and brand, work area, and explanation of each contaminated-sharps percutaneous injury.

**Exact text.**
> "The requirement to establish and maintain a sharps injury log shall apply to any employer who is required to maintain a log of occupational injuries and illnesses under 29 CFR part 1904." (29 CFR 1910.1030(h)(5)(ii)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Retain the log for 5 years following the end of the calendar year covered (29 CFR 1910.1030(h)(5)(iii); 29 CFR 1904.33(a)).

**Exceptions.**
- The federal log requirement is conditional on Part 1904 log coverage; dental partial exemption does not remove Tennessee's separate exposure-plan device documentation requirement (29 CFR 1910.1030(h)(5)(ii); Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-10-.04).

**Office checklist.**
- Practice workflow: Amanda: verify exemption status and protect injured employee identity in any required log. Owner dentist: sign off on coverage determination and Tennessee exposure-plan entry.

**Records to keep.** Keep the required sharps log for the period above; protect confidentiality (29 CFR 1910.1030(h)(5)(i), (iii)).
