# PPE hazard assessment and certification

**Rule.** The employer must assess workplace hazards requiring covered PPE, select properly fitting protection, and communicate the choice. The employer must certify the assessment in writing and train affected employees before they perform PPE-required work.

**Exact text.**
> "Defective or damaged personal protective equipment shall not be used." (29 CFR 1910.132(e)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Employees must demonstrate understanding and proper use before work requiring PPE; retrain when workplace/PPE changes or skill gaps make prior training inadequate (29 CFR 1910.132(f)(2)-(3)).

**Exceptions.**
- Assessment/training paragraphs apply only to the listed eye/face, head, foot, hand, and fall protection standards, not respiratory or electrical protective equipment. Payment exceptions are listed in (h); another standard's specific payment rule prevails (29 CFR 1910.132(g)-(h)).

**Office checklist.**
- Practice workflow: Amanda: maintain the certification, PPE inventory, and training evidence; flag new hazards. Owner dentist: certify workplace evaluated, certifier, dates, and certification identity, and approve selections.

**Records to keep.** Keep the written hazard-assessment certification; no retention duration stated (29 CFR 1910.132(d)(2)).
