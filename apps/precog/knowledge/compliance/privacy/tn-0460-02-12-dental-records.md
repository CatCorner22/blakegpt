# Tennessee dental chart retention and release

**Rule.** A dentist must create and maintain a dental record for each patient receiving services or professional consultation and must not withhold it for unpaid dental services. The dentist must retain records for at least 7 years after the dentist's or supervisee's last professional contact, subject to longer protected categories.

**Exact text.**
> "A dentist shall not withhold records for non-payment of current or prior dental services." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.12(4)(b)).

> "Dental records for incompetent patients shall be retained indefinitely." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.12(4)(g)1).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- General retention: at least 7 years from last professional contact (4)(g)).
- Minors: at least 1 year after majority or 7 years after last professional contact, whichever is longer; the source does not state a numeric age of majority (4)(g)2).
- Incompetent patients: indefinitely; disputed services: do not destroy until resolved (4)(g)1, 3).
- Required transition notices go within 30 days of the triggering event and may use public notice (4)(c)). Death/retirement, departure, and sale provisions concern patients seen during the prior 36 months; departure-notice responsibility follows the contract (4)(f)1–3).
- Include tooth charting, dated services, concise history, drug dates/types/amounts, and readable X-rays when required; X-rays and interpretations are part of the record (4)(e)).

**Exceptions.**
- Hospital, hospital emergency room, and hospital outpatient records are outside this rule's specified scope (3), (4)(d)).
- Destruction must follow established ordinary-course procedures, not selective targeting; use effective confidential destruction (4)(h)).
- Sale/abandonment rules require secure custody/access and authorized transfers; a practice cannot abandon access arrangements (4)(f)3–4).

**Office checklist.**
- For every proposed disposal, Amanda calculates the last-contact period and checks minor, incompetency, and dispute flags; the dentist signs off.
- At departure, retirement, death, or sale, Amanda assembles the affected-patient notice list and deadline; the dentist or authorized representative approves notices and secure custody.
- At each transfer request, Amanda separates lawful copying costs from unpaid treatment bills and preserves access.

**Records to keep.** Keep charts for the periods in (4)(g). Keep time, date, and circumstances of destruction sufficient to identify the group containing a patient's chart; (4)(h)4 gives no fixed retention period for that destruction record.
