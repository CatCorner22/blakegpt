# Tennessee patient records access

**Rule.** A Tennessee licensed provider must give a patient or authorized representative copies within 10 working days after receiving a written request. The provider must not substitute a summary for the full record right. This deadline is stricter than the current HIPAA 30-day outside deadline.

**Exact text.**
> "within ten (10) working days of receipt of a written request by the patient or the patient's authorized representative." (Tenn. Code Ann. § 63-2-101(a)(1)).

> "the provision of a summary does not satisfy the patient's or representative's right to receive, or serve as a substitute for, a full medical record" (Tenn. Code Ann. § 63-2-101(a)(1)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- The trigger is receipt of the patient's/representative's written request; 10 working days is not a fixed calendar-day period (a)(1)).
- Current eCFR HIPAA: 30 days plus only 1 extension up to 30 days with timely written reasons/completion date; that federal extension does not extend the state deadline (164.524(b)(2)).
- Pending final rule 2026-23429: act as soon as practicable within 15 calendar days; only 1 extension up to 30 calendar days with timely written reasons/date. A legally required period shorter than 15 calendar days is deemed practicable (revised 164.524(b)(2)).
- Scheduled publication 2026-11-19; effective 60 days after publication (derived 2027-01-18), compliance 240 days after publication (derived 2027-07-17); calculated dates, not quoted (final rule DATES p. 1).
- The provider definition covers people licensed under title 63; records include histories, diagnoses, treatment/medication, X-ray interpretations, and lab reports (c)(2), (4)).
- Source: Justia 2025 unofficial mirror; no amendment was found in the compilation search reported in the supplied checkpoint.

**Exceptions.**
- A provider may offer a summary, but it does not replace the full record right (a)(1)).
- This short access card does not authorize all third-party disclosure; state confidentiality and HIPAA permission rules remain relevant (b); see the public-interest and authorization cards.

**Office checklist.**
- At each written request, Amanda logs receipt, verifies authority, counts actual working days, and tracks delivery.
- The privacy official approves any denial or unusual request with the dentist; Amanda does not use a HIPAA extension to postpone the state deadline.
- The privacy official maintains the separate patient-dignity policy for educational records/images required by (b)(3).

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source; use the dental-record card for chart retention and HIPAA documentation rules for required written policies.
