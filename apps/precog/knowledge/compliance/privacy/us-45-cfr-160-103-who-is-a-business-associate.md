# Who is a business associate

**Rule.** A covered dental practice must classify outside vendors by their actual PHI functions, not their business labels. A vendor may be a BA when it creates, receives, maintains, or transmits PHI on the practice's behalf or performs a listed PHI-related service outside the workforce.

**Exact text.**
> "A health care provider, with respect to disclosures by a covered entity to the health care provider concerning the treatment of the individual." (45 CFR 160.103, Business associate (4)(i)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- No workforce-size exception appears in the definition.
- A dental provider is a covered entity when it transmits health information electronically in a HIPAA-covered transaction (160.103, Covered entity (3)).
- Guidance/application examples: outside billing, IT/MSP with qualifying PHI functions, cloud imaging storage, and hosted EHR services may qualify under Business associate (1); HHS expressly lists billing and practice management as examples. Assess actual access/maintenance/services.
- A dental lab receiving PHI only as a treating health care provider is not a BA for that disclosure; a lab performing a separate on-behalf-of PHI service may qualify. HHS's laboratory examples are guidance, not a rule that all labs are BAs.
- Pending amendment 2 adds a telecommunications-relay-provider exception for specified regulated relay services. Scheduled publication 2026-11-19; effective 60 days later (derived 2027-01-18), compliance 240 days later (derived 2027-07-17) (DATES p. 1).

**Exceptions.** 
- Workforce members are not BAs for their workforce activities; treatment-provider disclosures fall within (4)(i).
- A covered entity can also be another entity's BA; qualifying subcontractors are included (2), (3)(iii).

**Office checklist.**
- Before each vendor handles PHI, Amanda documents its functions and whether it is workforce, treating provider, BA, or another recipient.
- The privacy official signs the classification and obtains the needed agreement; reassess whenever the service changes.

**Records to keep.** The definition itself states no retention period. Keep required BA agreements/policies under 164.530(j) and security documentation under 164.316(b), each for 6 years from creation or last effective date, whichever is later.
