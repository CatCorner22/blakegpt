# Backup and emergency operations

**Rule.** A covered practice or business associate must establish contingency procedures for emergencies that damage electronic-PHI systems. The entity must maintain retrievable exact backups, recovery procedures, and emergency-mode operations.

**Exact text.**
> "Establish and implement procedures to create and maintain retrievable exact copies of electronic protected health information." (45 CFR 164.308(a)(7)(ii)(A)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Backup, disaster recovery, and emergency-mode operations are required specifications (164.308(a)(7)(ii)(A)–(C)).
- Periodic testing/revision and application/data criticality analysis are addressable; this source sets no fixed backup or testing interval (a)(7)(ii)(D)–(E).

**Exceptions.** 
- An addressable measure needs the assessment and documented alternative process in 164.306(d)(3).

**Office checklist.**
- On the security official's risk-based schedule, Amanda obtains backup and recovery-test evidence from IT.
- When a system changes or a test fails, the security official approves corrections and emergency contact instructions before closing the issue.

**Records to keep.** Keep security policies and required documented actions/assessments for 6 years from creation or last effective date, whichever is later; make them available to implementers and update as needed (45 CFR 164.316(b)(1)–(2)).
