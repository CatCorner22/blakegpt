# Privacy documentation retention

**Rule.** A covered practice must keep written or electronic privacy policies, required writings, records of required documented actions, and breach burden-of-proof documentation. The practice must retain them for 6 years from creation or last effective date, whichever is later.

**Exact text.**
> "for six years from the date of its creation or the date when it last was in effect, whichever is later." (45 CFR 164.530(j)(2)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- The 6-year period runs from the later date, not automatically from signature (164.530(j)(2)).
- This is a privacy-documentation rule, not a patient-chart destruction deadline.

**Exceptions.** None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**
- When a document changes, Amanda archives the retired version and records its last effective date.
- Before each scheduled disposal, the privacy official checks this period and any separate dental-record or preservation requirement.

**Records to keep.** Keep required written policies and documentation for 6 years from creation or last effective date, whichever is later (45 CFR 164.530(j)(1)–(2)).
