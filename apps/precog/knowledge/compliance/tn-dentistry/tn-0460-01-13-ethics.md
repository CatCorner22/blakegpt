# Codes of ethics adopted by the Board

**Rule.** Dentists must follow the ADA Principles of Ethics and Code of Professional Conduct, hygienists the ADHA Code of Ethics, and registered dental assistants the ADAA Principles of Ethics and Professional Conduct, to the extent each does not conflict with Tennessee law, rules or Board Position Statements.

**Exact text.**

> "the Board adopts, as if fully set out herein and to the extent that it does not conflict with state law, rules or Board Position Statements, the American Dental Association (ADA) Principles of Ethics and Code of Professional Conduct as it may, from time to time, be amended" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.13(1)).

> "For registered dental assistants, the Board adopts ... the American Dental Assistants Association (ADAA) Principles of Ethics and Professional Conduct as it may, from time to time, be amended." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.13(3)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- The codes apply "as it may, from time to time, be amended" (0460-01-.13(1)-(3)).

**Exceptions.**

- A code provision that conflicts with state law, rules or Board Position Statements does not apply (0460-01-.13(1)-(3)).

**Office checklist.**

- Keep a current copy of each code in the policy binder; replace it when the association amends it.
- Each year, staff sign that they have read the code for their role.

**Records to keep.**

No retention rule in this source.
