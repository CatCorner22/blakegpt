# Health Care Consumer Right-To-Know: malpractice and conviction reporting

**Rule.** A licensee must report malpractice judgments, awards or settlements of $25,000 or more, every felony conviction, and misdemeanor convictions involving sex, alcohol or drugs, injury or threats, abuse or neglect, or fraud or theft, under the Health Care Consumer Right-To-Know Act of 1998.

**Exact text.**

> "The threshold amount below which medical malpractice judgments, awards or settlements in which payments are awarded to complaining parties need not be reported pursuant to the "Health Care Consumer Right-To-Know Act of 1998" shall be twenty-five thousand dollars ($25,000)." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.17(1)).

> "Conviction of any felony; and" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.17(2)(a)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Malpractice reporting threshold: $25,000 (0460-01-.17(1)).
- Misdemeanor categories: 5 listed (0460-01-.17(2)(b)1.-5.).

**Exceptions.**

- Expunged misdemeanor: removed from the profile only after a judge-signed expungement order is submitted (0460-01-.17(2)(c)).

**Office checklist.**

- Remind each licensed dentist and hygienist at renewal to update the practitioner profile.
- Route any malpractice settlement of $25,000 or more to the dentist for reporting.
