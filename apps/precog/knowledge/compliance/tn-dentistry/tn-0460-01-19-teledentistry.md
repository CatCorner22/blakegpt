# Teledentistry: license, technology, consent and records

**Rule.** Only a dentist licensed in Tennessee may practice teledentistry on a patient in Tennessee, using secure video or store-and-forward technology, not audio only, email or fax. The dentist must obtain a signed informed consent that states the technology used, must keep records that meet Rule 0460-02-.12, and must not treat a patient under 18 by teledentistry without a parent or guardian present. Teledentistry does not change hygienist or assistant supervision rules.

**Exact text.**

> "Teledentistry shall not alter or amend the supervision requirements or procedures that are authorized for licensed dental hygienists or registered dental assistants" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.19).

> "Teledentistry as practiced under T.C.A § 63-5-108(b)(16) is not an audio only telephone conversation, email/instant messaging conversation or fax." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.19(1)(b)).

> "No patient seeking care via teledentistry who is under the age of eighteen (18) years of age can be treated unless there is a parent or guardian present, except as otherwise authorized by law." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.19(1)(d)).

> "Any dentist conducting a patient encounter via teledentistry must so document by an informed consent form which shall be added in the patient record and must state the technology used." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.19(2)(d)).

> "Any and all services provided via teledentistry shall be consistent with the in-person provision of those services." (T.C.A. § 63-5-108(b)(16) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Patients under 18: parent or guardian present (0460-01-.19(1)(d)).
- Records at every location where the patient is seen and where the dentist is (0460-01-.19(2)(b)).

**Exceptions.**

- "except as otherwise authorized by law" for minors (0460-01-.19(1)(d)).

**Office checklist.**

- Use a teledentistry consent form that names the technology and the information shared; file it in the chart.
- Confirm the remote dentist holds a Tennessee license before scheduling.
- A dentist website must show the dentist's full name and license type (0460-01-.19(2)(f)).
- The owner dentist approves the written teledentistry protocol required by T.C.A. § 63-5-108(b)(16).

**Records to keep.**

No separate retention rule; teledentistry records must contain the same information as Rule 0460-02-.12 records (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.19(2)(b)), so the 7-year rule applies.
