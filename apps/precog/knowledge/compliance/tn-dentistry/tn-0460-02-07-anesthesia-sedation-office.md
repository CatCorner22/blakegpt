# Anesthesia and sedation permits: what they mean for staff and the office (summary)

**Rule.** A dentist must hold a Board permit for conscious sedation or deep sedation/general anesthesia, and the office where the permit holder sedates must hold a dental facility permit and pass inspection every 5 years. Nitrous oxide and anxiolysis need no permit, but nitrous patients must be constantly monitored by an authorized person, and an office using anxiolysis must employ BLS-certified auxiliaries. The dentist must report an anesthesia-related injury needing hospitalization in writing within 30 days, and a death within 2 working days.

**Exact text.**

> "No permit is required for the administration of nitrous oxide inhalation analgesia; however, dentists must comply with the provisions of 0460-02-.07(4)." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.07(2)(a)).

> "An authorized person must constantly monitor each patient receiving nitrous oxide." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.07(4)(b)).

> "Dentists who administer conscious sedation by any method to children thirteen (13) and under must have a comprehensive conscious sedation permit." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.07(2)(c)3.(i)).

> "Members of the operating team must be trained for their duties according to protocol established by the dentist and must be currently certified in BLS for Healthcare Providers, or CPR/AED for Professional Rescuers, or an equivalent course" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.07(6)(b)2.(ii)).

> "A written report shall be submitted to the board by the dentist within thirty (30) days of any anesthesia-related incident resulting in patient injury or mortality" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.07(9)(a)).

> "this incident must be reported to the board within two (2) working days, to be followed by the written report within thirty (30) days" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.07(9)(a)).

> "The dental facility permit will expire five (5) years from the date of issuance or renewal of the dental facility permit." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.07(12)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Conscious sedation: at least 1 person besides the operating dentist present; clinical operatory and recovery staff must hold a current Tennessee license or registration (0460-02-.07(6)(b)2.(i), (iii)).
- Sedation equipment: inspect each day it is used and keep a log (0460-02-.07(6)(b)1.(ix)).
- Children 13 and under: comprehensive conscious sedation permit for any sedation other than nitrous oxide and oxygen (0460-02-.07(2)(c)3., (5)(c)).
- Nitrous equipment: no less than 30% oxygen and a scavenger (0460-02-.07(4)(d)-(e)).
- Injury requiring hospitalization: written report within 30 days; death: report within 2 working days, then written report within 30 days (0460-02-.07(9)(a)).
- Facility permit: expires 5 years from issuance or renewal; inspection every 5 years; Board notice up to 120 days before expiration; inspector report within 30 days (0460-02-.07(12)).
- Permit holders: 4 hours sedation CE within the 40 (0460-01-.05(1)(a)).

**Exceptions.**

- Only 1 facility permit is required per location (0460-02-.07(12)(a)).
- Exceptions to facility inspections exist in 0460-02-.07(15); not summarized here.

**Office checklist.**

- If any dentist sedates on site, keep the facility permit and inspection date on the compliance calendar; start inspection scheduling when the 120-day notice arrives.
- Keep the operating team's BLS cards and the written sedation protocol and training log; the permit holder signs the log.
- Keep the anesthesia incident report form ready; the permit holder files it.

**Records to keep.**

Rule 0460-02-.07(6) and (7) require that "Regular staff education programs and training sessions shall be provided and documented"; no retention period is stated. Keep the daily sedation equipment inspection log (0460-02-.07(6)(b)1.(ix)); no retention period is stated.
