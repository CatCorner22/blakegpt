# Hygienist scope that affects the assistant's work (summary)

**Rule.** Only a licensed hygienist (or dentist) may scale, root plane, perform curettage, expose radiographs without assistant radiology certification, give local anesthesia with a permit, or administer nitrous oxide; an assistant must not do these. A hygienist must perform root planing, curettage, nitrous administration and local anesthesia only under direct supervision. Starting January 1, 2027, a hygienist may take diagnostic radiographs, record tissue data, perform prophylaxis and apply fluoride before the dentist sees the patient.

**Exact text.**

> "duties involving the removal of hard and soft deposits by scaling or curretage from the surfaces of human teeth are assigned only to duly licensed and registered dental hygienists" (T.C.A. § 63-5-108(d) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

> "A dental hygienist shall perform, under direct supervision only, root planing, subgingival curettage, administering nitrous oxide, and local anesthesia." (T.C.A. § 63-5-108(c)(4) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

> "The serving as chairside assistant." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-03-.09(1)(i)).

> "The use of lasers for examination and/or for periodontal treatment under the supervision of a Tennessee licensed dentist." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-03-.09(1)(ww)).

> "Prior to a dentist seeing a new patient, a dental hygienist working under direct supervision of the dentist, or, prior to a dentist seeing an existing patient, a dental hygienist working under general supervision of the dentist may: (A) Complete diagnostic radiographs; (B) Assess and record existing hard and soft tissue data; (C) Perform prophylaxis; and (D) Apply fluoride agents." (2026 Tenn. Pub. Acts ch. 1107, § 1 (adding T.C.A. § 63-5-108(c)(6))).

> "For all other purposes, this act takes effect January 1, 2027, the public welfare requiring it." (2026 Tenn. Pub. Acts ch. 1107, § 4).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Hygienist general supervision limits: see tn-63-5-115-supervision-definitions (15 business days, 11-month exam, 3 hygienists).
- Hygienist prescriptions must be reviewed by a dentist within 30 days (T.C.A. § 63-5-108(h)(3)).
- Pub. Ch. 1107 takes effect January 1, 2027 for practice (rulemaking on enactment) (§ 4).
- Hygienist restorative or prosthetic course eligibility: 2 years full-time in the past 3 (0460-03-.10(1)).

**Exceptions.**

- Pub. Ch. 1107 § 2 expands hygienist prescriptive authority to fluoride agents, topical oral anesthetics, silver diamine fluoride, curodont, sealants and desiccation therapy that are not controlled substances.

**Office checklist.**

- Write duty assignments so that scaling, root planing, curettage, local anesthesia and nitrous administration go only to the hygienist or dentist.
- Before January 1, 2027, update the new-patient intake workflow to reflect Pub. Ch. 1107; the owner dentist approves.
- Watch for the Board rule implementing Pub. Ch. 1107 (listed on the July 2026 Board agenda).
