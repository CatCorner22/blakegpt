# Dental assistant scope of practice: delegable and prohibited duties

**Rule.** A dentist may delegate to a practical or registered dental assistant only the duties in 0460-04-.08(3) and duties commonly recognized as safe, and only under direct supervision. Only a registered assistant with the matching Board certification may polish, monitor nitrous oxide, place sealants, perform expanded restorative or prosthetic functions, or expose radiographs. No assistant may perform any duty in 0460-04-.08(4), such as diagnosis, cutting tissue, giving local anesthesia or nitrous oxide, or using a high-speed handpiece intraorally.

**Exact text.**

> "A lawfully licensed and duly registered dentist may delegate to dental assistants those procedures for which they have received adequate training and for which the dentist exercises direct supervision and full responsibility" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.08(1)).

> "a practical dental assistant or registered dental assistant may perform the following duties which are assigned or delegated by the employer/supervising dentist" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.08(3)).

> "The processing of radiographs, including digital, of the mouth, gums, jaws, teeth or any portion thereof for dental diagnosis." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.08(3)(a)).

> "Calling in prescriptions to the pharmacist as instructed by the employer/dentist." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.08(3)(ll)).

> "Administration of local anesthesia, nitrous oxide, conscious sedation, or general anesthesia;" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.08(4)(h)).

> "Use of a high-speed handpiece intraorally;" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.08(4)(l)).

> "Only dentists licensed by the Tennessee Board of Dentistry shall be authorized to perform procedures involving laser technology." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.08(4)(m)).

> "duties involving the removal of hard and soft deposits by scaling or curretage from the surfaces of human teeth are assigned only to duly licensed and registered dental hygienists" (T.C.A. § 63-5-108(d) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Delegable duties for practical and registered assistants: 0460-04-.08(3)(a) through (qq), 43 listed items, including processing (not exposing) radiographs, topical fluoride, vitals and medical history, infection control, alginate impressions not for permanent restorations, rubber dam, temporary restorations, suture removal, orthodontic band and wire tasks, topical anesthetic, cavity bases and liners, pulp testing, and calling in prescriptions as instructed.
- Certification-only duties for registered assistants: coronal polishing, nitrous oxide monitoring, sealants, expanded restorative, expanded prosthetic, exposure of radiographs (0460-04-.08(2)(a)-(f)).
- Prohibited for all assistants: 0460-04-.08(4)(a)-(o), 15 items.
- Unregistered (practical) assistant: may do only (3) duties under direct supervision; must not do any (2) certification duty (0460-04-.01(1)(b)2.; 0460-04-.08(2)).

**Exceptions.**

- 0460-04-.08(3)(qq): "Other duties specifically approved by the Board at a regularly scheduled meeting of the Board."
- T.C.A. § 63-5-115(a): assistants may practice outside the dentist's office in "authorized public health programs."

**Office checklist.**

- Post a duty matrix in each operatory: column 1 = all assistants (0460-04-.08(3)); column 2 = registered plus the named certification (0460-04-.08(2)); column 3 = never (0460-04-.08(4)).
- At hire and each year, have each assistant sign the matrix; the supervising dentist countersigns.
- Never let a practical (unregistered) assistant expose radiographs, polish, place sealants or monitor nitrous oxide.
- Quarterly, spot-check the schedule against the certification roster.
