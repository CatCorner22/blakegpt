# Tennessee Disability Act and guide dogs

**Rule.** A covered employer must not discriminate in hiring, firing, or other employment terms based solely on physical, mental, or visual disability, subject to the statutory job-performance exception. A covered employer must not discriminate against a blind person for using a guide dog.

**Exact text.**

> “unless such disability to some degree prevents the applicant from performing the duties required by the employment sought or impairs the performance of the work involved” (T.C.A. § 8-50-103(b) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “eight (8) or more persons within the state” (T.C.A. § 8-50-103(d) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “office of the attorney general and reporter” (T.C.A. § 8-50-103(c) — unofficial Justia mirror)

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Employer coverage starts at 8 persons in Tennessee (T.C.A. § 8-50-103(d)).
- Following the 2025 transfer of enforcement, administrative complaints go to the Attorney General under the Human Rights Act process with its 180-day deadline (T.C.A. §§ 8-50-103(c), 4-21-204(e), 2025 Tenn. Pub. Acts ch. 471).

**Exceptions.**

- The Tennessee text contains a job-performance exception and no express reasonable-accommodation command. Check federal ADA and pregnancy-accommodation duties before applying the state exception (T.C.A. § 8-50-103(b)).

**Office checklist.**

- At each disability-related decision, identify actual job duties and route any restriction or accommodation request to the owner for review.
- Keep medical material in restricted office records.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
