# Tennessee employment discrimination and retaliation

**Rule.** A covered employer must not discriminate in employment because of race, creed, color, religion, sex, age, or national origin. A person must not retaliate against someone who opposes a practice the Act prohibits or who participates in a charge, complaint, investigation, or proceeding.

**Exact text.**

> “eight (8) or more individuals within this state” (T.C.A. § 4-21-102(8) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “race, creed, color, religion, sex, age, or national origin” (T.C.A. § 4-21-401(a)(1) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “made a charge, filed a complaint, testified, assisted or participated” (T.C.A. § 4-21-301(a) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “within one hundred eighty (180) days” (T.C.A. § 4-21-204(e) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “within one (1) year after the alleged discriminatory practice ceases” (T.C.A. § 4-21-311(d) — unofficial Justia mirror)

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Coverage starts at 8 individuals in Tennessee (T.C.A. § 4-21-102(8)). The rule covers hiring, discharge, pay, employment terms, and discriminatory classifications (T.C.A. § 4-21-401(a)).
- An administrative complaint goes to the Attorney General's Civil Rights Enforcement Division within 180 days of the alleged act. The private action deadline is 1 year after the alleged practice ceases (T.C.A. §§ 4-21-204(e), 4-21-311(d)).
- The 2025 law replaced the Human Rights Commission's enforcement role with the Civil Rights Enforcement Division. The Attorney General announced the opening on July 1, 2025 (2025 Tenn. Pub. Acts ch. 471 and Attorney General announcement, agency guidance).

**Exceptions.**

- The employment section excludes employment by the individual's parent, spouse, or child and domestic service for the employer (T.C.A. § 4-21-401(b)).
- An English-only policy requires a legitimate business necessity and prior notice of the policy and the consequences of violating it (T.C.A. § 4-21-401(c)).

**Office checklist.**

- At each hiring, pay, or discipline decision, apply consistent job criteria. Have the owner review the documented reason before a contested action.
- Route a complaint promptly to the owner. Preserve relevant records and check subsequent schedule, pay, and discipline changes for retaliation.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
