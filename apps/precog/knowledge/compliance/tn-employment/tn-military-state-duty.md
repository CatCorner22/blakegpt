# Tennessee state military and Civil Air Patrol leave

**Rule.** An employer must grant unpaid leave and apply equivalent federal reemployment protections for an eligible employee on qualifying Tennessee active state duty. The employee must give advance notice of the orders unless notice is impossible or unreasonable.

**Exact text.**

> “An unpaid leave of absence” (T.C.A. § 8-33-110(a)(1)(A) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “Equivalent protections regarding the right to reemployment” (T.C.A. § 8-33-110(a)(1)(B) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “all other public employees of this state” (T.C.A. § 8-33-109(a) — unofficial Justia mirror)

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Tennessee Army or Air National Guard, Tennessee State Guard, or Civil Air Patrol members must be on qualifying active state duty under one of the authorities listed in T.C.A. § 8-33-110(a)(1). Public Chapter 687 added qualifying call-up authorities in 2024 (2024 Tenn. Pub. Acts ch. 687, § 1). Preserve regular leave or vacation and pay unrelated to time on leave (T.C.A. § 8-33-110(a)(1)(A)).
- For service of 30 days or less, report by the first full regular shift after safe return home and 8 hours of rest. For 31 through 180 days, apply within 14 days after service. For more than 180 days, apply within 90 days after service (subsection (a)(2)(A)–(C)).

**Exceptions.**

- Where timely reporting for service of 30 days or less or applying after service of 31–180 days is not reasonably practicable through no fault of the employee, the employee acts as soon as reasonably practicable (T.C.A. § 8-33-110(a)(2)(A)–(B)).
- T.C.A. § 8-33-109 provides public-sector reservist leave. Its state pay rule does not apply to a private dental office.

**Office checklist.**

- When orders arrive, log the leave and run the state and federal coverage checks together. Have the owner approve the return-to-work plan at the end of service.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
