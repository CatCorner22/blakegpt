# Tennessee distribution of mandatory service charges and gratuities

**Rule.** A business must distribute a covered automatic service charge or gratuity to the employees who rendered the service by the statutory deadline. A business must not reduce a payment derived from a mandatory service charge or tip as a penalty for employment-related conduct.

**Exact text.**

> “customarily assumed to be intended for the employee or employees who have served the customer” (T.C.A. § 50-2-107(a)(1) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “shall not be reduced, docked or otherwise diminished to penalize an employee” (T.C.A. § 50-2-107(a)(2) — unofficial Justia mirror)

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- When a customer pays a qualifying bill, distribute the charge at close of business on the day of receipt or at regular payday (T.C.A. § 50-2-107(a)(1)).
- If the business extends credit, distribute the charge at close of business on the day collected or on the first regular payday after collection (T.C.A. § 50-2-107(a)(1)).
- The Labor and Workforce Development FAQ directs general tip questions to the U.S. Department of Labor (agency guidance). The state statute explicitly covers this mandatory-charge situation (T.C.A. § 50-2-107(a)).

**Exceptions.**

- The statute excludes specified food or beverage bills at segregated event facilities and certain charges at qualifying guest ranches (T.C.A. § 50-2-107(a)(3)). A voluntary gratuity does not meet the automatic bill-charge trigger.

**Office checklist.**

- Before adding any service charge, have the owner approve the billing wording and distribution process. At each payroll that includes a qualifying charge, have the payroll lead reconcile collections and distribution.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
