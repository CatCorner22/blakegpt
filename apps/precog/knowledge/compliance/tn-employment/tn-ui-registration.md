# Unemployment insurance registration and premium reporting

**Rule.** A Tennessee employer must register with the Department of Labor and Workforce Development for a determination of unemployment insurance liability. A liable practice must file wage and premium reports and pay premiums quarterly.

**Exact text.**

> “Every employer in Tennessee is required to complete an online registration.” (Tennessee Labor and Workforce Development, Unemployment Insurance Tax (agency guidance))

> “one thousand five hundred dollars ($1,500) or more” (T.C.A. § 50-7-205(1)(A) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “not less than seven (7) years” (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-10-03-.10(8) — Cornell LII mirror)

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- A typical private practice is liable if it paid at least $1,500 in covered wages in any quarter of the current or preceding calendar year or employed at least 1 person for part of a day in each of 20 different weeks in either year. The weeks need not be consecutive or involve the same employee (T.C.A. § 50-7-205(1)(A)–(B)).
- For a liable employer the agency issues an 8-digit account number (Unemployment Insurance Tax, agency guidance).
- Quarterly premiums are generally due by the last day of the month after the quarter (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-10-01-.02(1)). Initial back reports are due by the last day of the month after liability notice (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-10-03-.10(7)(d)–(e)).

**Exceptions.**

- Other liability tests cover cases such as acquiring a covered business (T.C.A. § 50-7-205(2)–(3)). Electronic reporting hardship relief requires the statutory affidavit process (T.C.A. § 50-7-404(c)(3)).

**Office checklist.**

- At setup or acquisition, register in Employer e-Services and route the determination to the owner. Each quarter have the payroll lead reconcile wages, submit the report, and confirm payment.

**Records to keep.** Preserve the payroll and employment records required by the rule for at least 7 years (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-10-03-.10(4)–(8)).
