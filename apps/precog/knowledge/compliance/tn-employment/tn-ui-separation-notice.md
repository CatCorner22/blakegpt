# Unemployment separation notice at departure

**Rule.** An employer must complete and deliver the Department’s separation notice for an indefinite separation or an expected separation of 7 or more days, subject to the rule’s exceptions.

**Exact text.**

> “an indefinite period or for an expected duration of seven (7) days or more” (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-09-01-.02(1) — Cornell LII mirror)

> “within twenty-four (24) hours after the worker’s separation from employment” (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-09-01-.02(1)(a) — Cornell LII mirror)

> “Separation Notice” (Tennessee Department of Labor and Workforce Development, Form LB-0489, Instructions)

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Supply Form LB-0489 within 24 hours after an indefinite separation or one expected to last 7 or more days (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-09-01-.02(1)(a)–(b)).

**Exceptions.**

- A lack-of-work separation after less than 1 week of continuous employment is excluded unless the Department requests the notice (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-09-01-.02(2)).
- The Administrator waives the ordinary notice under a prior agreement for an approved electronic method, mass separation notice, or mail-in claim (subsection (3)).

**Office checklist.**

- At every separation, have the owner confirm dates and reason. Deliver the current state form and preserve evidence of delivery in restricted office records.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
