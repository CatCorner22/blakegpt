# Workers’ compensation coverage for dental offices

**Rule.** A dental practice that regularly employs 5 or more persons must buy and maintain workers’ compensation insurance or hold a valid state certificate of authority to self-insure. A smaller dental practice may elect coverage by buying a policy.

**Exact text.**

> “fewer than five (5) persons are regularly employed” (T.C.A. § 50-6-106(5) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “Insure and keep insured the employer’s liability under this chapter” (T.C.A. § 50-6-405(a)(1) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “construction services provider” (T.C.A. § 50-6-902(a) — unofficial Justia mirror)

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- The dental practice threshold is 5 regularly employed persons (T.C.A. § 50-6-106(5)). The Bureau's Non-Construction page (agency guidance) discusses part-time workers and treatment of family and owners when determining headcount.
- A covered employer keeps insurance in force or holds a valid state self-insurance certificate (T.C.A. § 50-6-405(a)).

**Exceptions.**

- The construction coverage rule in T.C.A. § 50-6-902 is a sector-specific rule. A non-construction employer below the normal threshold may elect coverage (T.C.A. § 50-6-106(5)).

**Office checklist.**

- Review headcount at every hire, staffing change, and ownership change. Have the owner approve coverage before the threshold is reached.
- At each renewal, check the policy dates, named employer, and work locations with the broker.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
