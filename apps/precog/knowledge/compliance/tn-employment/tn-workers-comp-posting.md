# Workers’ compensation posting at every worksite

**Rule.** A covered employer must continuously post the Bureau of Workers’ Compensation notice in a conspicuous place at each worksite.

**Exact text.**

> “continuous posting of the Tennessee Workers’ Compensation Posting Notice” (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-02-01-.12(2) — Cornell LII mirror)

> “in one (1) or more conspicuous place(s) at each worksite” (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-02-01-.12(2) — Cornell LII mirror)

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Post in at least 1 conspicuous place at each worksite. The adjusting entity must send the notice within 5 business days of policy procurement or renewal (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-02-01-.12(1)–(2)).

**Exceptions.** None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- At opening, each renewal, and any contact change, fill out the current notice and display it at each office. Have the owner verify the contacts and locations against bureau guidance.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
