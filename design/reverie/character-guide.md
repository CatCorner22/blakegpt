# Reverie — character guide

Character name: **Reverie**. Brand family: **Focus**, **Vision**, **Horizon**, **Telescope**, **Target**, and **Stratosphere**. Original saved reference set: **Aim & Imagine**, November 18, 2026; matching feature artwork and Garden Path poses were subsequently requested and added.

Pronouns: **she/her**. Her name means **a pleasant daydream**. The user selected Reverie as her current name; keep that name consistent in new writing, asset labels, and future character work. The name change does not authorize a redesign of her approved artwork.

Reverie should feel gentle, cute, kawaii, professional, reassuring, and quietly cheerful. She is friendly, artistic, and thoughtful. She adds warmth without making work feel childish or distracting from the task. See `ABOUT-REVERIE.md` for her introduction.

## Visual identity

- Cream fur with a warm ginger patch over the forehead on the **viewer's right**. Keep this orientation consistent; do not mirror the character casually.
- Ginger inner ears and rounded ginger cheek patches. Preserve the reference's placement, shape, and warmth.
- Thick, smooth, dark forest-green outlines and facial details. Use rounded ends and joins, with a simple readable silhouette.
- Large expressive forest-green eyes, white eye areas and highlights, small dark nose, tiny friendly curved mouth, and short whiskers.
- Rounded triangular ears, a generously sized head, compact body, and soft rounded paws. Keep the proportions close to the supplied artwork.
- Restrained palette: cream, forest green, ginger, and coral; small golden-yellow and leaf-green accents where the prop needs them. The supplied PNGs are the color reference. Preserve their gentle tonal variation rather than inventing a new palette.
- Flat illustrated appearance with minimal detail. The approved assets have subtle color variation; do not turn Reverie into photorealistic fur, a glossy 3D toy, an anime character, or a different cartoon cat.

## Consistency when creating another pose

1. Attach the original approved board and the closest relevant PNG from this kit as references.
2. Name the character **Reverie** and explicitly preserve the existing face, patch orientation, outline weight, palette, proportions, and simple illustration style.
3. Change only the pose, expression, and the single prop needed for the task. Keep expressions warm and restrained.
4. Prefer an isolated transparent background and no baked-in text for reusable illustrations and stickers. For official logos, preserve the approved lockups.
5. Inspect the result beside the references. Check eyes, ears, ginger patch, cheeks, whiskers, paws, outline, and silhouette before accepting it into the master set.
6. Review at the intended small display size. Keep the character recognizable and ensure props do not look aggressive or cluttered.

Suggested reference prompt:

> Create a new pose of **Reverie**, the exact cream-and-ginger cat shown in the attached approved references. Preserve the ginger forehead patch on the viewer's right, ginger cheeks and inner ears, large expressive forest-green eyes, short whiskers, friendly small mouth, compact body, round paws, and thick smooth forest-green outlines. Use the same gentle, cute, kawaii, professional illustration style and warm restrained palette. Change only [pose and prop]. Keep the expression calm and cheerful. Use a transparent background, no words, and no extra scene. Do not redesign or mirror the character.

This prompt is a reusable starting point, not a guarantee of identical generation. The approved images remain the source of truth.

## Use in interfaces and customer materials

Use Reverie sparingly at welcoming, creative, saved-work, and handoff moments. Keep essential instructions and status messages in readable text. Decorative illustrations should not imply a safety guarantee, replace an error message, or suggest that testing has passed before it has.

She guides preparation and helps make the next step approachable. She is not a test certification, security guarantee, or substitute for evidence that the actual customer project works.

Reverie belongs to Focus/Vision's identity. Do not automatically add the mascot or wordmarks to a customer's exported website or app. Use the transparent PNGs at their correct aspect ratio, preserve clear space, and avoid cropping ears, whiskers, or props.

**Cat Samurai** is Blake's app design and engineering principle: a calm, playful, intuitive surface with disciplined, reliable, powerful, clear, and logical behavior underneath. It calls for cool app surfaces and rejects beige as the app's default aesthetic. It does **not** change Reverie's approved cream fur or ginger markings. Keep character colors intact. The current cool transparent river is separate app scenery; an earlier beige river is not included in the active kit.

## Master files and future formats

The original PNG images in this kit preserve the existing artwork byte-for-byte. They are raster sources, not editable vector masters. The `code/public/brand/` folder separately preserves the copied app delivery assets. Retain the originals before making format-specific derivatives. Sticker platform size and packaging requirements should be checked when a particular sticker workflow is chosen; this archive itself does not install anything on an iPhone.
