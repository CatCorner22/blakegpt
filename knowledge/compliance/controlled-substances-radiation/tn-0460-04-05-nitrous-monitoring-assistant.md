# Registered dental assistant nitrous oxide monitoring certification

**Rule.** A dental assistant must not administer nitrous oxide and may monitor it only after Board certification, under a dentist's direct supervision, 1 patient at a time, physically remaining with the patient. The assistant may adjust or stop nitrous only at the dentist's direction and protocol or in patient distress.

**Exact text.**

> "Dental assistants may not administer nitrous oxide to patients but may monitor nitrous oxide sedation" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.05, Cornell LII mirror).

> "This assistant shall not monitor more than one (1) patient at a time and shall physically remain with the patient at all times." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.05(5), Cornell LII mirror).

> "This assistant is only permitted to adjust the dosage or terminate the nitrous oxide at the specific direction and under the protocol of the supervising dentist or in cases of patient distress." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.05(6), Cornell LII mirror).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- 1 patient at a time.

**Exceptions.**

None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- Verify each assistant's certification on the Board license lookup before scheduling them on nitrous; recheck at each registration renewal.

**Records to keep.**

No retention rule in this source; keep a copy of each certification in the personnel file.
