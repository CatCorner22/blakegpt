# Locked storage of controlled substances in the dental office

**Rule.** The office must store Schedule II-V controlled substances in a securely locked, substantially constructed cabinet. The office must keep effective controls and procedures to guard against theft and diversion.

**Exact text.**

> "All applicants and registrants shall provide effective controls and procedures to guard against theft and diversion of controlled substances." (21 CFR 1301.71(a)).

> "Controlled substances listed in Schedules II, III, IV, and V shall be stored in a securely locked, substantially constructed cabinet." (21 CFR 1301.75(b)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Applies to every Schedule II-V substance on hand; no quantity threshold.

**Exceptions.**

- The dispersal-in-stock alternative in 21 CFR 1301.75(b) is for pharmacies and institutional practitioners, not a private dental office.

**Office checklist.**

- Keep the cabinet locked; limit keys or codes to named staff; review the key list every 90 days. Dentist-registrant signs off.

**Records to keep.**

No retention rule in this source.
