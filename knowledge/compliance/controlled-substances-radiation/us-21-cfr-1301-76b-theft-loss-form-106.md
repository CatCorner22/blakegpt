# Report theft or significant loss of controlled substances (DEA Form 106)

**Rule.** The registrant must notify the DEA Field Division Office in writing within 1 business day after discovering a theft or significant loss of a controlled substance. The registrant must file a complete DEA Form 106 online within 45 days after discovery.

**Exact text.**

> "The registrant shall notify the Field Division Office of the Administration in his area, in writing, of the theft or significant loss of any controlled substances within one business day of discovery of such loss or theft." (21 CFR 1301.76(b)).

> "The registrant must also file a complete and accurate DEA Form 106 with the Administration through DEA's Diversion Control Division secure network application within 45 days after discovery of the theft or loss." (21 CFR 1301.76(b)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Written notice: 1 business day after discovery.
- DEA Form 106: 45 days after discovery.
- "Significant" is judged by the factors in 21 CFR 1301.76(b)(1)-(6) (quantity, substance, pattern, diversion potential, local trends).
- Amendment check: the eCFR source note cites 88 FR 40712 (June 22, 2023), which set the online Form 106 process.

**Exceptions.**

None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- On any count discrepancy, dentist decides same day whether it is significant; Amanda sends written notice next business day and calendars day 45.

**Records to keep.**

No retention rule in this source; keep the Form 106 copy with controlled-substance records for at least 2 years (21 CFR 1304.04(a), see us-21-cfr-1304-03-04-practitioner-records).
