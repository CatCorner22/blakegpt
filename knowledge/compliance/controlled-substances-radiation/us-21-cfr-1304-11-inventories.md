# Initial and biennial controlled-substance inventories

**Rule.** The office must take an initial inventory on the first day it dispenses controlled substances, recording a zero inventory if none are on hand, and a new inventory at least every 2 years. Each inventory must be complete, accurate and kept in written, typed or printed form at the registered location.

**Exact text.**

> "In the event a person commences business with no controlled substances on hand, he/she shall record this fact as the initial inventory." (21 CFR 1304.11(b)).

> "the registrant shall take a new inventory of all stocks of controlled substances on hand at least every two years." (21 CFR 1304.11(c)).

> "If the substance is listed in Schedule I or II, make an exact count or measure of the contents ... Schedule III, IV, or V, make an estimated count or measure of the contents, unless the container holds more than 1,000 tablets or capsules in which case he/she must make an exact count of the contents." (21 CFR 1304.11(e)(1)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Biennial: at least every 2 years, on any date within 2 years of the previous one (21 CFR 1304.11(c)).
- Opened Schedule III-V containers over 1,000 tablets or capsules: exact count.
- New-schedule substances: inventory on the scheduling rule effective date (21 CFR 1304.11(d)).

**Exceptions.**

None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- Calendar the biennial inventory 23 months after the last one; dentist and 1 witness count and sign.

**Records to keep.**

Keep each inventory at the registered location at least 2 years (21 CFR 1304.04(a), (b)(1)).
