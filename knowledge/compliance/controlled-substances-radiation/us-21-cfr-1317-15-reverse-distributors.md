# Reverse distributors for controlled-substance returns

**Rule.** The office may send controlled substances for destruction only to a person registered with DEA as a reverse distributor. The reverse distributor must destroy the substance within 30 calendar days after receipt.

**Exact text.**

> "Any person that reverse distributes a controlled substance shall be registered with the Administration as a reverse distributor, unless exempted by law or otherwise authorized pursuant to this chapter." (21 CFR 1317.15(a)).

> "no later than 30 calendar days after receipt." (21 CFR 1317.15(d)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- 30 calendar days from receipt for reverse-distributor destruction.

**Exceptions.**

None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- Verify the vendor's DEA reverse-distributor registration before each pickup.

**Records to keep.**

No retention rule in this source.
