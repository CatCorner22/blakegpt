# GINA employer coverage

**Rule.** The owner dentist must assess GINA employer coverage using its incorporated Title VII definition.

**Exact text.**
> "an employer (as defined in section 2000e(b) of this title)" (42 U.S.C. 2000ff(2)(B)(i)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- 15 employees on each working day in 20 calendar weeks in the current or preceding calendar year under the incorporated definition (42 U.S.C. 2000ff(2)(B)(i); 42 U.S.C. 2000e(b)).

**Exceptions.**
- The regulation excludes Indian tribes and specified tax-exempt private clubs (29 CFR 1635.2(d)).

**Office checklist.**
- Review coverage with each staffing change; owner dentist signs off.
- Keep the coverage analysis and supporting payroll or request records as office practice, subject to applicable retention and litigation holds.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
