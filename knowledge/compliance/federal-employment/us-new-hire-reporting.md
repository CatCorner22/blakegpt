# Report newly hired employees

**Rule.** The practice must report newly hired employees to the appropriate State Directory under the applicable reporting method. The practice must not treat payroll setup or employment verification as a substitute for new-hire reporting.

**Exact text.**
> "not later than 20 days after the date the employer hires the employee" (42 U.S.C. 653a(b)(2)(A)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- The federal outside deadline is 20 days after hire under the ordinary reporting method; state law determines the reporting time within the statutory requirements (42 U.S.C. 653a(b)(2)(A)).
- For magnetic or electronic reporting, the statute permits 2 monthly transmissions if necessary, at least 12 and no more than 16 days apart (42 U.S.C. 653a(b)(2)(B)).
- The statutory newly hired definition includes a rehire after at least 60 consecutive days separated (42 U.S.C. 653a(a)(2)).

**Exceptions.**
- Multistate employers transmitting electronically can designate a single qualifying state and must notify the federal Secretary as provided; this is not an automatic exception from reporting (42 U.S.C. 653a(b)(1)(B)).

**Office checklist.**
- For every hire and covered rehire, confirm the correct state destination, required data, and submission method; save the acknowledgment; owner dentist signs off on payroll's reporting workflow.
