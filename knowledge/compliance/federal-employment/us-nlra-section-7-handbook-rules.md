# Protected concerted activity and handbook rules

**Rule.** Covered employers must not interfere with protected concerted activity. The practice must review confidentiality, social-media, and wage-discussion rules for unlawful restrictions before issuing or enforcing them.

**Exact text.**
> "to engage in other concerted activities for the purpose of collective bargaining or other mutual aid or protection" (29 U.S.C. 157).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- The NLRB news release on Stericycle describes a rule as presumptively unlawful when the General Counsel proves a reasonable tendency to chill protected activity; the employer can rebut with a legitimate substantial interest that cannot be advanced by a narrower rule (Stericycle news release, standard discussion).
- The later NLRB news release describes enforcement and legal policy priorities, not a Board decision overruling Stericycle (NLRB news release on GC Memorandum 26-04).

**Exceptions.**
- NLRA jurisdiction and employee exclusions require separate screening. The agency explanatory pages and news releases are guidance or agency summaries, not statutory text or the full Board opinion.

**Office checklist.**
- Before publishing, revising, or enforcing handbook rules, distinguish patient confidentiality from employee wage and working-condition discussions; owner dentist and counsel sign off.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
