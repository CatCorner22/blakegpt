# Pregnancy accommodation and the partial vacatur

**Rule.** Covered employers must provide reasonable accommodations for known pregnancy-related limitations of qualified employees unless the accommodation imposes undue hardship.

**Exact text.**
> "reasonable accommodations to the known limitations related to the pregnancy, childbirth, or related medical conditions of a qualified employee" (42 U.S.C. 2000gg-1(1)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- 15-employee employer threshold (42 U.S.C. 2000gg(2)(B)(i)).
- The court opinion in State of Louisiana v. EEOC (No. 2:24-cv-00629, W.D. La., May 21, 2025, memorandum ruling, conclusion) partially vacated the regulation's inclusion of abortion as a related medical condition; the fetched eCFR text still includes abortion (29 CFR 1636.3(b)).

**Exceptions.**
- Undue hardship is a statutory defense; employers must not require leave if another reasonable accommodation can be provided (42 U.S.C. 2000gg-1(1), (4)).

**Office checklist.**
- Log each request promptly, discuss workable adjustments, and obtain owner dentist sign-off before any denial; flag the partial vacatur for counsel.
- Keep the coverage analysis and supporting payroll or request records as office practice, subject to applicable retention and litigation holds.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
