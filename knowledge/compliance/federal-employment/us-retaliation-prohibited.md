# Do not retaliate over protected discrimination or wage-hour activity

**Rule.** Covered employers must not retaliate against employees or applicants for protected opposition or participation in discrimination proceedings. The practice must not discharge or otherwise discriminate against employees for FLSA complaints, proceedings, testimony, or protected industry-committee service. The owner dentist must review proposed adverse actions involving protected activity for independent, documented reasons.

**Exact text.**
> "because he has made a charge, testified, assisted, or participated in any manner in an investigation, proceeding, or hearing under this subchapter" (42 U.S.C. 2000e-3(a)).

> "because such employee has filed any complaint or instituted or caused to be instituted any proceeding under or related to this chapter" (29 U.S.C. 215(a)(3)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Protection attaches to the opposition and participation described in the relevant statute; no employee filing deadline is created by these retaliation provisions (42 U.S.C. 2000e-3(a); 29 U.S.C. 623(d)).
- FLSA protection includes employees who have testified or are about to testify in a covered proceeding, or have served or are about to serve on an industry committee (29 U.S.C. 215(a)(3)).

**Exceptions.**
- Coverage and the scope of protected activity depend on the statute and facts. EEOC retaliation material is guidance, not law.

**Office checklist.**
- For each complaint, investigation, or subsequent discipline decision, restrict retaliatory schedule, pay, or assignment changes; owner dentist and counsel review the documented reasons.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
