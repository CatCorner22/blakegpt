# Tennessee healthcare-provider noncompetes, including dentists: 2-year and geographic safe limits

**Rule.** A practice may restrict an employed or contracted dentist after the relationship ends, and the restriction is deemed reasonable if it is in a signed writing, lasts 2 years or less, and covers no more than the greater of a 10-mile radius or the county of the primary practice site, or only the facilities where the practice provided services.

**Exact text.**

> "The duration of the restriction is two (2) years or less and either:" (T.C.A. 63-1-148(a)(2)).

> "(i) A ten-mile radius from the primary practice site of the healthcare provider while employed or contracted; or (ii) The county in which the primary practice of the healthcare provider while employed or contracted is located" (T.C.A. 63-1-148(a)(2)(A)).

> "This section shall apply to healthcare providers licensed under chapters 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9 and 11 of this title." (T.C.A. 63-1-148(c)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Duration: 2 years or less (T.C.A. 63-1-148(a)(2)).
- Area: greater of a 10-mile radius or the county (T.C.A. 63-1-148(a)(2)(A)), or facility-only restriction (T.C.A. 63-1-148(a)(2)(B)).

**Exceptions.**

- Sale-of-practice covenants are tested for reasonableness instead (T.C.A. 63-1-148(b)).
- Does not apply to physicians specializing in emergency medicine (T.C.A. 63-1-148(d)).
- No 6-year same-employer exception appears in the text.

**Office checklist.**

- Check each associate-dentist and hygienist agreement against the 2-year and 10-mile-or-county limits.
- Make sure both parties sign the restriction.
- Counsel reviews; owner dentist signs.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
