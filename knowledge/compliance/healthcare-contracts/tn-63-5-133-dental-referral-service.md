# Tennessee: dental referral services must register and may not charge per referral

**Rule.** A dentist must not agree to accept patients from a dental referral service unless the service meets all 4 requirements in T.C.A. 63-5-133(a), including Board registration and no fee tied to referral count or patient fees. A participating dentist must not charge a referred patient more than the dentist's usual and customary fee.

**Exact text.**

> "It does not impose a fee on participating dentists dependent on the number of referrals or amount of professional fees paid by the patient to the dentist" (T.C.A. 63-5-133(a)(3)).

> "Participating dentists shall not enter into an agreement to accept for dental care or treatment a person referred or recommended by a dental referral service unless the dental referral service meets all the requirements of subsection (a)." (T.C.A. 63-5-133(b)).

> "Participating dentists shall charge no more than their usual and customary fees to any patient so referred or recommended." (T.C.A. 63-5-133(b)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- 4 requirements, all required (T.C.A. 63-5-133(a)(1)-(4)).
- 2 required advertising disclaimer statements (T.C.A. 63-5-133(d)(1)-(2)).

**Exceptions.**

- None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- Before joining any lead-generation or referral platform, get proof of Board registration and its fee schedule.
- Reject per-lead or per-booked-patient pricing.
- Owner dentist signs the contract after review.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
