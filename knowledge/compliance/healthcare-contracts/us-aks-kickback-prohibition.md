# Anti-Kickback Statute: no pay for referrals of Federal health care program business

**Rule.** The practice and its staff must not knowingly and willfully offer, pay, solicit or receive any remuneration to induce or reward referrals of items or services payable by a Federal health care program, including TennCare dental. The practice may rely on a regulatory safe harbor in 42 CFR 1001.952 when every standard of that safe harbor is met.

**Exact text.**

> "Whoever knowingly and willfully solicits or receives any remuneration (including any kickback, bribe, or rebate) directly or indirectly, overtly or covertly, in cash or in kind— (A) in return for referring an individual to a person for the furnishing or arranging for the furnishing of any item or service for which payment may be made in whole or in part under a Federal health care program" (42 U.S.C. 1320a-7b(b)(1)(A)).

> "shall be guilty of a felony and upon conviction thereof, shall be fined not more than $100,000 or imprisoned for not more than 10 years, or both." (42 U.S.C. 1320a-7b(b)(1)).

> "a claim that includes items or services resulting from a violation of this section constitutes a false or fraudulent claim for purposes of subchapter III of chapter 37 of title 31." (42 U.S.C. 1320a-7b(g)).

> "a person need not have actual knowledge of this section or specific intent to commit a violation of this section." (42 U.S.C. 1320a-7b(h)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Criminal fine: not more than $100,000 per violation (42 U.S.C. 1320a-7b(b)(1), (b)(2)).
- Prison: not more than 10 years (same).
- Any claim tainted by a kickback is a false claim under the False Claims Act (42 U.S.C. 1320a-7b(g)).
- TennCare reach: "Federal health care program" in 42 U.S.C. 1320a-7b(f) includes a State health care program under 42 U.S.C. 1320a-7(h), which covers a State Medicaid plan; TennCare dental claims are paid under Tennessee's Medicaid program.

**Exceptions.**

- The statutory exceptions in 42 U.S.C. 1320a-7b(b)(3), including properly disclosed discounts, bona fide employee pay, and payment practices specified by regulation (the safe harbors in 42 CFR 1001.952).
- Patients paying cash only, with no Federal health care program payment possible, fall outside the statute; Tennessee and commercial-payer rules may still apply (see tn-63-5-124-fee-splitting-commissions).

**Office checklist.**

- Before signing any lease, management, marketing, or referral-source agreement, route it to the owner dentist and practice counsel for AKS review.
- Log every gift, meal, or payment to or from a referral source (oral surgeon, lab, specialist, vendor) quarterly; owner dentist signs off.
- Never pay staff or outside marketers per new TennCare patient.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source. Keep the arrangement log and signed agreements for as long as the related claims can be challenged (office policy; the False Claims Act limitations period in 31 U.S.C. 3731(b) was not fetched).
