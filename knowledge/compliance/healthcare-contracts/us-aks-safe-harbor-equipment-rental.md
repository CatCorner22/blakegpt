# AKS safe harbor: equipment rental

**Rule.** A dental practice that leases equipment to or from a referral source may use the equipment-rental safe harbor only if the lease meets all 6 standards: written and signed, covers all equipment, specifies any part-time schedule, lasts at least 1 year, sets aggregate rent in advance at fair market value without regard to referrals, and rents no more equipment than commercially reasonable.

**Exact text.**

> "The lease covers all of the equipment leased between the parties for the term of the lease and specifies the equipment covered by the lease." (42 CFR 1001.952(c)(2)).

> "The term of the lease is for not less than one year." (42 CFR 1001.952(c)(4)).

> "The aggregate equipment rental does not exceed that which is reasonably necessary to accomplish the commercially reasonable business purpose of the rental." (42 CFR 1001.952(c)(6)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- 6 standards, all required (42 CFR 1001.952(c)).
- Term: not less than 1 year (42 CFR 1001.952(c)(4)).

**Exceptions.**

- None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- List every shared or leased item (imaging units, CBCT, lasers) used with a referral partner; confirm a signed lease meets the 6 standards.
- Re-check at each renewal; owner dentist signs off.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source. Keep the signed agreement and fair-market-value support for the agreement term plus the office's general contract-retention period (office policy, not law).
