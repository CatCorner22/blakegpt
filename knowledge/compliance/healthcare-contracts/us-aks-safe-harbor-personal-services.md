# AKS safe harbor: personal services and management contracts

**Rule.** A dental practice that pays or is paid by an agent (management company, billing company, marketer, consultant) may use the personal-services safe harbor only if the written, signed agreement covers all services, lasts at least 1 year, sets the compensation methodology in advance at fair market value without regard to referrals, involves no unlawful activity, and buys no more services than commercially reasonable.

**Exact text.**

> "The term of the agreement is not less than 1 year." (42 CFR 1001.952(d)(1)(iii)).

> "The methodology for determining the compensation paid to the agent over the term of the agreement is set in advance, is consistent with fair market value in arm's-length transactions, and is not determined in a manner that takes into account the volume or value of any referrals or business otherwise generated between the parties" (42 CFR 1001.952(d)(1)(iv)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- 6 standards, all required (42 CFR 1001.952(d)(1)(i)-(vi)).
- Term: not less than 1 year (42 CFR 1001.952(d)(1)(iii)).
- Current text requires the compensation methodology, not the aggregate amount, to be set in advance (42 CFR 1001.952(d)(1)(iv)).

**Exceptions.**

- None stated in paragraph (d)(1); paragraph (d)(2) adds a separate outcomes-based payment safe harbor not summarized here.

**Office checklist.**

- For any management services or marketing agreement, confirm the fee formula is fixed in writing at signing and does not rise with TennCare or Medicare patient volume.
- A percentage-of-collections fee does not fit this safe harbor if it varies with Federal program business; flag it for counsel (see tn-63-5-121-dental-practice-ownership).
- Owner dentist signs the review.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source. Keep the signed agreement and fair-market-value support for the agreement term plus the office's general contract-retention period (office policy, not law).
