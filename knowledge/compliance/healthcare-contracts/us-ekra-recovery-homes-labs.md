# EKRA: no kickbacks for referrals to recovery homes, clinical treatment facilities, or laboratories, any payer

**Rule.** The practice and its staff must not knowingly and willfully solicit, receive, pay or offer remuneration for referrals to a recovery home, clinical treatment facility, or laboratory for services covered by any health care benefit program, including commercial dental and medical insurance. This includes dental pathology and other clinical laboratories.

**Exact text.**

> "whoever, with respect to services covered by a health care benefit program, in or affecting interstate or foreign commerce, knowingly and willfully— (1) solicits or receives any remuneration (including any kickback, bribe, or rebate) directly or indirectly, overtly or covertly, in cash or in kind, in return for referring a patient or patronage to a recovery home, clinical treatment facility, or laboratory" (18 U.S.C. 220(a)(1)).

> "shall be fined not more than $200,000, imprisoned not more than 10 years, or both, for each occurrence." (18 U.S.C. 220(a)).

> "the term \"laboratory\" has the meaning given the term in section 353 of the Public Health Service Act (42 U.S.C. 263a)" (18 U.S.C. 220(e)(4)).

> "This section shall not apply to conduct that is prohibited under section 1128B of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. 1320a–7b)." (18 U.S.C. 220(d)(1)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Fine: not more than $200,000 per occurrence (18 U.S.C. 220(a)).
- Prison: not more than 10 years (same).
- "Health care benefit program" has the meaning in 18 U.S.C. 24(b) (18 U.S.C. 220(e)(3)); that definition covers private plans (18 U.S.C. 24(b) not fetched).

**Exceptions.**

- Statutory exceptions in 18 U.S.C. 220(b), including properly disclosed discounts and certain employee pay.
- Conduct prohibited by the AKS is governed by the AKS instead (18 U.S.C. 220(d)(1)).

**Office checklist.**

- Confirm no lab (oral pathology, sleep-test, or COVID/strep testing lab) pays the practice or staff per specimen or per referral.
- Review lab agreements once a year; owner dentist signs off.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.

**Not verified.** 18 U.S.C. 24(b) (definition of health care benefit program) was not fetched; the all-payer reach rests on 18 U.S.C. 220(a) applying to "services covered by a health care benefit program" without a Federal-program limit. A dental lab that fabricates prosthetics is not necessarily a "laboratory" under 42 U.S.C. 263a; 42 U.S.C. 263a was not fetched.
