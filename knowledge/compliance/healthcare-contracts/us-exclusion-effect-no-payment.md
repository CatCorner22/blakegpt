# OIG exclusion: no Federal program payment for items or services of an excluded person

**Rule.** The practice must not bill Medicare, TennCare/Medicaid or another Federal health care program for items or services furnished, ordered or prescribed by an excluded individual or entity. OIG excludes individuals and entities under 42 U.S.C. 1320a-7.

**Exact text.**

> "Unless and until an individual or entity is reinstated into the Medicare, Medicaid, and other Federal health care programs in accordance with subpart F of this part, no payment will be made by Medicare, including Medicare Advantage and Prescription Drug Plans, Medicaid, or any other" (42 CFR 1001.1901(b)(1)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Payment bar lasts until reinstatement under 42 CFR part 1001, subpart F (42 CFR 1001.1901(b)(1)).

**Exceptions.**

- None stated in the quoted text; subpart F reinstatement ends the bar.

**Office checklist.**

- Screen each new hire and contractor against the OIG LEIE before the start date (see us-oig-exclusion-screening-guidance).
- Stop billing for any person found excluded and call practice counsel the same day.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
