# Daily sterilization method and instruments to sterilize between patients

**Rule.** The dentist must ensure that the office uses at least 1 listed sterilization method daily. Staff must sterilize the listed reusable instruments between patients, after removing debris.

**Exact text.**

> "The dentist shall ensure that at least one (1) of the following sterilization procedures is utilized daily for instruments and equipment" (0460-01-.11(1)).

> "U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) approved disinfectant shall be used in dilution amounts and specified time periods." (0460-01-.11(1)(d)).

> "The following instruments, unless disposable, shall be sterilized between patients, after removal of debris" (0460-01-.11(2)).

> "High speed handpieces and surgical handpieces" (0460-01-.11(2)(b)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- At least 1 method used daily: steam autoclave, dry heat, chemical vapor, or disinfectant/chemical sterilant (0460-01-.11(1)(a)-(d)), or any procedure in "MMWR, Vol 41, No. RR8" "or successor publications" (0460-01-.11(1)(e)).
- Between every patient: the 12 instrument groups in 0460-01-.11(2)(a)-(l), including handpieces, burs, air-water syringe tips, HVE tips, scaler tips, metal impression trays and heat-tolerant intra-oral radiographic equipment.

**Exceptions.**

- Disposable instruments ("unless disposable", 0460-01-.11(2)).
- Intra-oral radiographic equipment that cannot withstand heat sterilization is not on the list (0460-01-.11(2)(l)).

**Office checklist.**

- Post the 12-item list from 0460-01-.11(2) in the sterilization area.
- Confirm daily that the sterilizer ran and that handpieces went through it between patients; the lead dentist signs off monthly.
- Keep disinfectant/sterilant labels showing dilution and contact time.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this paragraph. See tn-0460-01-11-spore-testing-and-logs for the 2-year log rule.
