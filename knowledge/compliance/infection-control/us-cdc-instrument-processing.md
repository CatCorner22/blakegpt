# Instrument processing workflow (CDC guidance)

**Rule.** The office may follow the CDC workflow: clean per manufacturer instructions, inspect, package with an indicator, sterilize, monitor, and store, with a one-way flow from dirty to clean. Trained staff must wear PPE when reprocessing contaminated items.

**Exact text.**

> "If the manufacturer does not provide such instructions, the device may not be suitable for multi-patient use." (2016 Summary, Sterilization item 2).

> "Assign responsibilities for reprocessing of dental equipment to DHCP with appropriate training." (2016 Summary, Sterilization item 3).

> "Items are thoroughly cleaned according to manufacturer instructions and visually inspected for residual contamination before sterilization" (2016 Summary, Appendix A, II.6.C).

> "The instrument processing area has a workflow pattern designed to ensure that devices and instruments clearly flow from high contamination areas to clean / sterile areas (i.e., there is clear separation of contaminated and clean workspaces)" (2016 Summary, Appendix A, II.6.O).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- None numeric.

**Exceptions.**

- None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- Keep manufacturer reprocessing instructions in the sterilization area.
- Mark dirty, clean and sterile zones on the counter.
- Utility gloves for manual cleaning; prefer the ultrasonic or washer.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
