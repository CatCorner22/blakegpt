# Report severe work injuries to TOSHA

**Rule.** The employer must report qualifying work-related fatalities, inpatient hospitalizations, amputations, and eye losses to TOSHA even if exempt from logs. The employer must make a later fatality report after a previously reported hospitalization under TOSHA's interpretation.

**Exact text.**
> "By telephone to the TOSHA toll-free central telephone number, 1-800-249-8510." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-03-.05(1)(a)3(ii)).

> "In the scenario above, TOSHA would require the second report." (TOSHA memorandum, July 29, 2024, concluding paragraph).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Fatality: report within 8 hours; qualifying event must occur within 30 days of incident (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-03-.05(1)(a)1, (b)6).
- Inpatient hospitalization of 1 or more employees, amputation, or eye loss: report within 24 hours; event must occur within 24 hours of incident (same rule (1)(a)2, (b)6).
- Delayed awareness: report within 8 hours for death or 24 hours for other qualifying events after the employer or agent learns of the event or work relationship (same rule (1)(b)7-8).
- Central telephone: 1-800-249-8510 (same rule (1)(a)3(ii)).

**Exceptions.**
- Observation or diagnostic testing alone is not reportable hospitalization. Public-road vehicle incidents outside construction zones and commercial/public transportation events have reporting exceptions; log duties may remain. Do not leave an area-office voicemail, fax, or email when closed: use the central number or specified reporting application (same rule (1)(b)1, 3-4, 9-10).

**Office checklist.**
- Practice workflow: Amanda: contact TOSHA promptly; collect establishment, incident place/time, event type, affected employees, contact details, and brief description. Owner dentist: confirm reporting and later fatality follow-up without waiting for log review.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source; practice workflow: retain report confirmation securely.
