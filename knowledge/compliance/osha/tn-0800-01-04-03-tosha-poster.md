# Post the TOSHA workplace notice

**Rule.** The employer must post and keep the TOSHA notice conspicuously at each establishment where employee notices are customarily posted. The employer must keep it unaltered, undefaced, and uncovered.

**Exact text.**
> "Each employer shall take steps to insure that such notices are not altered, defaced or covered by other material." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-04-.03(1)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- The agency statutory copy states a penalty up to $7,000 for each posting violation (T.C.A. 50-3-406).
- If the employer has copies of the Act, rules, and standards, make them available the same day requested or at the earliest mutually convenient time (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-04-.03(3)).

**Exceptions.**
- Dispersed employees use the reporting or operating location described in -.03(2).

**Office checklist.**
- Practice workflow: Amanda: display the TOSHA poster and check visibility when noticeboards change. Owner dentist: confirm each practice location has the notice.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
