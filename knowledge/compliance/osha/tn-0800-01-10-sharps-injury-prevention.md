# Tennessee safer-sharps evaluation and incident documentation

**Rule.** The employer must document exposure-incident device type and brand in the exposure control plan and document engineered devices evaluated and currently used. The employer must evaluate and use available engineered sharps devices when more effective in preventing exposure incidents.

**Exact text.**
> "The documentation shall occur within six (6) days of the exposure incident." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-10-.04(1)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Document device type and brand within 6 days of the exposure incident (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-10-.04(1)).

**Exceptions.**
- Document a directly involved licensed professional's determination that the control jeopardizes patient safety or procedure success, or objective evidence that the device is not more effective. The pre-filled syringe exception is expressly time-limited, not a permanent exception (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-10-.05(2)(a)-(c)).

**Office checklist.**
- Practice workflow: Amanda: open the plan entry after every exposure incident and maintain device-evaluation records. Owner dentist: sign off on device choice and any documented clinical exception.

**Records to keep.** Keep incident device documentation and evaluated/current devices in the exposure control plan; no retention duration stated (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-10-.04).
