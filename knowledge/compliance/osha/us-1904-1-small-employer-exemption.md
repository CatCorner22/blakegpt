# Small-employer partial log exemption

**Rule.** The employer may omit routine OSHA injury and illness records if the entire company stayed within the small-employer limit throughout the prior calendar year, unless notified in writing otherwise. The employer must still report qualifying severe incidents.

**Exact text.**
> "The partial exemption for size is based on the number of employees in the entire company." (29 CFR 1904.1(b)(1)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Peak company employment must be 10 or fewer throughout the last calendar year; exceeding 10 removes this size exemption but an industry exemption may remain (29 CFR 1904.1(a)(1)-(2), (b)(2)).

**Exceptions.**
- OSHA or BLS written recordkeeping requests under the provisions referenced in the source override the exemption. Severe reporting remains required (29 CFR 1904.1(a)(1)).
