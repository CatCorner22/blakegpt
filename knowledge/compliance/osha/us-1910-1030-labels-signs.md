# Bloodborne pathogen labels and signs

**Rule.** The employer must label regulated-waste containers and containers or refrigerators holding blood or other potentially infectious materials as specified. The employer may substitute red bags or red containers for labels.

**Exact text.**
> "Red bags or red containers may be substituted for labels." (29 CFR 1910.1030(g)(1)(i)(E)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Attach warning labels as close as feasible to the container using a method that prevents loss or unintended removal (29 CFR 1910.1030(g)(1)(i)(D)).

**Exceptions.**
- Released clinical blood products, individual containers inside labeled containers, and decontaminated regulated waste have specified exemptions. Entrance signs in this paragraph apply to HIV/HBV research laboratories and production facilities, not automatically every dental treatment room (29 CFR 1910.1030(g)(1)(i)(F)-(I), (ii)(A)).

**Office checklist.**
- Practice workflow: Amanda: inspect color coding and fluorescent orange/orange-red contrasting labels when stocking or moving containers. Owner dentist: approve handling of equipment that cannot be fully decontaminated and identify contaminated portions.
