# Hazardous chemical labels

**Rule.** The employer must ensure hazardous-chemical workplace containers carry compliant labels or other permitted warnings. The employer must preserve incoming labels unless immediately replaced with required information and keep workplace warnings legible and in English.

**Exact text.**
> "The employer shall not remove or deface existing labels on incoming containers of hazardous chemicals, unless the container is immediately marked with the required information." (29 CFR 1910.1200(f)(9)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Warnings must remain available throughout each work shift (29 CFR 1910.1200(f)(10)).
- Revise labels within 6 months of learning significant new hazard information; the source states specific released-for-shipment and resumed-production rules (29 CFR 1910.1200(f)(11)).
- Federal update effective July 19, 2024; employer substance-related updates as necessary by November 20, 2026 and mixture-related updates as necessary by May 19, 2028 (29 CFR 1910.1200(j)(1), (2)(ii), (3)(ii)).

**Exceptions.**
- Immediate-use portable containers transferred and used only by the transferring employee are exempt; immediate use includes the same work shift. Stationary process containers may use accessible alternative written warnings. Pharmacy-dispensed drugs for direct patient administration have a labeling exemption (29 CFR 1910.1200(c), (f)(7)-(8)). Small-container supplier provisions are conditional, not a blanket workplace-container exemption (29 CFR 1910.1200(f)(12)).
- Federal transition permits the current text, the text revised as of July 1, 2023, or both until the applicable compliance dates (29 CFR 1910.1200(j)(4)). General coverage exclusions also appear in (b); do not assume all dental products are excluded.

**Office checklist.**
- Practice workflow: Amanda: check incoming and secondary-container labels when chemicals enter use. Owner dentist: approve the workplace warning system and any specific exemption.

**Records to keep.** No label retention rule in this paragraph.
