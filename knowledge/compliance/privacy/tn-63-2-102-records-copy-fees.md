# Tennessee records copy fee limits

**Rule.** A provider or records-release vendor must stay within Tennessee's copy-fee limits and any controlling lower HIPAA cost-based limit. A provider must apply HIPAA/HITECH fee rules to a patient's own electronic-record request, not the separate other-requester schedule.

**Exact text.**
> "Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as superseding any provision of law that establishes specific costs for the reproduction, copying or mailing of records." (Tenn. Code Ann. § 63-2-102(d)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Paper non-workers'-compensation records: no more than $25 for 5 pages or fewer; $0.50 per page after 5; actual mailing; hard-copy radiology no more than $20 per printed film; requested certification/notary flat fee $20 (a)(1)). These are ceilings, not automatic patient-access charges.
- A patient's own electronic copies: allowed HIPAA/HITECH costs only—copy labor, permitted supplies/media, and requested postage (a)(2)(A)).
- Other electronic requesters: up to $25 for 10 pages or fewer, then $0.25 per page up to $90; actual mailing/taxes; radiology up to $25 per CD/DVD/USB request or $15 per emailed/portal-file request; requested certification/notary flat fee $20 (a)(2)(B)(i)–(v)).
- Other electronic requesters' paper/fax copies use (a)(1); electronic Social Security disability claim/denial-appeal copies carry a flat $20 fee (a)(2)(B)(vi)–(vii)).
- Provide electronic records on request unless not ordinarily kept electronically; vendors cannot exceed the limits (a)(3)–(4)).
- A requested custodian affidavit under (c) may cost up to $20, subject to other controlling fee rules; it is not a required add-on to every patient access request.
- Current HIPAA permits only reasonable cost-based copy labor, allowed supplies/media, requested postage, and agreed summary/explanation costs (164.524(c)(4)).
- Pending rule narrows supplies/postage, expressly bars inspection/internet-access fees, and adds posted fee schedules (revised 164.524(c)(4), new 164.525). Scheduled publication 2026-11-19; effective 60 days later (derived 2027-01-18), compliance 240 days later (derived 2027-07-17) (DATES p. 1).
- Source: Justia 2025 unofficial mirror; no amendment was found in the compilation search reported in the supplied checkpoint.

**Exceptions.**
- Workers' compensation fees refer to 50-6-204, not this schedule; that fee text was not fetched (a)(5), (f).
- No copying/notarizing fee for specified Department of Health complaint/inspection/survey requests (a)(6)).
- Department of Human Services requests retain the prior 2010 fee treatment under (b); the historical schedule was not fetched.
- The statute permits prepayment of lawful copying costs and requires release without delay after payment (e); do not confuse copy costs with unpaid dental treatment bills.

**Office checklist.**
- For each request, Amanda classifies patient versus other requester, format, and any agency/special category before calculating fees.
- The privacy official approves the fee method against HIPAA and state limits; Amanda itemizes only the permitted cost categories for patient HIPAA access fees (164.524(c)(4)).

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source. Keep HIPAA fee policies under 164.530(j) for 6 years from creation or last effective date, whichever is later.
