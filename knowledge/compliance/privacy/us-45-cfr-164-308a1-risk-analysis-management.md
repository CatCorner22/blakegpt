# Security risk analysis and management

**Rule.** A covered practice or business associate must assess risks and weaknesses affecting all electronic PHI and implement reasonable and appropriate risk controls. The entity must also apply security sanctions and regularly review system activity.

**Exact text.**
> "Conduct an accurate and thorough assessment of the potential risks and vulnerabilities to the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of electronic protected health information held by the covered entity or business associate." (45 CFR 164.308(a)(1)(ii)(A)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Risk analysis, risk management, sanctions, and system-activity review are required specifications (164.308(a)(1)(ii)(A)–(D)).
- Review and modify controls as needed; this source sets no fixed annual risk-analysis interval (164.306(e)).

**Exceptions.** 
- Size, complexity, capability, cost, and risk affect the controls selected, not whether the standard applies (164.306(b)).

**Office checklist.**
- When systems, vendors, or operations change, Amanda updates the electronic-PHI inventory and routes risks to the security official.
- The security official approves the assessment, corrective owners, and review schedule; Amanda tracks open corrections.

**Records to keep.** Keep security policies and required documented actions/assessments for 6 years from creation or last effective date, whichever is later; make them available to implementers and update as needed (45 CFR 164.316(b)(1)–(2)).
