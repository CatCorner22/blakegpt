# Security documentation retention

**Rule.** A covered practice or business associate must maintain written or electronic security policies and required records of documented actions, activities, and assessments. The entity must make these records available to implementers and update them for relevant environmental or operational changes.

**Exact text.**
> "for 6 years from the date of its creation or the date when it last was in effect, whichever is later." (45 CFR 164.316(b)(2)(i)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Keep required documentation for 6 years from the later trigger (164.316(b)(2)(i)).
- Review periodically and update as needed; no fixed annual interval appears here (b)(2)(iii)).

**Exceptions.** None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**
- When a policy changes, Amanda archives its prior version and last effective date.
- Before disposal, the security official approves the retention calculation and checks any separate preservation need.

**Records to keep.** Keep security policies and required documented actions/assessments for 6 years from creation or last effective date, whichever is later; make them available to implementers and update as needed (45 CFR 164.316(b)(1)–(2)).
