# Business associate privacy agreements

**Rule.** A covered practice must obtain a compliant written business associate agreement or permitted arrangement for a BA relationship. The agreement must limit uses, require safeguards and reporting, support patient rights and HHS review, flow restrictions to subcontractors, and address termination and PHI return/destruction.

**Exact text.**
> "Make available protected health information in accordance with § 164.524" (45 CFR 164.504(e)(2)(ii)(E)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Obtain assurances before permitting the covered BA handling (164.502(e)).
- Current contract terms include access, amendments, accounting, reporting unauthorized uses/breaches, and Security Rule compliance for electronic PHI (164.504(e)(2)).
- Pending final rule: publication scheduled 2026-11-19; effective 60 days later (derived 2027-01-18), compliance 240 days later (derived 2027-07-17), not quoted calendar dates (DATES p. 1).
- Qualifying prepublication contracts meeting prior rules and not renewed/modified during the effective-to-compliance window may remain deemed compliant until earlier qualifying renewal/modification on/after compliance or 2028-01-18 (revised 164.532(e)(1)–(2)).

**Exceptions.** 
- Return/destruction may be infeasible; extend protection and limit later uses to the reasons making it infeasible (164.504(e)(2)(ii)(J)).
- Known material violations require reasonable cure/end steps and termination if unsuccessful and feasible (e)(1)(ii)).
- The pending transition does not postpone access, amendment, accounting, or mitigation duties (164.532(e)(3)).

**Office checklist.**
- Before each BA receives PHI, Amanda obtains the signed agreement and routes it to the privacy official for approval.
- At renewal, modification, or known violation, Amanda routes terms and corrective steps to the privacy official; track the pending transition's eligibility and end date.

**Records to keep.** Keep required written agreements and related privacy documentation for 6 years from creation or last effective date, whichever is later (164.530(j)).
