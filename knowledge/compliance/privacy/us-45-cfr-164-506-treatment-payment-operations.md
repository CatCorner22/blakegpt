# Treatment payment and operations disclosures

**Rule.** A covered practice may use or disclose PHI for treatment, payment, and health care operations within 164.506. The practice must not use or disclose PHI without a permitted or required legal basis. The practice must provide access/accounting disclosures required for individuals and disclosures required by HHS for compliance review.

**Exact text.**
> "A covered entity may use or disclose protected health information for its own treatment, payment, or health care operations." (45 CFR 164.506(c)(1)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- No fixed response deadline for routine treatment/payment sharing appears in 164.506.
- For another covered entity's operations, both entities must have or have had a patient relationship, the information must concern that relationship, and the purpose must qualify under (c)(4).
- Final rule 2026-23429 is scheduled for publication 2026-11-19; effective 60 days after publication (derived 2027-01-18), compliance 240 days after publication (derived 2027-07-17). These calendar dates are calculated, not quoted (final rule DATES, p. 1).
- Pending (c)(6) expressly permits individual-level coordination/case-management disclosures to qualifying social-service or similar third parties (amendment 6).

**Exceptions.** 
- Uses requiring authorization under 164.508(a)(2)–(4), prohibited genetic underwriting, and other applicable limits remain (164.506(a)).
- A general treatment consent does not replace a required HIPAA authorization (164.506(b)(2)).

**Office checklist.**
- Before each new disclosure workflow, Amanda identifies its legal basis and minimum-necessary limits; the privacy official approves.
- Send unusual requests to the privacy official before release.

**Records to keep.** Keep required written policies and documentation for 6 years from creation or last effective date, whichever is later (45 CFR 164.530(j)(1)–(2)).
