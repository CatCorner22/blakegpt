# Restrictions and private communications

**Rule.** A covered practice must accept restriction requests and must honor a qualifying fully paid self-pay restriction on health-plan payment or operations disclosures. The practice must accommodate reasonable requests for alternative communication methods or locations and must not demand the patient's reason.

**Exact text.**
> "A covered health care provider may not require an explanation from the individual as to the basis for the request as a condition of providing communications on a confidential basis." (45 CFR 164.522(b)(2)(iii)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- The mandatory health-plan restriction applies when the information concerns only an item/service paid in full by the patient or someone other than the plan, and disclosure is for payment/operations and not required by law (164.522(a)(1)(vi)).
- No fixed response deadline appears in this section.

**Exceptions.** 
- Other restrictions need not be accepted; accepted restrictions bind the practice subject to emergency treatment and listed disclosure exceptions (a)(1)(ii)–(v).
- The practice may require a written confidential-communication request, an alternative contact, and appropriate payment arrangements (b)(2).
- The practice cannot unilaterally terminate the mandatory paid-in-full restriction for the protected information (a)(2)(iii)).

**Office checklist.**
- At each self-pay request, Amanda checks full payment and blocks plan billing for the restricted service; the privacy official approves the flag.
- At each communication request, Amanda records the approved contact route and updates reminders before sending them.

**Records to keep.** Keep written restriction decisions and policy documentation for 6 years under 164.530(j). The fetched 164.522(a)(3) prints a cross-reference to 160.530(j); use the documentation rule in 164.530(j).
