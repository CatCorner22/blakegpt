# Section 1557 partial vacatur status

**Rule.** A covered practice must distinguish partial vacatur of the 2024 Section 1557 rule from repeal of all civil-rights duties. HHS's 2026 notice states that specified provisions are legally void to the extent they expand Title IX sex discrimination to gender-identity discrimination and that the other provisions remain in force.

**Exact text.**
> "The other provisions of the Section 1557 Rule remain in force." (91 FR 32887, Summary).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- The notice reports the court's final vacatur order dated 2025-10-22 and was published 2026-06-02, 91 FR 32887 (Summary/Background).
- The listed Part 92 provisions are 92.101(a)(2)(iv), 92.206(b)(1)–(4), 92.207(b)(3)–(5), 92.8(b)(1), 92.10(a)(1)(i), and 92.208, only to the stated gender-identity-expansion extent (Background, 91 FR 32888).
- HHS says OCR cannot and will not enforce the vacated provisions to that extent; scope applies to all covered entities (Scope, 91 FR 32888).
- The fetched Medicaid policy page also describes earlier stays/injunctions in Florida, Tennessee, and Texas, including broader sex-definition references in Texas; do not assume the final-vacatur notice resolves every other order.

**Exceptions.**
- The notice expressly preserves provisions not vacated and the specified provisions insofar as they concern matters other than the stated expansion (Scope).

**Office checklist.**
- At each civil-rights policy or notice revision, Amanda identifies the still-operative notice, language-access, and training requirements.
- The practice owner/coordinator obtains legal review for disputed sex-discrimination language rather than treating all of Part 92 as void.

**Records to keep.** The status notice states no retention period. Applicable training/grievance records retain the periods in 92.9(c) and 92.8(c)(2); see the duties card.
