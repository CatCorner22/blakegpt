# Working interviews: verify the license before any patient care

**Rule.** A dentist must not let an applicant treat patients during an employment interview until the dentist has visually inspected and verified the applicant's current, unrestricted Tennessee license or registration.

**Exact text.**

> "A dentist shall not conduct employment interviews with dentists, dental hygienists or dental assistants that include any patient care unless the dentist visually inspects and verifies the dentist's, dental hygienist's or dental assistant's current and unrestricted authorization to practice their profession in Tennessee." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.07(1)).

> "A licensee's failure to comply with the provisions of this rule shall constitute unprofessional conduct" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.07(2)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Verification before any patient care during the interview (0460-01-.07(1)).

**Exceptions.**

- None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- Before a working interview, print the Board license or registration verification and file it with the interview notes.
- Limit a practical (unregistered) assistant candidate to practical-level duties under direct supervision.
- The interviewing dentist signs the verification printout.

**Records to keep.**

No retention rule in this source.
