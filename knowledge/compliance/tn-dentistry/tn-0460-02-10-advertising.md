# Dental advertising: fees, content, specialty claims and ad records

**Rule.** An ad must not mislead, must not use patient information without consent, must name the licensee with address and phone, and must use a dentist title such as "D.D.S." or "D.M.D." Only a Board-certified specialist in a listed specialty may say "specialist" or "practice limited to". The office must honor advertised fees for the stated period, or 30 days if none is stated, and must keep a copy of every ad for 2 years after it last runs.

**Exact text.**

> "If no time period is stated in the advertisement of fees, the advertised fee shall be honored for thirty (30) days from the last date of publication or until the next scheduled publication whichever is later" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.10(3)(e)1.).

> "The communication of personal identifiable facts, data, or information about a patient without first obtaining patient consent." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.10(4)(h)).

> "Failure to include the corporation, partnership or individual licensee's name and address and telephone number in any advertisement." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.10(4)(p)).

> "After thirty (30) days, the use of the name of any licensee formerly practicing at or associated with any advertised location or on office signs or buildings." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.10(4)(r)).

> "A recording of every advertisement communicated by electronic media, and a copy of every advertisement communicated by print media, and a copy of any other form of advertisement shall be retained by the licensee for a period of two (2) years from the last date of broadcast or publication" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.10(6)(c)).

> ""the services are being performed or provided by a general dentist"" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.10(5)(b)2.).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Advertised fee with no stated period: honored 30 days from last publication or until the next scheduled publication, whichever is later (0460-02-.10(3)(e)1.).
- Former licensee's name: remove from ads and signs after 30 days (0460-02-.10(4)(r)).
- Ad copies kept 2 years from last broadcast or publication (0460-02-.10(6)(c)).

**Exceptions.**

- Retired or deceased former associate's name may stay if the status is disclosed (0460-02-.10(4)(r)).
- "Associate fellow", "fellow" or "diplomate" may be used with the required disclaimer (0460-02-.10(5)(c)).

**Office checklist.**

- Route every ad, website change and paid social post to the owner dentist for written approval before release.
- Save a dated copy of each ad, including web and social, in an ad archive; purge only after 2 years from last run.
- When a dentist leaves, remove the name from signs, web and ads within 30 days.
- Get written patient consent before any testimonial, photo or case story.

**Records to keep.**

Keep a recording or copy of every advertisement for 2 years from the last date of broadcast or publication (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.10(6)(c)). Keep the support for every factual claim (0460-02-.10(6)(d)).
