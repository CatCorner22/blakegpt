# Dental assistant levels of practice: practical and registered

**Rule.** Tennessee recognizes 2 levels of dental assistant: practical and registered. A practical dental assistant must work under the direct supervision of a licensed dentist and must not perform duties reserved to registered dental assistants or hygienists. A registered dental assistant may perform the added procedures for which the Board certified the assistant, under direct supervision.

**Exact text.**

> "It is the intent of the Board to authorize two distinct levels of practice for dental assistants in dental offices in Tennessee." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.01).

> "A practical dental assistant is an auxiliary employee of a licensed dentist(s) who is receiving practical chair side dental assisting training from a licensed dentist(s) or is a dental assistant student in an educational institution accredited by the Commission on Dental Accreditation of the American Dental Association." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.01(1)(a)).

> "A practical dental assistant must be under the direct supervision of a licensed dentist." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.01(1)(b)1.).

> "A registered dental assistant may perform those additional procedures for which they have received Board certification as provided by Rule 0460-04-.08 under the direct supervision of a dentist." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.01(2)(b)).

> "Such registered and/or practical dental assistants may practice as authorized in this section or § 63-5-108 only in the office of and under the direct supervision of a licensed and registered dentist except in authorized public health programs." (T.C.A. § 63-5-115(a) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- 2 levels of practice: practical dental assistant and registered dental assistant (0460-04-.01).
- Both levels: direct supervision at all times (0460-04-.01(1)(b)1., (2)(b); T.C.A. § 63-5-115(a)).

**Exceptions.**

- T.C.A. § 63-5-115(a) allows assistants to practice outside the dentist's office only in "authorized public health programs."
- T.C.A. § 63-5-115(c) permits registered dental assistants to "participate unsupervised in educational functions involving organized groups or health care institutions regarding preventive oral health care."

**Office checklist.**

- At hire, record each assistant's level (practical or registered) and, for registered assistants, the registration number and each Board certification.
- Monthly, confirm a dentist is on the premises whenever an assistant treats patients.
- The owner dentist signs off on the staff level roster each quarter.

**Records to keep.**
