# Coronal polishing certification for registered dental assistants

**Rule.** A dental assistant must be registered and Board-certified before coronal polishing. The employer dentist must be on the premises the whole time and must examine each patient immediately before and immediately after the polishing.

**Exact text.**

> "Coronal Polishing shall mean the polishing of the enamel and restorations on the clinical crown of human teeth by utilizing a combination of a polishing agent and a slow speed handpiece, a prophy angle, a rubber cup, or any home care cleaning device." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.04(1)).

> "Within thirty (30) days of an applicant's completion of the program, the program director/instructor must submit a letter to the Board administrator" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.04(2)(c)).

> "The passing grade for each examination is set at seventy-five percent (75%)." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.04(4)).

> "the employer dentist must be physically on the office premises at all times during the polishing" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.04(5)(a)).

> "A dental assistant may not perform coronal polishing for patients who have not been examined immediately prior to being assigned for polishing." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.04(5)(b)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Program director letter within 30 days of program completion (ADA-accredited route) (0460-04-.04(2)(c)).
- Passing grade 75% on written and on clinical exams; 2 retakes allowed before repeating the course (0460-04-.04(4)).
- Dentist exam immediately before and immediately after each polishing (0460-04-.04(5)(a)1.-2.).
- Certification lapses when the registration expires or is retired (0460-04-.04(3)).

**Exceptions.**

- Out-of-state course found equivalent by the Board consultant (0460-04-.04(2)(b)).

**Office checklist.**

- Keep a copy of each assistant's coronal polishing certification.
- Schedule polishing only after the dentist exam; record the pre- and post-exam in the chart.
- Supervising dentist signs off on the polishing roster each quarter.
