# Nitrous oxide monitoring certification for registered dental assistants

**Rule.** A dental assistant must not administer nitrous oxide. A registered assistant with Board nitrous monitoring certification may monitor 1 patient at a time under direct supervision, must stay with the patient at all times, and may adjust or stop the gas only on the dentist's direction or in patient distress.

**Exact text.**

> "Dental assistants may not administer nitrous oxide to patients but may monitor nitrous oxide sedation (as defined in rule 0460-02-.07) upon becoming certified" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.05).

> "This assistant shall not monitor more than one (1) patient at a time and shall physically remain with the patient at all times." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.05(5)).

> "This assistant is only permitted to adjust the dosage or terminate the nitrous oxide at the specific direction and under the protocol of the supervising dentist or in cases of patient distress." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.05(6)).

> "shall prominently display their current registration certificate, which is received upon registration and renewal, at their place of employment" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.05(7)).

> "The administering or supervising dentist must be on the premises at all times that nitrous oxide is in use." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.07(4)(a)).

> "All equipment for the administration of nitrous oxide must be designed specifically to guarantee that an oxygen concentration of no less than thirty percent (30%) can be administered to the patient." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.07(4)(d)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- 1 patient at a time per monitoring assistant (0460-04-.05(5)).
- Oxygen capability of no less than 30% (0460-02-.07(4)(d)).
- Scavenger system required on all nitrous equipment (0460-02-.07(4)(e)).
- Certification lapses with the registration (0460-04-.05(8)).

**Exceptions.**

- None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- Post each certified assistant's current registration certificate at the office.
- Keep a monitoring log per nitrous case: patient chart number, monitoring assistant, dentist on premises.
- Each year, check that every nitrous unit has a scavenger and 30% oxygen capability; the dentist signs.
