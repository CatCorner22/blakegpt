# Sealant application certification for registered dental assistants

**Rule.** Only a registered dental assistant with Board sealant certification may apply sealants, under direct supervision. The dentist must examine the patient immediately before and after the sealant application.

**Exact text.**

> "Sealant application shall mean the application of an organic polymer to the enamel surfaces of teeth." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.09(1)).

> "The dentist must examine the patient immediately before and after sealant application to determine the need for, and evaluate the results of, sealant application." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.09(3)(b)).

> "If the registration expires or is retired, the certification is also considered expired or retired, and the dental assistant may not apply sealants until the registration is reinstated or reactivated." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.09(4)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Dentist exam immediately before and after (0460-04-.09(3)(b)).
- Initial approval letter; certification ratified at the next Board meeting (0460-04-.09(2)(d)).

**Exceptions.**

- Out-of-state equivalent course (0460-04-.09(2)(c)).

**Office checklist.**

- Keep a copy of each sealant certification.
- Chart the dentist pre- and post-exam for each sealant visit.
