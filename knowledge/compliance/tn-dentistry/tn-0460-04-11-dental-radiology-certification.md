# Dental radiology certification for registered dental assistants

**Rule.** A dental assistant must be registered and hold Board radiology certification before exposing dental radiographs, and may expose them only under direct supervision. An unregistered or uncertified assistant may process radiographs but must not expose them.

**Exact text.**

> "Registered dental assistants with this certification may expose dental radiographs under the direct supervision of a licensed dentist." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.11).

> "Once eligible for certification, the registered dental assistant shall not expose dental radiographs until certification has been issued by the Board." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.11(2)).

> "shall prominently display their current registration certification, which is received upon registration and renewal, at their place of employment" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.11(4)).

> "The exposure of radiographs without certification as provided by Rule 0460-04-.11 and Rule 0460-04-.08 (2)." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.08(4)(n)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Certification lapses with the registration (0460-04-.11(5)).

**Exceptions.**

- Assistants registered before the rule's original effective date received certification without the course (0460-04-.11(1)(a)).
- DANB radiology exam passage or current DANB CDA certification, sent directly from DANB (0460-04-.11(1)(c)).
- Out-of-state equivalent course (0460-04-.11(1)(b)).

**Office checklist.**

- Post each certified assistant's current registration certificate.
- Lock imaging software exposure functions to certified staff if the system allows (office practice).
- Quarterly, compare who exposed images against the certification roster; the dentist signs.
