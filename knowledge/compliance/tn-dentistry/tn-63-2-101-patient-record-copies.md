# Patient requests for record copies: 10 working days and fee caps

**Rule.** The office must give a patient or authorized representative a copy of the record within 10 working days of a written request. A summary does not replace the full record. Copy fees must stay within T.C.A. § 63-2-102, and a patient's request for the patient's own electronic records follows HIPAA fee limits.

**Exact text.**

> "a health care provider shall provide to a patient or a patient's authorized representative a copy of such patient's medical records within ten (10) working days of receipt of a written request" (T.C.A. § 63-2-101(a)(1) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

> "the provision of a summary does not satisfy the patient's or representative's right to receive, or serve as a substitute for, a full medical record" (T.C.A. § 63-2-101(a)(1) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

> "The name and address and other identifying information shall not be sold for any purpose." (T.C.A. § 63-2-101(b)(2) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

> "Twenty-five dollars ($25.00) for paper copies of medical records five (5) pages or less in length;" (T.C.A. § 63-2-102(a)(1)(A) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

> "Fifty cents (50¢) per page for each page copied after the first five (5) pages;" (T.C.A. § 63-2-102(a)(1)(B) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Deadline: 10 working days from the written request (T.C.A. § 63-2-101(a)(1)).
- Paper: up to $25.00 for 5 pages or fewer, then $0.50 per page, plus actual mailing (T.C.A. § 63-2-102(a)(1)(A)-(C)).
- Printed radiology film: up to $20.00 each; certification or notary: flat $20.00 (T.C.A. § 63-2-102(a)(1)(D)-(E)).
- Electronic, non-patient requesters: up to $25.00 for 10 pages or fewer, then $0.25 per page up to $90.00; images up to $25.00 on media or $15.00 by email or portal (T.C.A. § 63-2-102(a)(2)(B)).
- Patient's own electronic copy: HIPAA cost-based limits (T.C.A. § 63-2-102(a)(2)(A)).
- No fee when the Department of Health requests records (T.C.A. § 63-2-102(a)(6)).

**Exceptions.**

- Workers' compensation records: fees per T.C.A. § 50-6-204 (T.C.A. § 63-2-102(a)(5)).
- Payment may be required before release (T.C.A. § 63-2-102(e)).

**Office checklist.**

- Date-stamp every written records request; send copies within 10 working days.
- Use a fee sheet that matches T.C.A. § 63-2-102; give electronic copies when asked and the records are electronic.
- The office manager signs each release log entry.

**Records to keep.**

No retention rule in this source; see Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.12(4)(g) for record retention.
