# Who may own a dental practice in Tennessee

**Rule.** Only a dentist licensed in Tennessee may own an active dental practice, and a dentist must not practice as an employee of an entity not primarily engaged in dentistry. Exceptions cover employer-provided employee dental services, state agencies, nonprofits, hospitals, charitable clinics, and an estate that may employ dentists for up to 2 years after an owner dies or becomes disabled. A management services agreement must not interfere with the dentist's independent judgment.

**Exact text.**

> "For any licensed dentist to practice dentistry as an employee of any person or other entity not engaged primarily in the practice of dentistry; or" (T.C.A. § 63-5-121(a)(1) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

> "For an owner of an active dental practice to be other than a dentist duly licensed to practice in this state." (T.C.A. § 63-5-121(a)(2) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

> "the estate or agent of such dentist may employ another dentist or dentists for a period of not more than two (2) years to provide services to patients until the practice can be sold or otherwise disposed of or closed" (T.C.A. § 63-5-121(b) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

> "Is the operator of a place where dental operations or dental services are performed" (T.C.A. § 63-5-108(b)(15) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

> "Entering into any agreement or arrangement for management services that: 1. interferes with a dentist's exercise of his/her independent professional judgment;" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.12(27)(c)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Estate or agent may employ dentists for not more than 2 years after death or substantial disability (T.C.A. § 63-5-121(b)).
- Charitable clinic: 501(c)(3), Tennessee clinical facilities, mission to serve low-income, uninsured or underserved people, free or discounted services, volunteers, not licensed under § 68-11-202(a)(1) (T.C.A. § 63-5-121(c)(2)).

**Exceptions.**

- Employer-provided employee dental services; state agencies or subdivisions; nonprofit organizations; hospitals (T.C.A. § 63-5-121(a)).
- Charitable clinic with a written contract that preserves independent judgment (T.C.A. § 63-5-121(c)(1)).

**Office checklist.**

- Keep the ownership chart on file; confirm each owner's Tennessee license each year.
- Have counsel review any management services or DSO agreement against 0460-01-.12(27).
- If an owner dentist dies or becomes disabled, calendar the 2-year deadline at once.
