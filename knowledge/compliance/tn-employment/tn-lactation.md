# Tennessee lactation breaks and space

**Rule.** A covered employer must provide reasonable daily unpaid break time for an employee who needs to express milk for an infant, subject to the state disruption exception. The employer must make reasonable efforts to provide a private place near the work area other than a toilet stall.

**Exact text.**

> “one (1) or more employees” (T.C.A. § 50-1-305(a) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “reasonable unpaid break time each day” (T.C.A. § 50-1-305(b) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “other than a toilet stall” (T.C.A. § 50-1-305(c) — unofficial Justia mirror)

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Coverage starts at 1 employee. Run the pumping time with an existing break when possible (T.C.A. § 50-1-305(a)–(b)).

**Exceptions.**

- The state break rule has an undue-disruption exception. The state space rule protects an employer that made reasonable efforts. Federal law can impose stronger duties (T.C.A. § 50-1-305(b)–(c)).

**Office checklist.**

- When a request arrives, arrange private space and patient coverage. Have the owner check the federal room and break rules before approving an exception.
- At each schedule change, confirm that the space and coverage remain usable.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
