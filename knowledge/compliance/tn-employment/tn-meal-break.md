# Meal or rest break for consecutive work

**Rule.** An employer must give each employee scheduled to work 6 consecutive hours a 30-minute unpaid meal or rest break, subject to the statutory exceptions. An employer must schedule the break after hour 1 of scheduled work.

**Exact text.**

> “a thirty-minute unpaid meal break if scheduled to work six (6) hours consecutively” (T.C.A. § 50-2-103(h)(1)(A) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “shall not be scheduled during or before the first hour of scheduled work activity” (T.C.A. § 50-2-103(h)(1)(A) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “a rest break or meal period” (T.C.A. § 50-2-103(h)(1)(B) — unofficial Justia mirror)

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- A schedule of 6 consecutive hours triggers the 30-minute break. The break cannot occur before or during hour 1 (T.C.A. § 50-2-103(h)(1)(A)).

**Exceptions.**

- A workplace whose business naturally provides ample opportunity for an appropriate break is excepted (T.C.A. § 50-2-103(h)(1)(A)).
- The written, knowing and voluntary waiver is for consenting employees principally serving food or beverages to customers who receive and report tips (subsection (h)(2)). It does not fit regular dental-office duties.

**Office checklist.**

- Block breaks on each qualifying schedule and assign coverage for patients, phones, and sterilization.
- Review interrupted or missed breaks each payroll. Have the payroll lead confirm that all working time was paid.
