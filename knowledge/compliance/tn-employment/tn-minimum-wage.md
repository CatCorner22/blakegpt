# Minimum wage in Tennessee

**Rule.** A Tennessee dental practice must pay at least the federal minimum wage to workers covered by the federal wage rule, subject to applicable federal exceptions.

**Exact text.**

> “No state minimum wage law.” (U.S. Department of Labor, State Minimum Wage Laws, Tennessee (agency guidance))

> “$7.25 an hour” (29 U.S.C. § 206(a)(1)(C) — Cornell LII mirror)

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- The U.S. Department of Labor’s state wage table confirms that Tennessee has no separate state minimum wage (agency guidance).
- Federal general minimum is $7.25 per hour (29 U.S.C. § 206(a)(1)(C)).
- For ordinary commercial enterprise coverage, the annual gross volume threshold is $500,000, with the commerce element also required. Individual coverage can apply to employees engaged in commerce even if the office does not meet the enterprise threshold (29 U.S.C. §§ 203(s)(1)(A), 206(a)).

**Exceptions.**

- Coverage and exemptions depend on the actual work and employer, including individual coverage. Check separately before using a tip credit.

**Office checklist.**

- Check coverage when the practice opens or changes ownership or operations. Have the owner approve wage classifications.
- At each payroll, have the payroll lead verify rates and flag any wage below the applicable floor.
