# Tennessee adoption, pregnancy, childbirth, and nursing leave

**Rule.** A covered employer must allow an eligible full-time employee up to 4 months of leave for adoption, pregnancy, childbirth, or nursing an infant and must restore the employee as the statute requires. The employer may decide whether to pay for the leave.

**Exact text.**

> “at least twelve (12) consecutive months as full-time employees” (T.C.A. § 4-21-408(a) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “one hundred (100) full-time employees on a permanent basis at the job site or location” (T.C.A. § 4-21-408(d)(2) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “restored to their previous or similar positions” (T.C.A. § 4-21-408(b)(1) — unofficial Justia mirror)

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Coverage starts at 100 permanent full-time employees at the job site or location. The employee needs 12 consecutive full-time months with the same employer (T.C.A. § 4-21-408(a), (d)(2)).
- Leave lasts up to 4 months. Adoption leave starts at custody. For restoration, the employee normally gives at least 3 months' advance notice of departure, leave length, and intent to return full-time (subsections (a), (b)(1)).
- The statute must appear in an employer handbook published after May 27, 2005, when the section applies (subsection (e)).

**Exceptions.**

- Medical emergency or late adoption notice can excuse missing advance notice (T.C.A. § 4-21-408(b)(2)–(3)).
- Restoration has exceptions when the employer cannot fill a unique job temporarily after reasonable efforts or finds that the employee worked elsewhere or actively pursued other employment while on leave. Notify the employee if either exception applies (subsection (c)(2)–(4)).
- Benefits for which the employee was eligible at the start of leave remain protected, with a limited exception for funding benefits during leave. A more generous policy or agreement can apply (subsections (c)(1), (d)(1)).

**Office checklist.**

- At each request, count permanent full-time staff at the location and review state and federal coverage. Have the owner approve the response and any return arrangements.
