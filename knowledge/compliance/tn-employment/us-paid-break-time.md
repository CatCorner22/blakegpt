# Pay for short breaks and working meals

**Rule.** A covered employer must count short rest periods as work time. A covered employer may treat a meal as unpaid when the employee is completely relieved from duty for the meal.

**Exact text.**

> “must be counted as hours worked” (29 CFR 785.18)

> “The employee must be completely relieved from duty for the purposes of eating regular meals.” (29 CFR 785.19(a))

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Short rest periods usually run from 5 to about 20 minutes (29 CFR 785.18).
- Ordinarily 30 minutes or more suffices for a bona fide meal period (29 CFR 785.19(a)).

**Exceptions.**

- A shorter meal period can qualify under special conditions (29 CFR 785.19(a)).
- An employee does not have to leave the premises if otherwise completely free from duties (29 CFR 785.19(b)).

**Office checklist.**

- Check each pay period for automatic meal deductions during front-desk coverage, charting, or patient care. Have the payroll lead approve corrections.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
