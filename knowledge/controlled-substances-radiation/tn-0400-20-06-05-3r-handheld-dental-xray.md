# Hand-held intraoral x-ray units: dosimetry, security and training

**Rule.** The office must provide whole-body and both-hand extremity dosimeters to every operator of a hand-held unit, secure the device against unauthorized use, keep bystanders at least 6 feet away, and keep signed training records.

**Exact text.**

> "The registrant shall provide and require individual personnel monitoring of external occupational dose via a whole body dosimeter and extremity dosimeters for both the right and left hands to all persons who may operate the hand-held device." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-06-.05(3)(r)1., Cornell LII mirror).

> "The registrant may be relieved of the requirement of this part after one year of personnel monitoring" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-06-.05(3)(r)1., Cornell LII mirror).

> "The operator prevents bystanders within a radius of at least six feet from the patient being examined with the hand-held device; and" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-06-.05(3)(r)3.(i), Cornell LII mirror).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Bystander radius: at least 6 feet.
- Dosimetry may be relieved after 1 year of monitoring results under (3)(r)1.
- Unauthorized use or removal: report immediately ((3)(r)2.).

**Exceptions.**

- Relief from dosimetry after 1 year if results meet the condition in (3)(r)1.

**Office checklist.**

- Lock the device when not in use; train each operator before first use and record date, names, signatures and topics.

**Records to keep.**

Initial and recurring training records with date, printed names and signatures, trainer signature and topics (0400-20-06-.05(3)(r)5.).
