# Register dental x-ray machines with the Division of Radiological Health

**Rule.** The office must register each x-ray machine within 10 days after acquiring it, pay the annual fee, and file a new Form RHS 8-4 within 10 days after a change of address, ownership, possessor or location. The office must notify the Division in writing within 60 days after permanently discontinuing or transferring all machines.

**Exact text.**

> "shall register such sources within 10 days after acquisition of such machine." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-10-.24(1), Cornell LII mirror).

> "An annual registration fee will be required each year as long as the radiation machine or service is subject to registration." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-10-.24(2), Cornell LII mirror).

> "the completion of a new Form RHS 8-4 shall be required within 10 days of the change." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-10-.24(5), Cornell LII mirror).

> "shall notify the Division in writing within 60 days of such action." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-10-.24(6), Cornell LII mirror).

> "shall register with the radiological health service within ten (10) days of such receipt" (T.C.A. § 68-202-208(a), Justia unofficial mirror).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Registration: 10 days after acquisition.
- Change of information: 10 days.
- Discontinue or transfer all machines: 60 days.
- Fee: annual. Dental machines are listed in Class I (0400-20-10-.24(3)(a)).
- The FDA 510(k) number must be provided before registration is processed (0400-20-10-.24(1)).

**Exceptions.**

- Machines exempted in Rule 0400-20-10-.07 (not reviewed).

**Office checklist.**

- Before installation, get the 510(k) number from the vendor; file registration within 10 days; calendar the annual fee.

**Records to keep.**

No retention rule in this source; keep registration certificates.
