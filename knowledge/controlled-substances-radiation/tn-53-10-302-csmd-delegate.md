# Who may be a CSMD delegate

**Rule.** A dentist may designate a person as a healthcare practitioner delegate to check the CSMD after registering that person as a delegate with the department. The dentist is responsible for the delegate's actions.

**Exact text.**

> ""Healthcare practitioner delegate" means any person designated by a healthcare practitioner to act as an agent of the healthcare practitioner, upon registering the person as a delegate and providing any information required by the department." (T.C.A. § 53-10-302(10), Justia unofficial mirror).

> "The healthcare practitioner shall be responsible for actions taken by their healthcare practitioner delegates pursuant to this part" (T.C.A. § 53-10-302(10), Justia unofficial mirror).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

None stated in the source.

**Exceptions.**

None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- Keep a list of registered delegates; remove a delegate in the CSMD on the day the person leaves. Dentist signs the list every 90 days.
