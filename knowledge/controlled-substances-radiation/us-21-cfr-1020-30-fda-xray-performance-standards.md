# FDA x-ray performance standards bind the manufacturer only

**Rule.** The FDA performance standards for diagnostic x-ray components bind the manufacturer and assembler, not the dental office. The office must keep maintenance and modification records for certified components under Tennessee rule 0400-20-06-.05.

**Exact text.**

> "Tube housing assemblies, x-ray controls, x-ray high-voltage generators, x-ray tables, cradles, film changers, vertical cassette holders mounted in a fixed location and cassette holders with front panels, and beam-limiting devices manufactured after August 1, 1974." (21 CFR 1020.30(a)(1)(i)(A)).

> "Each registrant shall keep a record of all maintenance and modifications performed on each diagnostic x-ray system containing any components certified under the applicable Federal standard during the period it is under his control, and transfer to the subsequent owner of the equipment." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-06-.05, Cornell LII mirror).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Components manufactured after August 1, 1974 (21 CFR 1020.30(a)(1)(i)(A)).

**Exceptions.**

None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- When buying or installing a unit, keep the installer's FDA Form 2579 assembler report and the 510(k) number (Tennessee registration needs it).

**Records to keep.**

Maintenance and modification records for certified components, transferred to the next owner (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-06-.05).
