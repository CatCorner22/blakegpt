# DEA registration and renewal for dentists

**Rule.** A dentist must hold a DEA registration before prescribing, administering or dispensing a controlled substance, and the office must hold a separate registration for each principal place of practice where controlled substances are dispensed. A registered dentist may apply for reregistration not more than 60 days before the registration expires.

**Exact text.**

> "No person required to be registered shall engage in any activity for which registration is required until the application for registration is granted and a Certificate of Registration is issued by the Administrator to such person." (21 CFR 1301.13(a)).

> "Any person who is registered may apply to be reregistered not more than 60 days before the expiration date of his/her registration" (21 CFR 1301.13(b)).

> "A separate registration is required for each principal place of business or professional practice at one general physical location where controlled substances are manufactured, distributed, imported, exported, or dispensed by a person." (21 CFR 1301.12(a)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Reregistration window opens 60 days before expiration (21 CFR 1301.13(b)).
- The practitioner category is "Dispensing or instructing", Schedules II-V, new application form 224 and renewal form 224a (21 CFR 1301.13(e) table).
- Amendment check: the eCFR source notes list amendments to 1301.13 at 90 FR 47563 (Oct. 2, 2025) and 91 FR 5239 (Feb. 5, 2026); those notices were not read.

**Exceptions.**

21 CFR 1301.12(b) lists locations that are not treated as places where controlled substances are dispensed; not summarized here.

**Office checklist.**

- Keep a calendar entry for each dentist's DEA expiration date; start renewal 60 days before it. Dentist signs; Amanda tracks.
- Confirm each office location that stocks or dispenses controlled substances has its own registration.
- At renewal, confirm the dentist has completed the MATE training attestation (see us-dea-mate-training-guidance).

**Records to keep.**

No retention rule in this source. Keep a copy of each current Certificate of Registration with the controlled-substance records.
