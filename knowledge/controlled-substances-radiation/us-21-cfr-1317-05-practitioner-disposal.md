# Disposal of office controlled-substance inventory

**Rule.** A registered dentist who disposes of controlled-substance inventory must promptly destroy it on site under 21 CFR part 1317 subpart C, promptly deliver it to a registered reverse distributor, return it for recall, or request DEA assistance. A registrant that destroys or causes destruction must record it on DEA Form 41 with the names and signatures of 2 witnessing employees.

**Exact text.**

> "Promptly destroy that controlled substance in accordance with subpart C of this part using an on-site method of destruction;" (21 CFR 1317.05(a)(1)).

> "Promptly deliver that controlled substance to a reverse distributor's registered location by common or contract carrier pick-up or by reverse distributor pick-up at the registrant's registered location;" (21 CFR 1317.05(a)(2)).

> "shall maintain a record of destruction on a DEA Form 41. The records shall be complete and accurate, and include the name and signature of the two employees who witnessed the destruction." (21 CFR 1304.21(e)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- 2 employee witnesses for Form 41 destruction records.

**Exceptions.**

- 21 CFR 1304.21(e) contains an exception for a substance dispensed for immediate administration at the registered location (wasted remainder); the full exception text was not summarized.

**Office checklist.**

- Use a reverse distributor for expired stock; keep the transfer paperwork; dentist signs.

**Records to keep.**

Form 41 and transfer records: at least 2 years (21 CFR 1304.04(a)).
