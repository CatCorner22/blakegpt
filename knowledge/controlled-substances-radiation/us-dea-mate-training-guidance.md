# DEA MATE Act one-time 8-hour substance use disorder training (guidance)

**Rule.** A DEA-registered dentist must complete a one-time total of 8 hours of training on treating and managing patients with opioid or other substance use disorders and must affirm completion on the next DEA initial or renewal application submitted on or after June 27, 2023. This card relies on DEA guidance, which is not law.

**Exact text.**

> "the Consolidated Appropriations Act of 2023 enacted a new one-time, eight-hour training requirement for all Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA)-registered practitioners on the treatment and management of patients with opioid or other substance use disorders." (DEA MATE training letter, p. 1 (guidance)).

> "Beginning on June 27, 2023, practitioners will be required to check a box on their online DEA registration form" (DEA MATE training letter, p. 1 (guidance)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- 8 hours total, one time (DEA guidance).
- Attestation starts with submissions on or after 2023-06-27 (DEA guidance).

**Exceptions.**

- DEA guidance excepts practitioners solely registered as veterinarians.

**Office checklist.**

- Before each dentist's renewal, collect the training certificate(s) adding up to 8 hours.
- Dentist checks the attestation box personally; Amanda files the certificates.

**Records to keep.**

No retention rule in this source. Keep training certificates with the dentist's DEA file.
