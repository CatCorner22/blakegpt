# ADA employment coverage

**Rule.** The owner dentist must assess ADA employer coverage using the statutory employee-count test.

**Exact text.**
> "15 or more employees for each working day in each of 20 or more calendar weeks in the current or preceding calendar year" (42 U.S.C. 12111(5)(A)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- 15 employees on each working day in 20 calendar weeks in the current or preceding calendar year (42 U.S.C. 12111(5)(A)).

**Exceptions.**
- The definition excludes the United States, specified federally owned corporations, Indian tribes, and specified private membership clubs (42 U.S.C. 12111(5)(B)).

**Office checklist.**
- Review headcounts when staffing changes and at year-end; owner dentist signs off.
- Keep the coverage analysis and supporting payroll or request records as office practice, subject to applicable retention and litigation holds.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
