# Age discrimination employer coverage

**Rule.** The owner dentist must assess private-employer ADEA coverage using the statutory employee-count test.

**Exact text.**
> "twenty or more employees for each working day in each of twenty or more calendar weeks in the current or preceding calendar year" (29 U.S.C. 630(b)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- 20 employees on each working day in 20 calendar weeks in the current or preceding calendar year (29 U.S.C. 630(b)).

**Exceptions.**
- The definition excludes specified federal entities and tax-exempt private membership clubs (29 U.S.C. 630(b)).

**Office checklist.**
- Review headcounts when staffing changes and at year-end; owner dentist signs off.
- Keep the coverage analysis and supporting payroll or request records as office practice, subject to applicable retention and litigation holds.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
