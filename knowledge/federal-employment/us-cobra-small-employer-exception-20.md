# COBRA small-employer exception

**Rule.** The plan administrator must assess the small-employer exception before treating a group health plan as exempt from federal COBRA.

**Exact text.**
> "fewer than 20 employees on a typical business day during the preceding calendar year" (29 U.S.C. 1161(b)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Fewer than 20 employees on at least 50 percent of typical business days during the preceding calendar year qualifies under the regulation (26 CFR 54.4980B-2 Q&A-5(b)).

**Exceptions.**
- Count full-time and part-time common-law employees; part-time employees count as fractions under the regulation. Consider all employers maintaining the plan (26 CFR 54.4980B-2 Q&A-5(c), (d), (e)). Tennessee state continuation law is not covered here.

**Office checklist.**
- At each plan-year review, obtain the administrator's documented count and owner dentist sign-off; recheck before denying continuation.
- Keep the coverage analysis and supporting payroll or request records as office practice, subject to applicable retention and litigation holds.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
