# Federal E-Verify participation rules

**Rule.** A participating practice must use E-Verify consistently for all new hires at the participating worksite. The practice must not use E-Verify to prescreen applicants.

**Exact text.**
> "If a worksite participates, the employer must use E-Verify for all new hires at that location." (E-Verify, E-Verify Fact vs. Fiction, Consistency and participation guidance).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Federal agency guidance describes participation as voluntary apart from applicable mandates and identifies consistent use at participating worksites; no numeric size threshold is stated here (E-Verify Fact vs. Fiction guidance, participation and Consistency).

**Exceptions.**
- Federal contractor and state requirements can make participation mandatory. These agency pages are guidance, not statutes or regulations.

**Office checklist.**
- Before enrolling or changing hiring procedures, check applicable mandates; for each participating-worksite hire follow the program process and do not treat a tentative mismatch as a final result; owner dentist signs off.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
