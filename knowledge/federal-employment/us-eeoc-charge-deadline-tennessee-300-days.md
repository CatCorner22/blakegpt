# EEOC charge deadlines in Tennessee

**Rule.** The practice must route a discrimination complaint or charge promptly to the owner dentist and preserve relevant records. The practice must not assume a filing deadline eliminates a complaint without claim-specific review.

**Exact text.**
> "An individual has 300 days from the date of alleged harm to file a charge with this office against an employer with 15 or more employees" (EEOC Nashville Area Office, Timeliness, Nashville Area Office Information (guidance)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Title VII uses a general 180-day charge deadline, extended to 300 days under the statutory state-or-local-proceeding conditions (42 U.S.C. 2000e-5(e)(1)).
- EEOC Nashville guidance states 300 days in Tennessee for listed discrimination claims against employers with at least 15 employees, and for age claims against employers with at least 20 employees (EEOC Nashville Timeliness guidance).
- That guidance states 180 days for filing covered smaller-employer claims with the appropriate state agency; this is not an extension of federal coverage (EEOC Nashville Timeliness guidance).
- ADEA uses 180 days ordinarily; in qualifying state-law cases the deadline is the earlier of 300 days after the practice or 30 days after state-proceeding termination notice (29 U.S.C. 626(d)(1)).
- The final Federal Register rule effective July 16, 2026 removed FEPA lists from the regulations in favor of lists on the EEOC website; the old regulatory list is not a current roster (91 FR 43539, summary and dates).

**Exceptions.**
- Equal Pay Act claims do not require a prior EEOC charge; other filing and suit rules remain claim-specific (EEOC Nashville Timeliness guidance). Agency pages are guidance, not law.

**Office checklist.**
- At receipt of any complaint or charge, record receipt and alleged-event dates, send it to counsel, and suspend routine destruction; owner dentist signs off.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
