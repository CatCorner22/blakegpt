# EEOC personnel and ADEA payroll record retention

**Rule.** Covered employers must preserve personnel and employment records for the applicable retention period. The practice must suspend routine destruction of relevant records when a charge or covered enforcement action requires preservation.

**Exact text.**
> "until final disposition of the charge or the action" (29 CFR 1602.14).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Under the general personnel-record rule, retain records for 1 year after creation or the personnel action, whichever is later; for involuntary termination keep the terminated person's records for 1 year after termination (29 CFR 1602.14).
- Under ADEA recordkeeping, retain payroll records for 3 years and listed personnel records for 1 year after the action (29 CFR 1627.3(a), (b)(1)).
- ADEA benefit-plan and seniority/merit-system records remain required throughout operation and for at least 1 year afterward (29 CFR 1627.3(b)(2)).

**Exceptions.**
- Charges and covered actions extend preservation beyond routine periods. The general rule reaches related employees and applicants; ADEA enforcement-action holds continue until final disposition (29 CFR 1602.14; 1627.3(b)(3)).

**Office checklist.**
- At each separation and before any deletion, check the retention schedule and legal holds; owner dentist signs off on destruction only after hold clearance.

**Records to keep.** Keep applications, hiring and personnel-action records, payroll, and covered plan records for the periods above; preserve all charge-relevant material until final disposition (29 CFR 1602.14; 1627.3).
