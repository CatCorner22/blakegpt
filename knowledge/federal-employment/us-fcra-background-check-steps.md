# Employment background reports and adverse action

**Rule.** Before obtaining an employment consumer report, the practice must give a clear standalone written disclosure and obtain written authorization. Before report-based adverse action, the practice must give the person a report copy and the required rights summary. When taking adverse action, the practice must provide the required adverse-action notice.

**Exact text.**
> "in a document that consists solely of the disclosure" (15 U.S.C. 1681b(b)(2)(A)(i)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- The statutory sequence is before procurement, before adverse action, and notice when adverse action occurs; these provisions do not set a fixed waiting interval for an ordinary dental-office employment decision (15 U.S.C. 1681b(b)(2)(A), (3)(A); 1681m(a)).

**Exceptions.**
- Special statutory procedures address specified remote transportation applications; do not import them into ordinary dental-office hiring. Authorization may appear on the disclosure document (15 U.S.C. 1681b(b)(2)(A)(ii), (B)-(C), (3)(B)-(C)).

**Office checklist.**
- For every vendor report, track disclosure, authorization, report delivery, rights summary, review, and final notice. Include the reporting agency's contact details, its nondecision role, and the person's copy and dispute rights; owner dentist signs off. FTC employer materials are guidance, not law.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
