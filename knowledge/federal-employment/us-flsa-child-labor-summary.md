# Federal child labor screening

**Rule.** The practice must screen a minor's age, tasks, equipment, and schedule before assigning work. The practice must not employ minors in prohibited occupations or outside permitted hours.

**Exact text.**
> "Outside of school hours" (29 CFR 570.35(a)(1)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- The general nonagricultural minimum age is 16, with limited permitted work for ages 14 and 15; hazardous-occupation restrictions extend to age 18 (29 CFR 570.2(a)).
- For ages 14 and 15, ordinary limits are 3 hours on school days and 18 hours in school weeks; otherwise 8 hours daily and 40 hours weekly, outside school hours (29 CFR 570.35(a)(1)-(5)).
- Work must fall between 7 a.m. and 7 p.m.; the evening limit becomes 9 p.m. from June 1 through Labor Day (29 CFR 570.35(a)(6)).

**Exceptions.**
- Special regulatory programs have exceptions. Do not treat a student placement as permission to ignore prohibited tasks; review the specific program and occupation rules (29 CFR 570.33; 570.35(c)).

**Office checklist.**
- Before hire and each schedule or task change, document age and permitted tasks; owner dentist signs off. Do not assign clinical or equipment tasks without a task-specific review.
