# Salary level and salary basis for white-collar exemptions

**Rule.** The practice must satisfy the applicable salary-level, salary-basis, and duties tests before using a white-collar exemption. The practice must not treat a salary or job title alone as enough.

**Exact text.**
> "an employee must be compensated on a salary basis at a rate of not less than $684 per week" (29 CFR 541.600(a)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- The ordinary salary level is $684 per week; longer pay periods require equivalent amounts (29 CFR 541.600(a), (b)).
- The final Federal Register rule published May 15, 2026 restored pre-2024 regulatory text after Texas v. U.S. Department of Labor and Flint Avenue vacated the 2024 rule. Do not use that vacated rule's higher salary levels as current thresholds (91 FR 27833, summary and background).
- The vacated rule used $844 per week beginning July 1 of 2024 and scheduled $1,128 per week for January 1 of 2025. The first court vacatur preceded that scheduled later increase; neither amount is the current salary level (91 FR 27833, background, discussing the Texas and Flint Avenue judgments).

**Exceptions.**
- Salary requirements do not apply to the practicing medical professionals identified in the regulation. Salary deductions have limited exceptions; review them before docking pay (29 CFR 541.600(e); 541.602(b)).

**Office checklist.**
- Review actual duties and pay at hiring, role changes, and before deductions; owner dentist signs off on the exemption analysis.
