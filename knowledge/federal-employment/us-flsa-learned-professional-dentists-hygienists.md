# Dentists and hygienists under the professional exemption

**Rule.** The practice must evaluate dental hygienists against the education, duties, and applicable pay tests before claiming exemption. The practice may apply the medical-practice exemption to dentists holding a valid license and actually practicing.

**Exact text.**
> "generally meet the duties requirements for the learned professional exemption" (29 CFR 541.301(e)(3)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- The hygienist example requires 4 academic years of qualifying pre-professional and professional study; the source spells out the number (29 CFR 541.301(e)(3)).
- The ordinary professional salary level is $684 per week, subject to the regulation's exceptions (29 CFR 541.300(a)(1); 541.600(a)).

**Exceptions.**
- The salary requirements do not apply to qualifying practicing dentists (29 CFR 541.304(b), (d)). Do not extend the hygienist example to assistants or front-desk staff based on title alone; the primary-duty and specialized-learning tests still govern (29 CFR 541.300; 541.301(a)-(d)).

**Office checklist.**
- At hiring and each duties change, collect credentials and actual duties; owner dentist signs off. DOL Fact Sheet guidance explains the exemption but is not law.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
