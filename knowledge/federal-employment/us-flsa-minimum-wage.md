# Federal minimum wage

**Rule.** The practice must pay covered nonexempt employees at least the federal minimum wage.

**Exact text.**
> "$7.25 an hour" (29 U.S.C. 206(a)(1)(C)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- $7.25 per hour is the federal statutory rate (29 U.S.C. 206(a)(1)(C)).

**Exceptions.**
- Coverage and exemptions require a separate analysis. Tennessee minimum-wage rules are not covered here.

**Office checklist.**
- At every payroll, compare each covered employee's pay with recorded hours; owner dentist signs off on corrections. DOL minimum-wage material is guidance, not law.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
