# FMLA employer notices

**Rule.** Covered employers must post the general FMLA notice and provide required employee notices. The practice must give eligibility, rights-and-responsibilities, and designation notices when the regulatory triggers occur.

**Exact text.**
> "Covered employers must post this general notice even if no employees are eligible for FMLA leave." (29 CFR 825.300(a)(2)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Ordinarily provide eligibility notice within 5 business days after a leave request or learning that leave may qualify (29 CFR 825.300(b)(1)).
- Ordinarily provide written designation notice within 5 business days after enough information is available to determine whether leave qualifies (29 CFR 825.300(d)(1)).
- Give written rights-and-responsibilities notice with the eligibility notice; include general information in the handbook or provide it on hire when the rule requires (29 CFR 825.300(a)(3), (c)).

**Exceptions.**
- Extenuating circumstances can affect the notice deadlines; an ineligible employee must receive at least a reason for ineligibility (29 CFR 825.300(b)(1), (2), (d)(1)).

**Office checklist.**
- Check poster access at onboarding and after workplace changes; track notices for every leave matter; owner dentist signs off on eligibility and designation. DOL employer materials are guidance, not law.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
