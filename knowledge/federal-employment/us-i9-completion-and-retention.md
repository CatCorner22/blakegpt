# Employment verification and Form I-9 retention

**Rule.** The practice must ensure the employee completes and signs the employee section at hire. The employer or authorized representative must examine acceptable documentation and complete the employer section within the applicable deadline.

**Exact text.**
> "three years after the date of the hire or one year after the date the individual's employment is terminated, whichever is later" (8 CFR 274a.2(b)(2)(i)(A)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Complete Section 1 at hire, meaning by the first workday; complete Section 2 within 3 business days of hire (8 CFR 274a.2(b)(1)(i)(A), (ii)).
- Retain the form until the later of 3 years after hire or 1 year after employment ends (8 CFR 274a.2(b)(2)(i)(A)).

**Exceptions.**
- For employment lasting less than 3 business days, complete verification at hire. Use remote examination only under an authorized alternative procedure, not as an informal substitute (8 CFR 274a.2(b)(1)(iii), (ix)).

**Office checklist.**
- Track each hire from offer acceptance through completion; record the retention calculation at separation; owner dentist signs off on corrections. USCIS pages are guidance, not law.

**Records to keep.** Keep completed employment-verification forms through the later deadline above, with secure access and inspection availability (8 CFR 274a.2(b)(2)).
