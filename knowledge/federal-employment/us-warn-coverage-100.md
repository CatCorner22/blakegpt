# WARN coverage and advance notice

**Rule.** Covered employers must give required written WARN notice before a qualifying plant closing or mass layoff.

**Exact text.**
> "100 or more employees, excluding part-time employees" (29 U.S.C. 2101(a)(1)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Employer test: 100 employees excluding part-time employees, or 100 employees collectively working at least 4,000 hours weekly excluding overtime (29 U.S.C. 2101(a)(1)).
- Ordinarily give 60 days' notice to affected employees or representatives and designated government recipients (29 U.S.C. 2102(a)).

**Exceptions.**
- Employer size alone does not trigger notice; event definitions also apply. Limited notice reductions apply to specified capital-seeking, unforeseeable-business, and disaster situations; give as much notice as practicable and explain the reduction (29 U.S.C. 2101(a)(2)-(3); 2102(b)).

**Office checklist.**
- Before a closure or group layoff, send counts and event facts to counsel; owner dentist signs off before notices or any exception decision.
- Keep the coverage analysis and supporting payroll or request records as office practice, subject to applicable retention and litigation holds.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
