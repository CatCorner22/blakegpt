# Tennessee professional corporations: share ownership limits

**Rule.** A dental professional corporation may issue shares to persons not licensed in Tennessee only if the licensing board specifically authorizes it, and then only to the qualified holders listed in T.C.A. 48-101-610(a)(2)(A)-(D). Shares issued in violation of this section are void.

**Exact text.**

> "A professional corporation may only issue shares for sale to other persons not licensed to practice such profession in Tennessee if the licensing authority which licenses the professionals forming such corporations specifically authorizes the issuance of such shares." (T.C.A. 48-101-610(a)(2)).

> "Shares issued in violation of this section or a rule promulgated under this section are void." (T.C.A. 48-101-610(c)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- No number or deadline in these subsections.

**Exceptions.**

- Licensing-board authorization under (a)(2); board may further restrict by rule under (b).

**Office checklist.**

- At any stock issuance or transfer, confirm the recipient is a Tennessee-licensed dentist; also apply T.C.A. 63-5-121(a)(2).
- Practice counsel signs off on every transfer.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
