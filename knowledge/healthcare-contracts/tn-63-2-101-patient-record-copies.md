# Tennessee: give patients a copy of their records within 10 working days

**Rule.** The practice must give a patient or the patient's authorized representative a copy of the patient's records within 10 working days after receiving a written request. The practice may offer a summary but a summary does not replace the full record.

**Exact text.**

> "a health care provider shall provide to a patient or a patient's authorized representative a copy of such patient's medical records within ten (10) working days of receipt of a written request by the patient or the patient's authorized representative." (T.C.A. 63-2-101(a)(1)).

> "\"Health care provider\" means any person required to be licensed under this title;" (T.C.A. 63-2-101(c)(2)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- 10 working days from receipt of a written request (T.C.A. 63-2-101(a)(1)).

**Exceptions.**

- None stated in subsection (a).

**Office checklist.**

- Date-stamp each written records request on receipt and calendar 10 working days.
- Office manager logs completion; review the log monthly.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this section.
