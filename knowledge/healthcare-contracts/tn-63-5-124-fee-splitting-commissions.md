# Tennessee dentistry: no fee splitting for referrals and no commissions on professional fees

**Rule.** A dentist must not divide or agree to split fees for professional services with any person for bringing or referring a patient. A dentist must not pay or accept commissions in any form on fees for professional services, references, consultations, radiographs, prescriptions, or other services or articles supplied to patients.

**Exact text.**

> "(11) Division of fees or agreeing to split or divide fees received for professional services with any person for bringing or referring a patient;" (T.C.A. 63-5-124(a)(11)).

> "(15) Payment or acceptance of commissions in any form or manner on fees for professional services, references, consultations, pathological reports, radiographs, prescriptions or on other services or articles supplied to patients;" (T.C.A. 63-5-124(a)(15)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- No number or deadline in these paragraphs; violation is a ground for license discipline and civil penalty under T.C.A. 63-5-124(a).

**Exceptions.**

- None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- Flag any marketing, MSO, or referral contract that pays or charges a percentage of patient fees; send to counsel because (a)(15) reaches commissions "in any form or manner".
- Confirm no staff receives a bounty per referred patient from a specialist or lab.
- Owner dentist signs off once a year.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
