# Tennessee Medicaid False Claims Act: penalties for false TennCare claims

**Rule.** The practice must not knowingly present, or cause to be presented, a false or fraudulent claim under the Tennessee Medicaid program, or make a false record material to such a claim or to an obligation to repay the state.

**Exact text.**

> "(A) Knowingly presents, or causes to be presented, a false or fraudulent claim for payment or approval under the medicaid program;" (T.C.A. 71-5-182(a)(1)(A)).

> "is liable to the state for a civil penalty of not less than five thousand dollars ($5,000) and not more than twenty-five thousand dollars ($25,000), adjusted by the Federal Civil Penalties Inflation Adjustment Act of 1990 ... plus three (3) times the amount of damages which the state sustains because of the act of that person." (T.C.A. 71-5-182(a)(1)).

> "furnished officials of the state responsible for investigating false claims violations with all information known to such person about the violation within thirty (30) days after the date on which the defendant first obtained the information" (T.C.A. 71-5-182(a)(2)(A)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Penalty: $5,000 to $25,000 per claim, as adjusted, plus 3 times the state's damages (T.C.A. 71-5-182(a)(1)).
- Reduced damages possible if reported within 30 days and other (a)(2) conditions are met (T.C.A. 71-5-182(a)(2)).

**Exceptions.**

- Reduced damages under T.C.A. 71-5-182(a)(2) when all its conditions are met.

**Office checklist.**

- Include TennCare claims in the quarterly claim audit (see us-fca-false-claims).
- Report suspected false TennCare claims to the owner dentist and counsel at once.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
