# 42 CFR Part 2: when substance use disorder confidentiality rules reach a dental office

**Rule.** A dental practice must apply Part 2 only if it holds itself out as providing substance use disorder diagnosis, treatment, or referral and is federally assisted, or if it receives Part 2 records with a Part 2 notice. A general dental office that only records a patient's substance use history is not, by that fact, a Part 2 program.

**Exact text.**

> "Program means: (1) A person (other than a general medical facility) that holds itself out as providing, and provides, substance use disorder diagnosis, treatment, or referral for treatment" (42 CFR 2.11).

> "Part 2 program means a federally assisted program (federally assisted as defined in § 2.12(b) and program as defined in this section)." (42 CFR 2.11).

> "These regulations cover any information (including information on referral and intake) about patients receiving diagnosis, treatment, or referral for treatment for a substance use disorder created by a part 2 program." (42 CFR 2.12(e)(1)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- No number or deadline in these sections.

**Exceptions.**

- See 42 CFR 2.12(c)-(d) for listed exceptions (not summarized).

**Office checklist.**

- If a records request or referral packet from an addiction-treatment provider carries a Part 2 notice, keep it segregated and do not redisclose without counsel review.
- Review once a year whether the practice advertises any substance use disorder service; owner dentist signs off.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in these sections.
