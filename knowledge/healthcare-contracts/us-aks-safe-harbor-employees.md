# AKS safe harbor: bona fide employees

**Rule.** A dental practice may pay a bona fide employee for furnishing items or services payable by a Federal health care program without that pay counting as remuneration under the AKS; "employee" has the meaning in 26 U.S.C. 3121(d)(2).

**Exact text.**

> "\"remuneration\" does not include any amount paid by an employer to an employee, who has a bona fide employment relationship with the employer, for employment in the furnishing of any item or service for which payment may be made in whole or in part under Medicare, Medicaid or other Federal health care programs." (42 CFR 1001.952(i)).

> "the term employee has the same meaning as it does for purposes of 26 U.S.C. 3121(d)(2)." (42 CFR 1001.952(i)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- No number or deadline in this paragraph.

**Exceptions.**

- Independent contractors (1099 workers) are not covered; use the personal-services safe harbor (us-aks-safe-harbor-personal-services).

**Office checklist.**

- Confirm dentists, hygienists and assistants paid production bonuses are W-2 employees.
- Treat 1099 dentists or marketers under the personal-services checklist.
- Office manager reviews classification at hire; owner dentist signs off.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source. Keep the signed agreement and fair-market-value support for the agreement term plus the office's general contract-retention period (office policy, not law).
