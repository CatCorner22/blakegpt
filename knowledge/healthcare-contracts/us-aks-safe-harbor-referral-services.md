# AKS safe harbor: referral services

**Rule.** A dental practice may pay a patient-referral service only if the service admits every qualified participant, charges every participant equally based only on operating cost and not on referral volume or value, does not control how the practice treats referred patients beyond pricing, and makes the required written disclosures to each person seeking a referral.

**Exact text.**

> "Any payment the participant makes to the referral service is assessed equally against and collected equally from all participants and is based only on the cost of operating the referral service, and not on the volume or value of any referrals" (42 CFR 1001.952(f)(2)).

> "The referral service makes the following five disclosures to each person seeking a referral" (42 CFR 1001.952(f)(4)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- 4 standards, all required (42 CFR 1001.952(f)).
- 5 written disclosures to each person seeking a referral (42 CFR 1001.952(f)(4)).

**Exceptions.**

- None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- Before joining any dentist-referral or lead-generation service, get its fee schedule and disclosure form in writing.
- Reject any per-lead, per-patient, or per-booked-appointment fee for TennCare or Medicare patients.
- Also confirm Tennessee registration (tn-63-5-133-dental-referral-service). Owner dentist signs off.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source. Keep the signed agreement and fair-market-value support for the agreement term plus the office's general contract-retention period (office policy, not law).
