# AKS safe harbor: space rental

**Rule.** A dental practice that leases office space to or from a referral source may use the space-rental safe harbor only if the lease meets all 6 standards: written and signed, covers all premises, specifies any part-time schedule, lasts at least 1 year, sets aggregate rent in advance at fair market value without regard to referrals, and rents no more space than commercially reasonable.

**Exact text.**

> "The lease agreement is set out in writing and signed by the parties." (42 CFR 1001.952(b)(1)).

> "The term of the lease is for not less than one year." (42 CFR 1001.952(b)(4)).

> "The aggregate rental charge is set in advance, is consistent with fair market value in arms-length transactions and is not determined in a manner that takes into account the volume or value of any referrals or business otherwise generated between the parties" (42 CFR 1001.952(b)(5)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- 6 standards, all required (42 CFR 1001.952(b)).
- Term: not less than 1 year (42 CFR 1001.952(b)(4)).
- Part-time leases must state the exact schedule, length, and rent for each interval (42 CFR 1001.952(b)(3)).

**Exceptions.**

- None stated in the source; fair market value excludes any added value from proximity to referral sources (42 CFR 1001.952(b), note).

**Office checklist.**

- Check each lease with a referral source (for example, a specialist subleasing an operatory) against all 6 standards at signing and at renewal.
- Get a written fair-market-value rent opinion or comparable listings before signing.
- Owner dentist signs the checklist.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source. Keep the signed agreement and fair-market-value support for the agreement term plus the office's general contract-retention period (office policy, not law).
