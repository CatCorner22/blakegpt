# Beneficiary inducement CMP and the $15 / $75 nominal-value guidance

**Rule.** The practice must not offer or give remuneration to a Medicare or TennCare/Medicaid patient that it knows or should know is likely to influence the patient to choose the practice. The practice may give non-cash gifts worth no more than $15 per item and $75 per patient per year under OIG's nominal-value guidance (guidance, not law).

**Exact text.**

> "offers to or transfers remuneration to any individual eligible for benefits under subchapter XVIII of this chapter, or under a State health care program (as defined in section 1320a–7(h) of this title) that such person knows or should know is likely to influence such individual to order or receive from a particular provider, practitioner, or su[pplier]" (42 U.S.C. 1320a-7a(a)(5)).

> "The term \"remuneration\" includes the waiver of coinsurance and deductible amounts (or any part thereof), and transfers of items or services for free or for other than fair market value." (42 U.S.C. 1320a-7a(i)(6)).

> "are interpreting \"nominal value\" as having a retail value of no more than $15 per item or $75 in the aggregate per patient on an annual basis. As with our previous interpretation, the items may not be cash or cash equivalents." (OIG Policy Statement, December 7, 2016).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Nominal value: no more than $15 per item and $75 aggregate per patient per year (OIG Policy Statement, December 7, 2016).
- Cash and cash equivalents (gift cards usable like cash) are never nominal (same).

**Exceptions.**

- Statutory exclusions from "remuneration" listed in 42 U.S.C. 1320a-7a(i)(6), including non-routine, financial-need copay waivers that are not advertised (not all summarized here).

**Office checklist.**

- Keep a log of patient gifts (toothbrush kits, raffle prizes, referral thank-yous) with retail value and patient payer.
- Block cash or gift-card rewards for TennCare and Medicare patients.
- Review the log quarterly; owner dentist signs off.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
