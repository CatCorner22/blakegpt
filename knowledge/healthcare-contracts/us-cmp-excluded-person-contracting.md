# Civil money penalty for employing or contracting with an excluded person

**Rule.** The practice must not employ or contract with a person it knows or should know is excluded to provide items or services payable by a Federal health care program. OIG may impose a penalty per item or service and an assessment of up to 3 times the claims or the excluded person's total costs.

**Exact text.**

> "arranges or contracts (by employment or otherwise) with an individual or entity that the person knows or should know is excluded from participation in a Federal health care program (as defined in section 1320a–7b(f) of this title), for the provision of items or services for which payment may be made under such a program" (42 U.S.C. 1320a-7a(a)(6)).

> "For each individual violation of § 1003.200(b)(4), the OIG may impose a penalty of not more than $10,000 for conduct that occurred on or before February 9, 2018, and not more than $20,000 for conduct that occurred after February 9, 2018, for each separately billable or non-separately-billable item or service provided, furnished, ordered, or pr[escribed]" (42 CFR 1003.210(a)(4)).

> "The total costs (including salary, benefits, taxes, and other money or items of value) related to the excluded individual or entity incurred by the person that employs, contracts with, or otherwise arranges for an excluded individual or entity" (42 CFR 1003.210(b)(2)(ii)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Penalty: not more than $20,000 per item or service for conduct after February 9, 2018 (42 CFR 1003.210(a)(4)).
- Assessment: not more than 3 times the amount claimed or the total costs of the excluded person, including salary and benefits (42 CFR 1003.210(b)(2)).

**Exceptions.**

- None stated in the source; liability turns on "knows or should know" (42 U.S.C. 1320a-7a(a)(6)).

**Office checklist.**

- Screen every employee, dentist, hygienist, assistant, billing vendor and contractor against the LEIE at hire and monthly (see us-oig-exclusion-screening-guidance).
- Office manager runs the check; owner dentist initials the monthly log.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source. Keep screening printouts as long as the practice keeps billing records (office policy; no period is stated in these sections).
