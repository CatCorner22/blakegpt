# Federal False Claims Act: liability for false claims to the Government

**Rule.** The practice must not knowingly present, or cause to be presented, a false or fraudulent claim, or make a false record material to a claim. "Knowingly" includes deliberate ignorance and reckless disregard.

**Exact text.**

> "(A) knowingly presents, or causes to be presented, a false or fraudulent claim for payment or approval; (B) knowingly makes, uses, or causes to be made or used, a false record or statement material to a false or fraudulent claim" (31 U.S.C. 3729(a)(1)(A)-(B)).

> "is liable to the United States Government for a civil penalty of not less than $5,000 and not more than $10,000, as adjusted by the Federal Civil Penalties Inflation Adjustment Act of 1990 ... plus 3 times the amount of damages which the Government sustains because of the act of that person." (31 U.S.C. 3729(a)(1)).

> "(ii) acts in deliberate ignorance of the truth or falsity of the information; or (iii) acts in reckless disregard of the truth or falsity of the information" (31 U.S.C. 3729(b)(1)(A)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Penalty: $5,000 to $10,000 per claim, as inflation-adjusted, plus 3 times damages (31 U.S.C. 3729(a)(1)).
- Reduced damages if the person reports within 30 days after first obtaining the information and cooperates (31 U.S.C. 3729(a)(2)).

**Exceptions.**

- Reduced damages under 31 U.S.C. 3729(a)(2) when all its conditions are met.

**Office checklist.**

- Audit a sample of TennCare claims against chart notes every quarter; owner dentist signs off.
- Report a suspected false claim to the owner dentist and counsel at once; the 30-day clock in 3729(a)(2) runs from first knowledge.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
