# HIPAA: do not condition treatment on a signed authorization; compound authorizations are limited

**Rule.** The practice must not condition treatment, payment, or eligibility on a patient signing a HIPAA authorization, except as 45 CFR 164.508(b)(4) allows. The practice must not combine an authorization with another document to create a compound authorization except as 45 CFR 164.508(b)(3) allows.

**Exact text.**

> "A covered entity may not condition the provision to an individual of treatment, payment, enrollment in the health plan, or eligibility for benefits on the provision of an authorization, except" (45 CFR 164.508(b)(4)).

> "An authorization for use or disclosure of protected health information may not be combined with any other document to create a compound authorization, except as follows" (45 CFR 164.508(b)(3)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- No number or deadline in these paragraphs.

**Exceptions.**

- Research-related treatment (45 CFR 164.508(b)(4)(i)) and other listed exceptions in (b)(4).
- Research and psychotherapy-note combinations listed in (b)(3)(i)-(iii).

**Office checklist.**

- Keep marketing, photo-release, and testimonial authorizations on separate forms from consent-to-treat and financial policies.
- Never refuse care because a patient declines a marketing or photo authorization.
- Review intake packet once a year; privacy officer signs off.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this section.
