# HIPAA: business associate must notify the covered entity of a breach within 60 calendar days

**Rule.** A business associate must notify the practice of a breach of unsecured PHI without unreasonable delay and no later than 60 calendar days after discovery. The practice may set a shorter deadline in its business associate agreement.

**Exact text.**

> "a business associate shall provide the notification required by paragraph (a) of this section without unreasonable delay and in no case later than 60 calendar days after discovery of a breach." (45 CFR 164.410(b)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Outer limit: 60 calendar days after discovery (45 CFR 164.410(b)).
- "Without unreasonable delay" can require earlier notice (same).

**Exceptions.**

- Law-enforcement delay under 45 CFR 164.412.

**Office checklist.**

- Confirm each business associate agreement requires breach notice to the practice sooner than 60 days.
- Review vendor list once a year; office manager signs off as privacy officer.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this section; HIPAA documentation retention is in 45 CFR 164.530(j) (not fetched).
