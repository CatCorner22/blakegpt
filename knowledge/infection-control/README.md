# Infection prevention in dental settings

Scope: Tennessee and federal rules and CDC guidance on infection prevention for a small Tennessee dental office: sterilization and its monitoring, waterlines, hand hygiene, PPE, respiratory hygiene, safe injections, single-use devices, surface disinfection, instrument processing, staff immunization and TB screening, the OSHA hepatitis B duty, medical waste and sharps, and amalgam separators. CDC items are guidance, not law; Tennessee Rule 0460-01-.11 and the federal regulations are law.
