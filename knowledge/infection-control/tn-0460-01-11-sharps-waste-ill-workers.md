# Resuscitation devices, sharps safety, waste disposal and workers with weeping lesions

**Rule.** The practitioner must keep mouthpieces, resuscitation bags or other ventilation devices available. Staff must follow 29 CFR 1910.1030 to prevent sharps injuries and must dispose of sharps and contaminated waste under federal, state and local rules. Workers with exudative lesions or weeping dermatitis must not give patient care until healed unless barriers prevent contact.

**Exact text.**

> "a practitioner shall ensure that mouthpieces, resuscitation bags, or other ventilation devices, appropriate to the patient population served, are available." (0460-01-.11(13)).

> "If a needlestick injury occurs, the dentist shall comply with the requirements established by OSHA." (0460-01-.11(14)).

> "All sharp items and contaminated wastes must be packaged and disposed of according to the requirements established by any federal, Tennessee state, and/or local government agencies which regulate health or environmental standards." (0460-01-.11(15)).

> "All dental health care workers who have exudative lesions or weeping dermatitis shall refrain from contact with equipment, devices, and appliances that may be used for or during patient care" (0460-01-.11(16)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- No numbers; triggers are a needlestick (14) and an exudative lesion or weeping dermatitis (16).

**Exceptions.**

- A worker with lesions may treat patients if "barrier techniques would prevent patient contact" with the worker's blood or body fluid (0460-01-.11(16)).

**Office checklist.**

- Check adult and child resuscitation masks/bags in the emergency kit monthly.
- Route every needlestick to the OSHA post-exposure process the same day.
- Confirm the medical waste hauler contract is current each year.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source. See us-osha-1910-1030-h-records.
