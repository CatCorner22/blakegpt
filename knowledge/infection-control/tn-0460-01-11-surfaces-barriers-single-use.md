# Surface cleaning, EPA-registered disinfectant or bleach, barriers and single-use items

**Rule.** Staff must clean contaminated surfaces before disinfecting them, and must disinfect with an EPA-registered disinfectant used per label or with 1:10 or 1:100 bleach between patients when no barrier is used. Staff must replace barriers between patients and must not reuse items labeled single use.

**Exact text.**

> "Environmental surfaces that are contaminated by blood or saliva must be properly cleaned prior to disinfecting." (0460-01-.11(5)).

> "Disinfection must be accomplished with an appropriate disinfectant that is registered with the EPA and used in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions or with bleach used in a dilution ratio of one (1) to ten (10) or one hundred (100) [1:10 or 1:100]." (0460-01-.11(6)).

> "The disinfection process must be followed between each patient in the absence of a barrier." (0460-01-.11(6)).

> "The barrier must be removed, discarded, and then replaced between patients." (0460-01-.11(7)).

> "All single use or disposable items, labeled as such, used to treat a patient must be discarded and not reused." (0460-01-.11(8)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Bleach: 1:10 or 1:100 (0460-01-.11(6)).
- Between each patient: disinfect unbarriered surfaces; change barriers (0460-01-.11(6)-(7)).

**Exceptions.**

- Surfaces covered by a barrier need not be disinfected between patients under (6) ("in the absence of a barrier").

**Office checklist.**

- Stock only EPA-registered disinfectants; keep the label contact time posted at each operatory.
- Use barriers on hard-to-clean items (switches, light handles, keyboards).
- Spot-check 1 operatory turnover weekly; record on the IPC checklist.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
