# Environmental surface disinfection with EPA-registered products (CDC guidance)

**Rule.** This is guidance; Tennessee 0460-01-.11(5)-(7) is the binding rule. The office may follow CDC: barrier hard-to-clean surfaces and change barriers between patients, or clean and disinfect clinical contact surfaces with an EPA-registered hospital disinfectant after each patient, using a tuberculocidal product when blood is visible.

**Exact text.**

> "Clean and disinfect clinical contact surfaces that are not barrier-protected with an EPA-registered hospital disinfectant after each patient. Use an intermediate-level disinfectant (i.e., tuberculocidal claim) if visibly contaminated with blood." (2016 Summary, Environmental item 1.b).

> "Follow manufacturer instructions for use of cleaners and EPA-registered disinfectants (e.g., amount, dilution, contact time, safe use, disposal)." (2016 Summary, Environmental item 3).

> "Disinfectant products should not be used as cleaners unless the label indicates the product is suitable for such use." (2016 Summary, Environmental Infection Prevention and Control).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- After each patient for unbarriered clinical contact surfaces.

**Exceptions.**

- Barrier-protected surfaces: change the barrier instead.

**Office checklist.**

- Check each disinfectant has an EPA registration number and a health care label claim.
- Post contact times by product.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
