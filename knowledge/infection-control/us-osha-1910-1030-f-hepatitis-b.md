# OSHA: offer hepatitis B vaccine within 10 working days

**Rule.** The employer must offer the hepatitis B vaccine series at no cost, after bloodborne training and within 10 working days of initial assignment, to every employee with occupational exposure. The employer must get a signed Appendix A declination from any employee who declines, and must still vaccinate that employee on later request.

**Exact text.**

> "Hepatitis B vaccination shall be made available after the employee has received the training required in paragraph (g)(2)(vii)(I) and within 10 working days of initial assignment to all employees who have occupational exposure" (29 CFR 1910.1030(f)(2)(i)).

> "Made available at no cost to the employee" (29 CFR 1910.1030(f)(1)(ii)(A)).

> "The employer shall assure that employees who decline to accept hepatitis B vaccination offered by the employer sign the statement in appendix A." (29 CFR 1910.1030(f)(2)(iv)).

> "The employer shall not make participation in a prescreening program a prerequisite for receiving hepatitis B vaccination." (29 CFR 1910.1030(f)(2)(ii)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Within 10 working days of initial assignment, after training (f)(2)(i).
- No cost (f)(1)(ii)(A).

**Exceptions.**

- Not required if the employee already had the complete series, antibody testing shows immunity, or the vaccine is medically contraindicated (f)(2)(i).

**Office checklist.**

- On day 1 for clinical hires: bloodborne training, then schedule vaccine or collect proof within 10 working days.
- File signed Appendix A declinations.

**Records to keep.** Vaccination status and dates go in the employee medical record, kept for employment plus 30 years ((h)(1)(ii)(B), (h)(1)(iv)).
