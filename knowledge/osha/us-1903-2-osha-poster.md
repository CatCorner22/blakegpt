# Federal OSHA notice and state-poster substitution

**Rule.** The employer must post and keep the required safety notice conspicuously and protect it from alteration, defacement, and covering. A covered state-plan employer may use its approved state poster to satisfy the federal posting requirement.

**Exact text.**
> "Each employer shall take steps to insure that such notices are not altered, defaced, or covered by other material." (29 CFR 1903.2(a)(1)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Federal poster reproductions must be at least 8 1/2 by 14 inches with print at least 10 pt; heading generally at least 36 pt (29 CFR 1903.2(a)(3)).

**Exceptions.**
- An approved state poster satisfies the federal notice requirement for operations covered by the state plan; operations outside state-plan issues follow the federal rule (29 CFR 1903.2(a)(2)). For this practice use the TOSHA posting card.
