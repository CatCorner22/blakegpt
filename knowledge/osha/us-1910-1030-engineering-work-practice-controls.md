# Engineering and work-practice controls

**Rule.** The employer must use engineering and work-practice controls to eliminate or minimize exposure and add PPE when exposure remains. Employees must wash after glove removal and exposure, and must not shear or break contaminated needles.

**Exact text.**
> "Engineering controls shall be examined and maintained or replaced on a regular schedule to ensure their effectiveness." (29 CFR 1910.1030(d)(2)(ii)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Wash hands immediately or as soon as feasible after removing PPE; wash exposed skin or flush mucous membranes immediately or as soon as feasible (29 CFR 1910.1030(d)(2)(v)-(vi)).

**Exceptions.**
- Bending, recapping, or removal requires a demonstration that no alternative is feasible or a specific medical or dental procedure requires it; use a mechanical device or 1-handed technique (29 CFR 1910.1030(d)(2)(vii)(A)-(B)).

**Office checklist.**
- Practice workflow: Amanda: check handwashing access, sharps containers, and maintenance schedule; keep food out of exposure areas. Owner dentist: approve procedure-specific recapping justification and controls.
