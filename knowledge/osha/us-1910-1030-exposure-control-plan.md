# Bloodborne pathogen exposure control plan

**Rule.** The employer must maintain an accessible written exposure control plan and determine exposure without considering PPE. The employer must review the plan annually, consider safer devices, and document input from exposed non-managerial direct-care employees.

**Exact text.**
> "The Exposure Control Plan shall be reviewed and updated at least annually" (29 CFR 1910.1030(c)(1)(iv)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Review and update at least annually and when tasks, procedures, or exposed positions change; document annual safer-device consideration and implementation (29 CFR 1910.1030(c)(1)(iv)).

**Exceptions.**
- None stated for employers with occupational exposure (29 CFR 1910.1030(c)(1)(i)).

**Office checklist.**
- Practice workflow: Amanda: maintain job/task exposure lists and implementation schedule; collect employee input during each review. Owner dentist: sign off on plan updates and device decisions.

**Records to keep.** Keep the current plan, safer-device review, and employee-input documentation; no fixed plan retention period stated (29 CFR 1910.1030(c)(1)(ii)-(v)).
