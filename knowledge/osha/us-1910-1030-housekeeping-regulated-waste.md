# Housekeeping and regulated waste

**Rule.** The employer must maintain a clean, sanitary worksite and implement a written cleaning and decontamination schedule. Employees must place contaminated sharps and other regulated waste in appropriate closed, leak-resistant, labeled or color-coded containers.

**Exact text.**
> "Broken glassware which may be contaminated shall not be picked up directly with the hands." (29 CFR 1910.1030(d)(4)(ii)(D)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Decontaminate surfaces after procedures, immediately or as soon as feasible after overt contamination or spills, and at shift end if contamination may have occurred since cleaning (29 CFR 1910.1030(d)(4)(ii)(A)).
- Discard contaminated sharps immediately or as soon as feasible; replace containers routinely without overfilling (29 CFR 1910.1030(d)(4)(iii)(A)(1)-(2)).

**Exceptions.**
- Regulated-waste disposal must also comply with applicable federal, state, territorial, and local regulations; this card does not establish those separate disposal rules (29 CFR 1910.1030(d)(4)(iii)(C)).

**Office checklist.**
- Practice workflow: Amanda: maintain the written schedule, check containers, and arrange laundry handling with minimal agitation. Owner dentist: sign off on methods suitable for each area and task.

**Records to keep.** Keep the written cleaning schedule; no retention period stated (29 CFR 1910.1030(d)(4)(i)).
