# Bloodborne pathogen medical and training records

**Rule.** The employer must maintain accurate confidential medical records for occupationally exposed employees and required training records. The employer must provide access to authorized recipients and follow record-transfer requirements.

**Exact text.**
> "Training records shall be maintained for 3 years from the date on which the training occurred." (29 CFR 1910.1030(h)(2)(ii)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Medical records: employment plus 30 years (29 CFR 1910.1030(h)(1)(iv)).
- Training records: 3 years from the training date (29 CFR 1910.1030(h)(2)(ii)).

**Exceptions.**
- Medical disclosure requires the employee's express written consent except as required by this section or law; training access and medical access differ (29 CFR 1910.1030(h)(1)(iii), (h)(3)).

**Office checklist.**
- Practice workflow: Amanda: separate confidential medical files from training rosters and log access requests. Owner dentist: approve authorized disclosures and ensure record transfer if the practice closes.

**Records to keep.** Medical contents include employee name, vaccination status/dates, examination/testing/follow-up results, limited opinion, and supplied healthcare-professional information. Training contents include dates, summary, trainer names/qualifications, and attendee names/job titles (29 CFR 1910.1030(h)(1)(ii), (h)(2)(i)).
