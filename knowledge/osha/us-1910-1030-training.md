# Bloodborne pathogen employee training

**Rule.** The employer must train occupationally exposed employees at no cost during working hours at initial assignment and annually thereafter. The employer must cover the required topics in understandable language and allow interactive questions with a knowledgeable trainer.

**Exact text.**
> "Annual training for all employees shall be provided within one year of their previous training." (29 CFR 1910.1030(g)(2)(iv)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Repeat within 1 year of previous training; add training when changed tasks or procedures affect exposure (29 CFR 1910.1030(g)(2)(iv)-(v)).

**Exceptions.**
- Additional change-related training may be limited to new exposures (29 CFR 1910.1030(g)(2)(v)).

**Office checklist.**
- Practice workflow: Amanda: schedule initial and annual sessions; retain topics, attendance, and trainer qualifications. Owner dentist: verify workplace-specific content includes exposure response, PPE, vaccination, and interactive questions.

**Records to keep.** Keep training dates, content or summary, trainer names/qualifications, and attendee names/job titles for 3 years from training (29 CFR 1910.1030(h)(2)).
