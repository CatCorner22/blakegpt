# Hazard communication information and training

**Rule.** The employer must provide effective chemical-hazard information and training at initial assignment and when a new untrained hazard enters the work area. The employer must cover detection, hazards, protection measures, labels, SDSs, and the workplace program.

**Exact text.**
> "Chemical-specific information must always be available through labels and safety data sheets." (29 CFR 1910.1200(h)(1)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Train at initial assignment and whenever a chemical hazard not previously trained about enters the work area (29 CFR 1910.1200(h)(1)).
- Federal update effective July 19, 2024; employer substance-related updates as necessary by November 20, 2026 and mixture-related updates as necessary by May 19, 2028 (29 CFR 1910.1200(j)(1), (2)(ii), (3)(ii)).

**Exceptions.**
- Training may address hazard categories or individual chemicals, but chemical-specific information must remain available through labels and SDSs (29 CFR 1910.1200(h)(1)). This federal paragraph does not itself require annual refresher training; Tennessee does.
- Federal transition permits the current text, the text revised as of July 1, 2023, or both until the applicable compliance dates (29 CFR 1910.1200(j)(4)). General coverage exclusions also appear in (b); do not assume all dental products are excluded.

**Office checklist.**
- Practice workflow: Amanda: arrange initial and new-hazard training and identify the program/SDS locations. Owner dentist: confirm coverage of actual workplace hazards and coordinate Tennessee annual refreshers.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this federal paragraph; Tennessee training retention is covered in tn-50-3-2001-hazcom-right-to-know.
