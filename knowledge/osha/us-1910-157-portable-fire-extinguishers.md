# Portable extinguishers and evacuation exemptions

**Rule.** The employer must inspect, maintain, and test workplace portable extinguishers unless a stated exemption applies. The employer must educate employees when extinguishers are provided for employee use and train designated users of firefighting equipment.

**Exact text.**
> "Portable extinguishers or hose used in lieu thereof under paragraph (d)(3) of this section shall be visually inspected monthly." (29 CFR 1910.157(e)(2)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Visually inspect monthly; perform an annual maintenance check (29 CFR 1910.157(e)(2)-(3)).
- Education: on initial employment and at least annually for employees provided extinguishers for use; designated equipment-user training: initial assignment and at least annually (29 CFR 1910.157(g)(1)-(4)).
- Applicable stored-pressure dry chemical units: maintenance every 6 years where the hydrostatic interval is 12 years; testing intervals otherwise depend on extinguisher type (29 CFR 1910.157(e)(4), (f)(2), Table L-1).

**Exceptions.**
- Full (b)(1) exemption requires an implemented written immediate-total-evacuation fire safety policy including compliant emergency-action and fire-prevention plans, no extinguishers available at work, and no specific standard requiring one. Where extinguishers remain but are not for employee use and compliant plans exist, only (e) and (f) apply. Designated-user plans under (b)(2) exempt distribution requirements only (29 CFR 1910.157(a)-(b)).

**Office checklist.**
- Practice workflow: Amanda: arrange monthly visual checks and annual maintenance; retain service evidence and schedule type-specific testing. Owner dentist: choose and sign off on employee-use versus evacuation policy and ensure corresponding education, training, and plans.

**Records to keep.** Record annual maintenance date; retain for 1 year after last entry or shell life, whichever is less. Keep hydrostatic certification (test date, tester signature, extinguisher identifier) until next required retest or removal from service, whichever occurs first (29 CFR 1910.157(e)(3), (f)(16)).
