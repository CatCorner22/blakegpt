## Reading the cards

- `verified` means the stated rules, numbers, deadlines, and quotations have support in the supplied source text, not that counsel approved the practice's implementation.
- `unverified` flags a specific source or scope gap in **Not verified**; it does not discard the supported rules in that card.
- Amanda is the reader role, not an identified person. Checklists assign suggested office work and sign-off owners; they do not claim that every assignment or signature is a statutory requirement.
- Recheck each card on the `review_days` schedule and whenever a relevant law, court order, or practice activity changes.
- The current eCFR text can retain vacated clauses. Use the court-status cards with the substantive cards.
- The 2026 Privacy final rule is in public inspection, scheduled for publication 2026-11-19. Its effective and compliance dates are expressed as offsets in the fetched text. The cards label the calendar dates as derived and keep them separate from the current eCFR rule.
- Tennessee statutes use Justia's 2025 unofficial mirror. The dental-record regulation uses Cornell LII's unofficial mirror; official SOS text was not obtained. The supplied checkpoint reports no amendments found in the fetched Public Chapter compilation search, not an official current-code certification.
- This README is an index, not a card, and intentionally has no `status` frontmatter.
