# Tennessee data breach notice and HIPAA exemption

**Rule.** A nonexempt information holder must notify affected Tennessee residents after a qualifying breach of computerized personal information. A holder subject to HIPAA as expanded by HITECH is exempt under (i)(2), so staff must not automatically apply this statute's 45-day deadline to a HIPAA-covered dental practice.

**Exact text.**
> "This section does not apply to any information holder that is subject to:" (Tenn. Code Ann. § 47-18-2107(i)).

> "The Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (42 U. S. C. § 1320d et seq.), as expanded by the Health Information Technology for Clinical and Economic Health Act (42 U. S. C. § 300jj et seq., and 42 U. S. C. § 17921 et seq.)." (Tenn. Code Ann. § 47-18-2107(i)(2)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- For nonexempt holders, resident notice is due no later than 45 days from discovery or notification; a maintainer must notify the owner/licensee within the same period (b)–(c).
- After a qualifying law-enforcement delay, notify within 45 days after the agency determines notice will not compromise the investigation (d).
- Personal information means name/initial plus surname with a Social Security number, driver license number, or qualifying financial-account/card credentials; breach covers unencrypted data or encrypted data with the key (a)(1), (4).
- Notice may be written or qualifying electronic notice. Substitute notice applies if cost exceeds $250,000, affected people exceed 500,000, or contact information is insufficient; it requires available email, website posting if maintained, and statewide media notice (e).
- If notifying more than 1,000 people at once under this statute, notify national consumer reporting agencies/credit bureaus without unreasonable delay (g).
- Source: Justia 2025 unofficial mirror. The supplied checkpoint reports no amendment found in the 2025–2026 Public Chapter compilations; that search is not an official consolidated-code certification.

**Exceptions.**
- HIPAA/HITECH-subject holders and qualifying federal financial-law holders are exempt (i)(1)–(2).
- Qualifying good-faith employee/agent acquisition without misuse/further unauthorized disclosure is excluded (a)(1)(B)).
- A compliant own-notice policy may satisfy notice requirements if it meets statutory timing (f).

**Office checklist.**
- At each incident, Amanda confirms and documents the holder's HIPAA status with the privacy official before choosing the notice law.
- If nonexempt, the privacy official approves the trigger, affected residents, deadline, and notice method; Amanda logs dispatch.
