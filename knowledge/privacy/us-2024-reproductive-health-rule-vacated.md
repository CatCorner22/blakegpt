# Reproductive health rule vacatur

**Rule.** A covered practice must distinguish vacated reproductive-health provisions from surviving HIPAA duties. HHS reports that the court vacated most of the reproductive-health rule and only notice clauses 164.520(b)(1)(ii)(F)–(H); staff must not treat those eCFR clauses as operative requirements.

**Exact text.**
> "The remaining modifications to the NPP requirements are undisturbed and remain in effect" (HHS reproductive-health guidance, opening Purl status paragraph).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- HHS identifies the order date as 2025-06-18 and the remaining notice compliance date as 2026-02-16 (opening status paragraph).
- Ordinary HIPAA disclosure limits still apply. The pending 2026 final rule is separate; see its status card.

**Exceptions.** 
- The decision did not remove all notice duties; surviving modifications remain in effect according to HHS.

**Office checklist.**
- At each notice revision or unusual reproductive-health request, Amanda uses the HHS status statement, not stale eCFR text alone.
- The privacy official approves the legal basis for any disclosure and the notice language.

**Records to keep.** Keep approved notice versions and privacy policies for 6 years under 164.520(e) and 164.530(j).
