# Assigned security official

**Rule.** A covered practice or business associate must identify a security official responsible for developing and implementing its Security Rule policies and procedures.

**Exact text.**
> "Identify the security official who is responsible for the development and implementation of the policies and procedures required by this subpart for the covered entity or business associate." (45 CFR 164.308(a)(2)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- The standard applies without a workforce-size exception (164.308(a)).
- No fixed appointment-renewal period appears in this source.

**Exceptions.** None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**
- At appointment and each change, Amanda records the official's role and contact route.
- The practice owner approves the assignment; the security official approves security policies.

**Records to keep.** Keep security policies and required documented actions/assessments for 6 years from creation or last effective date, whichever is later; make them available to implementers and update as needed (45 CFR 164.316(b)(1)–(2)).
