# Electronic access and audit controls

**Rule.** A covered practice or business associate must limit electronic-PHI system access to approved people or programs, assign unique user identities, and support emergency access. The entity must record and examine system activity and manage access rights.

**Exact text.**
> "Assign a unique name and/or number for identifying and tracking user identity." (45 CFR 164.312(a)(2)(i)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Unique user identification and emergency access procedures are required (164.312(a)(2)(i)–(ii)).
- Automatic logoff and encryption/decryption are addressable, not blanket optional controls (164.312(a)(2)(iii)–(iv); 164.306(d)(3)).
- Review system activity regularly; the source does not set a log-review frequency or a universal audit-log retention duration (164.308(a)(1)(ii)(D)).

**Exceptions.** 
- Addressable access authorization/establishment controls require reasonableness analysis and documented alternatives as applicable (164.308(a)(4)(ii)(B)–(C); 164.306(d)(3)).

**Office checklist.**
- For each hire, role change, or departure, Amanda routes access additions/removals for security-official approval and IT action.
- On the security official's documented review schedule, Amanda obtains audit-review results and escalates unexplained access.
- The security official approves emergency access and addressable-control decisions.

**Records to keep.** Keep security policies and required documented actions/assessments for 6 years from creation or last effective date, whichever is later; make them available to implementers and update as needed (45 CFR 164.316(b)(1)–(2)). This documentation period does not itself establish a universal raw-log retention period.
