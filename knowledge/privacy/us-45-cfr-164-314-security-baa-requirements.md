# Security terms in BA agreements

**Rule.** A covered practice or business associate must document the required electronic-PHI assurances in an agreement meeting the security contract rules. The contract must require Security Rule compliance, compliant subcontractor agreements, and security-incident reporting including unsecured-PHI breaches.

**Exact text.**
> "Report to the covered entity any security incident of which it becomes aware, including breaches of unsecured protected health information as required by § 164.410" (45 CFR 164.314(a)(2)(i)(C)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Assurances apply before permitting BA electronic-PHI handling; the BA obtains assurances from its subcontractor, rather than the practice contracting directly with each downstream subcontractor (164.308(b)).
- Pending 2026-23429: scheduled publication 2026-11-19; effective 60 days later (derived 2027-01-18), compliance 240 days later (derived 2027-07-17) (DATES p. 1).
- Revised 164.532(e) includes limited deemed compliance for qualifying prior security BA agreements until earlier qualifying renewal/modification or 2028-01-18; see the transition status card.

**Exceptions.** 
- Permitted government/other arrangements must satisfy 164.504(e)(3), not informal verbal assurances (164.314(a)(2)(ii)).

**Office checklist.**
- For each electronic-PHI vendor, Amanda checks that the agreement covers security incidents as well as breaches.
- The security official approves security terms before onboarding and at relevant renewal/change; the privacy official checks transition eligibility.

**Records to keep.** Keep security policies and required documented actions/assessments for 6 years from creation or last effective date, whichever is later; make them available to implementers and update as needed (45 CFR 164.316(b)(1)–(2)).
