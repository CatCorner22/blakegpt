# Breach notice to media

**Rule.** A covered practice must notify prominent media outlets serving a state or jurisdiction when a breach of unsecured PHI involves more than 500 residents there. Media notice does not replace individual notice.

**Exact text.**
> "more than 500 residents of a State or jurisdiction" (45 CFR 164.406(a)).

> "If the statement is in writing and specifies the time for which a delay is required, delay such notification, notice, or posting for the time period specified by the official" (45 CFR 164.412(a)).

> "If the statement is made orally, document the statement, including the identity of the official making the statement, and delay the notification, notice, or posting temporarily and no longer than 30 days from the date of the oral statement, unless a written statement as described in paragraph (a) of this section is submitted during that time." (45 CFR 164.412(b)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Trigger: more than 500 residents of the same state/jurisdiction, not 500 exactly (164.406(a)).
- Notify without unreasonable delay and no later than 60 calendar days after discovery; use individual-notice content requirements (b)–(c).

**Exceptions.** 
- A law-enforcement official must state that notice, notification, or posting would impede a criminal investigation or damage national security before the delay rule applies (164.412).
- For a written statement specifying the required delay, delay for the time specified by the official (164.412(a)).
- For an oral statement, document it, including the official\'s identity; delay temporarily for no longer than 30 days from the oral statement unless a qualifying written statement arrives during that time (164.412(b)).

**Office checklist.**
- For each law-enforcement delay, Amanda preserves the statement, official\'s identity, oral-statement date if applicable, and delay end date; the privacy official approves the grounds and tracks any timely written statement (164.412).
- At each breach, Amanda counts residents by state/jurisdiction, not just total patients.
- The privacy official approves the count, media outlets, content, and dispatch timing; Amanda preserves delivery proof.

**Records to keep.** Keep notification evidence or the documented no-breach basis for 6 years from creation or last effective date, whichever is later (45 CFR 164.414(b); 164.530(j)(1)(iv), (2)).
