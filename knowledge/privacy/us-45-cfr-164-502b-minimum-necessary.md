# Minimum necessary information

**Rule.** A covered practice and its business associates must make reasonable efforts to limit protected health information (PHI) to the minimum needed for the purpose. The practice must set role-based access and routine disclosure protocols and review nonroutine requests individually.

**Exact text.**
> "a covered entity or business associate must make reasonable efforts to limit protected health information to the minimum necessary to accomplish the intended purpose of the use, disclosure, or request." (45 CFR 164.502(b)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Apply the limit whenever using, disclosing, or requesting PHI unless an exception applies (164.502(b)).
- Use the entire record only when specifically justified as reasonably necessary (164.514(d)(5)).

**Exceptions.** 
- The limit does not apply to treatment disclosures to or requests by a health care provider, disclosures to the patient, valid authorizations, required HHS disclosures, disclosures required by law, or HIPAA compliance uses/disclosures (164.502(b)(2)).

**Office checklist.**
- For each new staff role, Amanda records needed access; the privacy official approves.
- For each nonroutine disclosure, Amanda routes the purpose and scope to the privacy official before release.

**Records to keep.** Keep required written policies and documentation for 6 years from creation or last effective date, whichever is later (45 CFR 164.530(j)(1)–(2)).
