# Family and friends involved in care

**Rule.** A covered practice may share only PHI directly relevant to a person's involvement in the patient's care or payment when the conditions in 164.510(b) apply. Staff may obtain oral agreement; family status alone does not create unlimited record access.

**Exact text.**
> "The covered entity may orally inform the individual of and obtain the individual's oral agreement or objection to a use or disclosure permitted by this section." (45 CFR 164.510 introductory text).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- When the patient is present and able to decide, obtain agreement, give a chance to object, or reasonably infer no objection using professional judgment (164.510(b)(2)).
- No fixed response period appears in 164.510(b).

**Exceptions.** 
- If the patient is absent or unable to agree because of incapacity or emergency, a professional may decide that limited sharing serves the patient's best interest (164.510(b)(3)).
- After death, relevant sharing with people involved before death may occur unless inconsistent with a known prior preference (164.510(b)(5)).

**Office checklist.**
- At each family/friend request, Amanda checks the patient's preference and limits the details shared.
- For incapacity or uncertainty, the treating dentist makes the professional-judgment decision before release.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
