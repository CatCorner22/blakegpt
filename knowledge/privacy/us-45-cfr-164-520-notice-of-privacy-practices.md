# Notice of privacy practices

**Rule.** A covered dental practice must give patients a plain-language privacy notice, post it at the office, and post it on its service website. Under the current eCFR rule, the practice must make a good-faith effort to obtain a written receipt acknowledgment outside emergency treatment. The pending final rule removes that effort and adds a new header, contact email, and right to discuss the notice.

**Exact text.**
> "The covered entity, including any covered entity receiving or maintaining records subject to 42 U.S.C. 290dd-2 , must provide a notice that is written in plain language and that contains the elements required by this paragraph." (45 CFR 164.520(b)(1)).

> "The notice must contain the name or title, telephone number, and email address for a designated person or office" (2026-23429, revised 45 CFR 164.520(b)(1)(vii)).

> "THIS NOTICE DESCRIBES HOW MEDICAL INFORMATION ABOUT YOU MAY BE USED AND DISCLOSED AND HOW YOU CAN GET ACCESS TO THIS INFORMATION. PLEASE REVIEW IT CAREFULLY." (current 45 CFR 164.520(b)(1)(i)).

> "HOW TO EXERCISE YOUR RIGHT TO GET COPIES OF YOUR RECORDS AT LIMITED COST OR, IN SOME CASES, FREE OF CHARGE." (2026-23429, revised 45 CFR 164.520(b)(1)(i), header excerpt).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Give the notice no later than first service; for emergency treatment, as soon as reasonably practicable afterward (164.520(c)(2)(i)).
- Describe permitted uses, rights, duties, complaints, contact, and effective date (164.520(b)(1)).
- Final rule 2026-23429 is scheduled for publication 2026-11-19; effective 60 days after publication (derived 2027-01-18), compliance 240 days after publication (derived 2027-07-17). These calendar dates are calculated, not quoted (final rule DATES, p. 1).
- The pending rule replaces the prominent header and retains first-service delivery and office/website availability; it removes acknowledgment records from revised (e) (amendment 9).
- The eCFR still displays vacated (b)(1)(ii)(F)–(H). Do not treat those reproductive-health provisions as operative; remaining notice changes had compliance date 2026-02-16 (HHS Purl status excerpt).

**Exceptions.** 
- Emergency treatment delays notice and excuses the acknowledgment effort, not later notice (164.520(c)(2)).

**Office checklist.**
- At first service, Amanda gives the current approved notice and logs receipt or failed acknowledgment effort.
- At each notice change, Amanda replaces posted and website copies; the privacy official signs off. Keep the current process until the pending rule's compliance change.
- Use the Purl status card to separate vacated language from surviving substance-use-record notice language.

**Records to keep.** Keep issued notices and current acknowledgment/effort records under 164.520(e); retain for 6 years under 164.530(j)(2). The pending revised (e) retains notice copies, not acknowledgment records.
