# Patient access to records

**Rule.** A covered practice must allow patient inspection and copies of PHI in designated record sets, subject to limited denial grounds. The current eCFR deadline is 30 days, but the Tennessee provider-copy rule requires 10 working days and is stricter today. The pending final rule requires action as soon as practicable within 15 calendar days and treats a legally required shorter period as practicable.

**Exact text.**
> "the covered entity must act on a request for access no later than 30 days after receipt of the request" (45 CFR 164.524(b)(2)(i)).

> "as soon as practicable, but no later than 15 calendar days after receipt of the request" (2026-23429, revised 45 CFR 164.524(b)(2)(i)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Current HIPAA: only 1 extension, no more than 30 days; send reasons and a completion date in writing within the original deadline (164.524(b)(2)(ii)). This does not extend Tennessee's separate deadline.
- Final rule 2026-23429 is scheduled for publication 2026-11-19; effective 60 days after publication (derived 2027-01-18), compliance 240 days after publication (derived 2027-07-17). These calendar dates are calculated, not quoted (final rule DATES, p. 1).
- Pending HIPAA: only 1 extension of up to 30 calendar days with written reasons/date before the original deadline; shorter state/federal access periods are deemed practicable (revised (b)(2)(ii)–(iii)).
- Tennessee: furnish requested copies within 10 working days of written request under 63-2-101(a)(1); count actual working days, not a presumed calendar equivalent.
- Current eCFR (c)(3)(ii) prints a broad signed-written third-party direction rule. HHS access guidance warns that Ciox vacated provisions are rescinded; do not rely on that text alone for disputed third-party demands.
- Pending (d) expressly provides a right to direct an electronic copy of PHI in an electronic health record to a designated person; clear, conspicuous, specific requests may be electronic or paper; the 15-calendar-day/1-extension framework also applies (d)(1), (5).
- Provide the requested readily producible form/format; otherwise agree on the permitted alternative (current (c)(2)).

**Exceptions.** 
- Psychotherapy notes and litigation-preparation material are excluded; other denial grounds are limited (164.524(a)(1)–(3)).
- A reviewable denial requires an independent licensed professional review on request; send a plain-language written denial with complaint/review rights (164.524(a)(4), (d)).

**Office checklist.**
- At every request, Amanda logs receipt and the Tennessee due date, checks identity/authority, and routes any denial to the privacy official and clinician.
- The privacy official approves written extensions and denials. Amanda tracks delivery, not just acknowledgment.
- Before the pending compliance date, the privacy official updates request forms and third-party direction procedures.

**Records to keep.** Keep designated record-set descriptions and responsible office titles (164.524(e)) for 6 years under 164.530(j). Pending (f) also requires electronic-health-record descriptions for access-to-direct.
