# Patient copy fees and posted fee schedules

**Rule.** A covered practice may charge only a reasonable cost-based fee for a patient's HIPAA copy request within the allowed cost categories. The practice must not treat Tennessee's fee ceiling as permission to exceed HIPAA. The pending final rule narrows supply/postage categories, expressly bars inspection and internet-access fees, and requires fee-schedule availability.

**Exact text.**
> "the covered entity may impose a reasonable, cost-based fee" (45 CFR 164.524(c)(4)).

> "the covered entity must post a fee schedule on its website, if it has one, and make the fee schedule available to individuals at the point of service and upon request." (2026-23429, new 45 CFR 164.525).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Current categories: copy labor, paper supplies or requested portable electronic media, requested mailing postage, and an agreed summary/explanation (164.524(c)(4)(i)–(iv)).
- Final rule 2026-23429 is scheduled for publication 2026-11-19; effective 60 days after publication (derived 2027-01-18), compliance 240 days after publication (derived 2027-07-17). These calendar dates are calculated, not quoted (final rule DATES, p. 1).
- Pending (c)(4)(i): copy labor, non-electronic-copy supplies, requested non-electronic-copy/summary/explanation postage, and agreed summary/explanation labor only.
- Pending (c)(4)(ii): no fee for inspection or internet-based access to electronic PHI, such as through a personal health application.
- Pending 164.525: if charging fees, list free access and standard copy fees for available electronic/non-electronic formats under access and authorization; post online if a website exists, at point of service, and on request.

**Exceptions.** 
- A summary/explanation requires advance agreement to the summary and its fee (current 164.524(c)(2)(iii); pending (c)(2)(iv)).
- Third-party requests require separate analysis; do not automatically apply patient-access fee rules to every authorization.

**Office checklist.**
- For each patient copy bill, Amanda records allowed actual costs and checks the Tennessee ceiling; the privacy official signs off on the fee method.
- Before pending compliance, Amanda prepares a complete fee schedule for privacy-official approval and replaces it whenever approved rates change.

**Records to keep.** Keep written fee policies for 6 years under 164.530(j); this source gives no separate invoice-retention period.
