# Patient requests to amend records

**Rule.** A covered practice must allow patients to request amendment of PHI in designated record sets for as long as it keeps the information there. The practice must accept and link an approved amendment or give a written denial with disagreement and complaint rights.

**Exact text.**
> "The covered entity must act on the individual's request for an amendment no later than 60 days after receipt of such a request" (45 CFR 164.526(b)(2)(i)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Act within 60 days after receipt; only 1 extension up to 30 days with written reasons and completion date within the original period (164.526(b)(2)).
- The practice may require writing and a supporting reason only after advance notice (164.526(b)(1)).

**Exceptions.** 
- Denial may apply to information not created by the practice (unless the originator is unavailable), outside the designated record set, excluded from access, or accurate and complete (a)(2).

**Office checklist.**
- At each request, Amanda logs receipt and deadlines and routes the clinical issue to the dentist.
- The privacy official signs the response; Amanda links accepted amendments and informs agreed recipients, or handles disagreement/rebuttal records under (c)–(d).

**Records to keep.** Keep required request/response documentation and responsible office titles under 164.526(f) and 164.530(j) for 6 years.
