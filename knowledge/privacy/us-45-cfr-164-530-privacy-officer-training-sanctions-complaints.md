# Privacy official training complaints and sanctions

**Rule.** A covered practice must name a privacy official and complaint contact, train its workforce, maintain privacy policies, and provide a complaint process. The practice must apply appropriate sanctions, mitigate known harmful effects when practicable, and must not retaliate against people exercising privacy rights.

**Exact text.**
> "A covered entity must designate a privacy official who is responsible for the development and implementation of the policies and procedures of the entity." (45 CFR 164.530(a)(1)(i)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Train new staff within a reasonable period after joining and affected staff within a reasonable period after material policy changes (164.530(b)(2)).
- The source does not impose a fixed annual privacy-training interval.
- Promptly document and implement policy changes needed for changes in law (164.530(i)(3)).

**Exceptions.** 
- Sanctions exclude qualifying whistleblower and protected participation conduct described in 164.530(e)(1).

**Office checklist.**
- At hire and each material policy change, Amanda schedules role-specific training; the privacy official signs the training record.
- For each complaint or violation, Amanda records disposition and routes investigation, sanctions, and mitigation to the privacy official.
- When law or practice changes, the privacy official approves revised policies before implementation.

**Records to keep.** Keep official/contact designations, training, complaints/dispositions, applied sanctions, and written policies for 6 years from creation or last effective date, whichever is later (164.530(a)(2), (b)(2)(ii), (d)(2), (e)(2), (j)).
