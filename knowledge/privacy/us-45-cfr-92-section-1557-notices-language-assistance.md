# Section 1557 notices and language assistance

**Rule.** A practice receiving qualifying HHS federal financial assistance must provide required nondiscrimination and language-assistance notices, written civil-rights procedures, and meaningful language access. A covered practice with 15 or more employees must designate a Section 1557 coordinator and adopt written grievance procedures. Staff must distinguish this coverage from HIPAA and apply the court-status limits in the related card.

**Exact text.**
> "A covered entity must provide a notice of nondiscrimination to participants, beneficiaries, enrollees, and applicants of its health programs and activities, and members of the public." (45 CFR 92.10(a)).

> "no less than three (3) calendar years." (45 CFR 92.9(c)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Coverage: any part of a health program/activity receiving HHS assistance directly or indirectly; Medicaid participation is an example to screen as an HHS-assisted program, not a 15-employee exemption (92.2(a)(1), 92.4 Federal financial assistance).
- At 15 or more employees: designate/authorize at least 1 employee coordinator and implement written prompt/equitable grievance procedures (92.7(a), 92.8(c)(1)).
- All covered practices: maintain dated written nondiscrimination, language-access, effective-communication, and reasonable-modification procedures and revise as needed (92.8(a), (b), (d)–(h)).
- Nondiscrimination notice: provide annually and on request; post conspicuously online if a site exists and in prominent physical locations in at least 20-point sans serif font (92.10(a)(2)). Include assistance/modifications, how to obtain them, applicable coordinator/grievance contacts, and OCR complaint details (a)(1)).
- Availability notice: English plus at least the 15 most common limited-English-proficiency languages of the relevant state(s), with disability-accessible alternate formats; annually, on request, website if any, physical locations with at least 20-point sans serif font, and listed documents including the privacy notice, intake forms, consents, bills, and complaints (92.11(b)–(c)).
- Language help must be free, accurate, timely, and protect privacy and independent decisions. Offer qualified interpreters/use qualified translators when required; critical/complex machine translations require qualified human review (92.201(b)–(c)).
- Training: initial deadline as soon as possible, within 30 days after policy implementation and no later than 300 days after 2024-07-05; later new/affected employees within a reasonable period after hire/material change (92.9(b)). These initial periods have already elapsed by the verification date.
- The 2026 vacatur notice leaves the other provisions in force and limits vacatur of 92.8(b)(1) and 92.10(a)(1)(i) to the gender-identity expansion; do not discard the entire notice/policy duties (91 FR 32887–32888).

**Exceptions.**
- 92.11(d) allows individualized annual opt-out with documented safeguards and no assumed opt-out from silence, or documented primary-language/aid approaches meeting its conditions.
- 92.201(e) narrowly limits reliance on unqualified adults/children; emergency temporary help and specific documented adult requests have conditions. Do not require the patient to supply or pay for an interpreter.
- A patient need not accept language assistance (92.201(h)).

**Office checklist.**
- At onboarding and each payer/program change, Amanda documents federal-assistance coverage; the practice owner approves the coverage decision.
- Annually and on each form/website change, Amanda checks required notices and placements; the coordinator or practice owner signs off.
- At each language need, Amanda obtains qualified help and records the approved route without charging the patient.
- At hire or policy change, Amanda schedules relevant-employee training; the coordinator or practice owner approves completion records.

**Records to keep.** Keep contemporaneous training-completion records for at least 3 calendar years (92.9(c)). If the 15-employee grievance rule applies, retain grievances and required details for at least 3 calendar years from resolution and protect identity as required (92.8(c)(2)–(3)).
