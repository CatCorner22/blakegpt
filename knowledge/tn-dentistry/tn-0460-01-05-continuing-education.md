# Continuing education hours for dentists, hygienists and registered dental assistants

**Rule.** Each 2-calendar-year cycle (January 1 of an odd year through December 31 of the next even year), a dentist must complete 40 hours, a hygienist 30 hours, and a registered dental assistant 24 hours of Board-approved CE, each including at least 2 hours of chemical dependency education. Each must keep the course certificates for 3 years from the end of the calendar year of the course.

**Exact text.**

> "each licensed dentist must successfully complete forty (40) hours of continuing education in courses approved by the Board during the two (2) calendar years (January 1st of an odd-numbered year through December 31st of the subsequent even-numbered year) that precede the licensure renewal year. At least two (2) hours of the forty (40) hour requirement shall pertain to chemical dependency education." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.05(1)(a)).

> "each licensed dental hygienist must successfully complete thirty (30) hours of continuing education" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.05(1)(b)).

> "each registered dental assistant must successfully complete twenty-four (24) hours of continuing education in courses approved by the Board" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.05(1)(c)).

> "This documentation must be retained for a period of three (3) years from the end of the calendar year in which the course is completed." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.05(2)(c)).

> "A maximum of ten (10) continuing education credits may be earned in this category" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.05(3)(d)3.).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Dentist 40 hours; hygienist 30 hours; registered assistant 24 hours; at least 2 hours chemical dependency for each (0460-01-.05(1)(a)-(c)).
- Sedation or anesthesia permit holders: at least 4 of the 40 hours in anesthesia or sedation (0460-01-.05(1)(a)).
- Cycle: January 1 odd year to December 31 next even year; due December 31 of the cycle (0460-01-.05(2)(a)).
- New licensees and registrants are exempt during their initial 2-calendar-year cycle (0460-01-.05(1)(d)).
- Course certificates kept 3 years from the end of the calendar year of completion (0460-01-.05(2)(c)).
- Individual course approval: evaluation form due within 30 days after the course (0460-01-.05(3)(b)2.).
- Dental meeting attendance: 5 hours each, maximum 10 per cycle (0460-01-.05(3)(d)3.).
- 3 semester hours = 15 CE hours (0460-01-.05(3)(d)2.).

**Exceptions.**

- Waiver when the failure was beyond the person's control or physical capabilities (0460-01-.05(5)(a)).
- Hours earned under a disciplinary order do not count (0460-01-.05(7)(c)).

**Office checklist.**

- Keep a CE tracker per clinician with hours, chemical dependency hours and certificate copies.
- Midway through the final year of each cycle, send each clinician a shortfall notice for the cycle ending December 31 (office practice).
- The owner dentist reviews the tracker twice a year (office practice).

**Records to keep.**

Each dentist, hygienist and registered dental assistant must keep CE certificates (name, course title, date, hours, sponsor) for 3 years from the end of the calendar year of completion (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.05(2)(c)-(d)). The office may keep copies; the duty rests on the practitioner.
