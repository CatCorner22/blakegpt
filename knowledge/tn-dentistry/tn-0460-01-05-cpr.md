# CPR training for dentists, hygienists and registered dental assistants

**Rule.** Each dentist, hygienist and dental assistant must hold current CPR training from an in-person course with a manikin skills exam and must attest to it at each biennial renewal. Each must keep proof for 3 years from the end of the calendar year of training.

**Exact text.**

> "which indicates current training in cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) which is defined as successful completion of a BLS for Healthcare Providers, or CPR/AED for Professional Rescuers, or an equivalent course, which provides training for healthcare professionals in CPR and the use of an AED. The course must be conducted in person and include a skills examination on a manikin with a certified instructor." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.05(4)(a)).

> "Each dentist, dental hygienist and registered dental assistant must retain independent documentation of CPR training for a period of three (3) years from the end of the calendar year in which the training is received." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.05(4)(c)).

> "A dentist may not perform dental procedures if CPR training is waived unless another dentist, a dental hygienist or dental assistant currently trained in CPR is present within the confines of the dental office." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.05(5)(d)).

> "A dentist using antianxiety premedication must employ auxiliary personnel who are certified in BLS for Healthcare Providers, or CPR/AED for Professional Rescuers, or an equivalent course" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.07(5)(b)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- CPR attestation at each biennial renewal (0460-01-.05(4)(a)).
- Proof kept 3 years from the end of the calendar year of training (0460-01-.05(4)(c)).
- CPR hours may count as CE (0460-01-.05(4)(b)).
- Approved providers: American Red Cross, American Heart Association, hospital programs, or Board-approved courses (0460-01-.05(4)(d)).

**Exceptions.**

- Board waiver on an individual basis (0460-01-.05(5)).

**Office checklist.**

- Keep a CPR roster with card expiration dates for every clinician; check monthly.
- Book an in-person BLS class well before any card expires (office practice).
- If the office uses antianxiety premedication, confirm every auxiliary on duty holds current BLS-level certification; the dentist signs quarterly.

**Records to keep.**

Each dentist, hygienist and registered dental assistant must keep independent CPR documentation for 3 years from the end of the calendar year of training (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.05(4)(c)).
