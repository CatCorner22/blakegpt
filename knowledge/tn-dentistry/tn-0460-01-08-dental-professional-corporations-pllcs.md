# Dental professional corporations (D.P.C.) and dental PLLCs (D.P.L.L.C.)

**Rule.** Only Tennessee-licensed dentists, or entities wholly owned by them, may own shares of a D.P.C. or be members of a D.P.L.L.C. All officers, directors and governors must be eligible owners, except the secretary, assistant secretary, treasurer and assistant treasurer of a D.P.C. and the secretary and treasurer of a D.P.L.L.C. Any licensed health professional may be an employee or contractor of either.

**Exact text.**

> "Only the following may form and own shares of stock in a foreign or domestic D.P.C. doing business in Tennessee:" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.08(1)(b)).

> "Dentists licensed pursuant to Tennessee Code Annotated Title 63, Chapter 5; and/or" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.08(1)(b)1.).

> "Nothing in these rules shall be construed as prohibiting any health care professional licensed pursuant to Tennessee Code Annotated, Title 63 from being an employee of or a contractor to a D.P.C." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.08(1)(d)2.).

> "must, within sixty (60) days after service of the notice of violation, correct each alleged violation or show to the Board's satisfaction that the alleged violation(s) did not occur" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.08(3)(c)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Cure period after a Board notice of violation: 60 days (0460-01-.08(3)(c)).
- D.P.L.L.C.s formed in Tennessee to serve only other states: annual notarized statement to the Board (0460-01-.08(2)(d)5.).
- A D.P.C. need not file its charter or annual statement with the Board (0460-01-.08(1)(a)).

**Exceptions.**

- Non-owner officer roles allowed: D.P.C. secretary, assistant secretary, treasurer, assistant treasurer (0460-01-.08(1)(c)1.); D.P.L.L.C. secretary and treasurer (0460-01-.08(2)(c)1.).

**Office checklist.**

- Keep the charter or articles and the current owner, officer and director list in the compliance binder.
- Check that any non-dentist officer holds only an allowed role; the owner dentist and counsel confirm each year.
- Practice only in the areas of practice stated in the charter or articles (0460-01-.08(1)(d)1., (2)(d)1.).
