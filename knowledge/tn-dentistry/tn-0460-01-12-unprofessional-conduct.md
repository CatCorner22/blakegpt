# Unprofessional conduct: acts the Board can discipline

**Rule.** A licensee must not pay or take referral fees, delegate to an unqualified person, falsify records, practice with an expired registration, or let staff violate the Dental Practice Act. A licensee must report suspected child abuse. No person, including a manager, may set maximum procedure times or otherwise interfere with a dentist's professional judgment.

**Exact text.**

> "Directly or indirectly offering, giving, soliciting, or receiving or agreeing to receive, any fee or other consideration to or from a third party for the referral of a patient or client" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.12(2)).

> "Delegating professional responsibilities to a person when the licensee delegating such responsibilities knows or has reason to know that such person is not qualified, by training, by experience or by licensure, to perform them." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.12(7)).

> "Failing to report suspected child abuse to the proper authorities, as required by T.C.A. § 37- 1-403(a)(2)." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.12(11)).

> "Practicing with an expired, retired, suspended or revoked license, permit, or registration." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.12(18)).

> "Failing to exercise reasonable diligence to prevent partners, associates, and employees from engaging in conduct which would violate any provisions of the Tennessee Dental Practice Act" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.12(24)).

> "Setting a maximum or other standardized time for the performance of specific dental procedures." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.12(27)(a)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Controlled-substance prescriptions need a dentist/patient relationship: treatment "on at least one (1) occasion within the preceding year" or documented history (0460-01-.12(21)).
- Patient termination notice: see tn-0460-01-12-26-patient-termination-notice (14 days).

**Exceptions.**

- Emergency exception for performing without adequate supervision "where a person's life or health is in danger" (0460-01-.12(6)).
- Disclosure of patient information "as authorized or required by law" (0460-01-.12(5)).

**Office checklist.**

- Do not set production quotas framed as maximum procedure times; route scheduling templates to the dentist for approval.
- Never pre-sign or stamp blank prescription forms (0460-01-.12(23)).
- Train staff each year on child-abuse reporting; log the training.
- Answer Department of Health written requests on time (0460-01-.12(12)); the owner dentist signs each response.

**Records to keep.**

No retention rule in this source. Rule 0460-01-.12(13) bars destroying records "in contemplation of an investigation by the Board or a lawsuit".
