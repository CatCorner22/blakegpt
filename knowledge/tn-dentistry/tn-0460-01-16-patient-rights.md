# Patient rights in the dental office

**Rule.** The office must give every patient at least the 9 rights in Rule 0460-01-.16, including privacy, confidential records, information about diagnosis and options, the right to refuse treatment, a copy or summary of the record, pain management and freedom from abuse. If abuse occurs, the dentist must notify Adult Protective Services or the Department of Children's Services immediately.

**Exact text.**

> "Each patient shall, at a minimum, be afforded the following rights:" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.16).

> "To refuse any diagnostic procedure or treatment and be advised of the consequences of that refusal." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.16(6)).

> "To obtain a copy or summary of their personal dental record, pursuant to T.C.A. §§ 63-2-101, et seq." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.16(7)).

> "Should this right be violated, the dentist must notify the Tennessee Department of Human Services, Adult Protective Services or Tennessee Department of Children's Services immediately as required by law." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.16(9)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- 9 minimum rights (0460-01-.16(1)-(9)).
- Abuse report: immediately (0460-01-.16(9)).

**Exceptions.**

- None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- Post or hand out a patient-rights notice listing the 9 rights (office practice; the rule does not require posting).
- Document informed refusal in the chart with the consequences explained.
- Each year, review the abuse-reporting contacts with staff; the owner dentist signs.

**Records to keep.**

No retention rule in this source.
