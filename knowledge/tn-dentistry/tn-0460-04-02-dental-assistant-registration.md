# Dental assistant registration: who needs it and what the application requires

**Rule.** A dental assistant must hold a Board registration before practicing beyond the practical dental assistant scope. The applicant must be at least 18 years old, hold a high school diploma or GED, pass a criminal background check, hold current in-person CPR training, and pass the Board Ethics and Jurisprudence examination.

**Exact text.**

> "To practice as a dental assistant beyond the scope of a practical dental assistant a person must possess a lawfully issued registration from the Board." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.02).

> "proof of having attained at least eighteen (18) years of age" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.02(2)(b)).

> "a signed "passport" style photograph taken within the preceding twelve (12) months" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.02(2)(c)).

> "at least two (2) letters attesting to the applicant's character from dental professionals on the signator's letterhead" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.02(3)).

> "The course must be conducted in person and include a skills examination on a manikin with a certified instructor." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.02(9)).

> "An applicant must successfully complete the Tennessee Board of Dentistry Ethics and Jurisprudence examination." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.02(10)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Minimum age: 18 years (0460-04-.02(2)(b)).
- Photo taken within the preceding 12 months (0460-04-.02(2)(c)).
- At least 2 character letters from dental professionals (0460-04-.02(3)).
- Criminal background check sent directly from the Board's vendor (0460-04-.02(7)).
- CPR: BLS for Healthcare Providers, CPR/AED for Professional Rescuers, or equivalent, in person with a manikin skills exam (0460-04-.02(9)).
- Proof of U.S. or Canadian citizenship or legal entitlement to live in the United States (0460-04-.02(4)).

**Exceptions.**

- None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- Before an assistant performs any registered-level duty, look up the registration on the Board license verification site and print or save the result.
- Keep a list of registration expiration dates; review it monthly.
- The owner dentist signs off before an assistant moves from practical to registered duties.

**Records to keep.**

No retention rule in this source. Keep the verification printout with the personnel file (office practice, not a rule).
