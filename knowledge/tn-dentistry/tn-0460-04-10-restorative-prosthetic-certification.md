# Expanded restorative and prosthetic function certifications for registered dental assistants

**Rule.** A registered dental assistant must hold the Board restorative or prosthetic certification before performing those functions, and may perform them only under direct supervision and full responsibility of a dentist. No assistant may diagnose, cut tooth or tissue, remove caries, deliver or adjust appliances, or use a high-speed handpiece intraorally.

**Exact text.**

> "Dental assistants who have a minimum of two (2) years continuous full-time employment within the past three (3) years in a dental practice as a dental assistant are eligible for admission to a Board-approved certification course in restorative and/or prosthetic functions." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.10(1)).

> "shall prominently display their current renewal certificate at their place of employment" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.10(3)).

> "may perform restorations or prosthetic functions only under the direct supervision and full responsibility of a licensed dentist" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.10(4)).

> "Establishing vertical dimension of occlusion and interocclusal records;" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.10(5)(b)2.).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Course eligibility: at least 2 years continuous full-time employment within the past 3 years (0460-04-.10(1)).
- Certification lapses with the registration (0460-04-.10(2)).

**Exceptions.**

- Out-of-state equivalent course (0460-04-.10(6)).

**Office checklist.**

- Post each certified assistant's current renewal certificate.
- Before enrolling an assistant in a course, confirm 2 of the last 3 years were full-time assisting; keep the payroll proof.
