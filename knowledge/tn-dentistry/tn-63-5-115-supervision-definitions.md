# Supervision levels: direct and general supervision

**Rule.** An assistant must work only under direct supervision: a supervising dentist must be continuously present within the physical confines of the office. A hygienist may work under general supervision (dentist absent) only when the T.C.A. § 63-5-108(c)(5) conditions are met, and a dentist must not allow more than 3 hygienists to work under general supervision at 1 time.

**Exact text.**

> ""direct supervision" means the continuous presence of a supervising dentist within the physical confines of the dental office when licensed and registered dental hygienists or registered dental assistants perform lawfully assigned duties and functions" (T.C.A. § 63-5-115(b)(1) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

> ""general supervision" is defined as those instances when the dentist is not present in the dental office or treatment facility while procedures are being performed by the dental hygienist, but the dentist has personally diagnosed the condition to be treated, has personally authorized the procedures being performed and will evaluate the performance of the dental hygienist" (T.C.A. § 63-5-115(b)(2) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

> "Under general supervision a dental hygienist may provide to patients, for not more than fifteen (15) consecutive business days, all other dental hygiene services not otherwise limited to direct supervision" (T.C.A. § 63-5-108(c)(5) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

> "The supervising dentist examined the patient not more than eleven (11) months prior to the date the dental hygienist provides the dental hygiene services" (T.C.A. § 63-5-108(c)(5)(C) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

> "a licensed dentist shall not allow, under general supervision, more than three (3) dental hygienists to work at any one (1) time" (T.C.A. § 63-5-108(f)(1) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Assistants: direct supervision only (T.C.A. § 63-5-115(a)).
- Hygienist general supervision: at most 15 consecutive business days (T.C.A. § 63-5-108(c)(5)).
- Hygienist needs at least 1 year full-time experience (T.C.A. § 63-5-108(c)(5)(A)).
- Patient examined by the dentist within the prior 11 months (T.C.A. § 63-5-108(c)(5)(C)).
- Patient told in advance the dentist will be absent (T.C.A. § 63-5-108(c)(5)(E)).
- Maximum 3 hygienists under general supervision at once (T.C.A. § 63-5-108(f)(1), as amended by 2024 Pub. Ch. 561).

**Exceptions.**

- T.C.A. § 63-5-108(f)(2): up to 10 hygienists under direct supervision at a nonprofit free mobile clinic.
- T.C.A. § 63-5-108(f)(3): up to 5 hygienists under general supervision for a health department or safety-net entity.
- T.C.A. § 63-5-115(c): unsupervised preventive education to organized groups.

**Office checklist.**

- When no dentist is on site, send every assistant home or to non-clinical work; log dentist-absent days.
- Before a dentist-absent hygiene day, confirm the written emergency protocol, the written treatment plan, the 11-month exam date and the advance patient notice; the dentist signs the day sheet.
