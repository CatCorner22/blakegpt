## Scope gaps

- [`tn-accurate-recruiting`](tn-accurate-recruiting.md) — A separate Tennessee service-letter law for private dental practices could not be confirmed. The supplied T.C.A. § 50-1-102 is a relocation recruiting law.
- [`tn-donation-leave`](tn-donation-leave.md) — A generally applicable Tennessee private-employer blood or bone marrow donation leave entitlement could not be confirmed. The state employee manual and 2026 living-organ law address specified state employment.
- [`tn-drug-free-workplace`](tn-drug-free-workplace.md) — The Bureau posted a November 10 rulemaking hearing notice. A final effective rule adding kratom to the workplace program could not be confirmed in the promulgated rules reviewed.
- [`tn-provider-noncompetes`](tn-provider-noncompetes.md) — No Tennessee appellate decision was found resolving the interaction between the 2026 employee compensation floor and the healthcare-provider statute, or adopting the federal trial court’s geographic interpretation.
