# Accurate recruiting and service-letter citation

**Rule.** A recruiter must not induce a worker to relocate within or into Tennessee by misrepresenting work, pay, conditions, or labor disputes. A recruiter must disclose an existing strike, lockout, or other labor trouble in an advertisement, proposal, or contract covered by the statute.

**Exact text.**

> “false or deceptive representations, false advertising or false pretenses” (T.C.A. § 50-1-102(a)(1) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “workers are brought into this state, or induced to go from one place to another in this state” (T.C.A. § 50-1-102(e) — unofficial Justia mirror)

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- The recruiting rule applies when a worker is induced to relocate within or into the state under the statutory conditions. T.C.A. § 50-1-102 has no service-letter requirement or service-letter deadline.

**Exceptions.**

- Use the unemployment separation notice rule for departures that trigger that notice.

**Office checklist.**

- Before distributing a recruiting ad or relocation offer, have the owner sign off on actual duties, compensation, and conditions.
- At separation, have the payroll lead use the state separation-notice checklist.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
