# Age discrimination in Tennessee employment

**Rule.** A covered employer must not use age to deny employment opportunities or change employment terms for a person aged 40 or older, subject to the statutory exceptions.

**Exact text.**

> “at least forty (40) years of age” (T.C.A. § 4-21-407(b) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “reasonable factors other than age” (T.C.A. § 4-21-407(a)(1) — unofficial Justia mirror)

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- The Tennessee employment protections start at age 40 and employer coverage starts at 8 individuals in Tennessee (T.C.A. §§ 4-21-102(8), 4-21-401(a), 4-21-407(b)).

**Exceptions.**

- A bona fide occupational qualification reasonably necessary to normal operations or a reasonable factor other than age can apply (T.C.A. § 4-21-407(a)(1)).
- A bona fide seniority system or benefit plan cannot be a subterfuge or excuse a failure to hire. A limited executive retirement exception requires age 65, a bona fide executive or high policymaking role for the preceding 2 years, and at least $44,000 in qualifying immediate nonforfeitable annual retirement benefits (subsections (a)(2), (c)–(d)). Subsection (e) addresses certain public-safety hires outside dental practice.

**Office checklist.**

- Review every job post and screening rubric for age-based requirements. Have the owner sign off on case-specific job criteria before making an adverse decision.
