# Tennessee Drug-Free Workplace Program participation

**Rule.** A participating employer must apply, renew participation annually, and give the required notice before implementation. A participating employer must follow the statutory testing triggers and protect the confidentiality of testing records.

**Exact text.**

> “shall renew the application each year” (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-02-12-.13(1) — Cornell LII mirror)

> “at least sixty (60) days elapse” (T.C.A. § 50-9-105(b) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “after a conditional offer of employment” (T.C.A. § 50-9-106(a)(1) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “at least five percent (5%)” (T.C.A. § 50-6-418(a)(1) — unofficial Justia mirror)

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Renew every year. Filing a complete application creates a rebuttable entitlement presumption for the next 12 consecutive months (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-02-12-.13(1)).
- Allow at least 60 days after the general employee notice before starting. Post the policy notice, make copies available, and place notice on vacancy announcements for positions requiring testing (T.C.A. § 50-9-105(b)–(c)).
- Trigger testing after conditional offers, on reasonable suspicion, under applicable routine fitness-for-duty policies, for qualifying follow-up, and after accidents that trigger the rule. Write the controlled-substance reasonable-suspicion observations by 24 hours after observation or release of results, whichever is earlier. Required follow-up testing occurs at least annually for 2 years after program completion without advance notice (T.C.A. § 50-9-106(a)(1)–(5)).
- The premium credit is at least 5% unless the commissioner finds that rate actuarially unsound (T.C.A. § 50-6-418(a)(1)).
- Effective July 1, 2026, Public Chapter 950 directs the commissioner to adopt a rule adding kratom to the statutory drug definition. A Bureau notice identified a November 10 rulemaking hearing (2026 Tenn. Pub. Acts ch. 950, §§ 6, 8; Bureau rulemaking notice, agency guidance).

**Exceptions.**

- Applicant alcohol testing is optional. Follow-up is optional for voluntary participation in an assistance or rehabilitation program. The routine-testing provision excludes specified health, wellness, and limited surveillance screenings (T.C.A. § 50-9-106(a)(1), (3)(B), (4)).
- Rescission uses a dated posting and 60 days’ written notice to the insurer, with a 60-day minimum before it takes effect for workers and applicants (T.C.A. § 50-9-105(d)).

**Office checklist.**

- Before enrollment or renewal, have the owner approve the written policy, notice, testing vendor, and application. At each testing trigger, have the designated administrator document the basis and restrict access to results.

**Records to keep.** Keep original reasonable-suspicion documentation at least 1 year and protect the confidentiality of testing information (T.C.A. §§ 50-9-106(a)(2), 50-9-109).
