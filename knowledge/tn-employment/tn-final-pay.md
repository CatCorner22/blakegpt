# Final paycheck deadlines and owed leave

**Rule.** An employer must pay final wages by the later of the next regular payday or 21 days after separation, subject to the piece-work or commission exception. Where the private-employment payday rule applies, the employer must include vacation pay or other compensatory time owed under company policy or a labor agreement.

**Exact text.**

> “whichever occurs last” (2026 Tenn. Pub. Acts ch. 617, § 2)

> “by the last day of the succeeding month following the date of discharge or voluntary leaving” (2026 Tenn. Pub. Acts ch. 617, § 2)

> “owed to the employee by virtue of company policy or labor agreement” (T.C.A. § 50-2-103(a)(4) — unofficial Justia mirror)

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- For resignation or discharge, calculate the next regular payday and 21 days after separation. Use the later deadline for ordinary wages (T.C.A. § 50-2-103(g), as amended).
- The subsection (a)(4) vacation and compensatory-time rule uses the subsection (b) definition of private employment, which starts at 5 employees.
- Public Chapter 617 applies to compensation earned on or after July 1, 2026 (§ 3).

**Exceptions.**

- For a worker employed on a piece-work or commission basis, the amended statute sets the deadline for that compensation at the last day of the month after separation (2026 Tenn. Pub. Acts ch. 617, § 2).
- Subsection (a)(4) does not require the office to offer paid or unpaid vacation.

**Office checklist.**

- At each separation, calculate each deadline, verify accrued amounts against policy or agreement, and send the calculation to the payroll lead for sign-off.
- Send the unemployment notice on its own deadline.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
