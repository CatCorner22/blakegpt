# Paid sick leave in a private dental practice

**Rule.** A Tennessee private practice may set paid sick leave by policy or agreement, subject to applicable leave and accommodation laws.

**Exact text.**

> “The State of Tennessee does not have a law that regulates fringe benefits.” (Tennessee Labor and Workforce Development, fringe benefits FAQ, answer (agency guidance))

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Tennessee Labor and Workforce Development confirms in its fringe benefits FAQ that Tennessee has no general private-employer paid sick leave requirement.

**Exceptions.**

- Review applicable federal family and medical leave, state and federal accommodation duties, and any paid-leave terms the office adopted (29 U.S.C. §§ 2611, 2612).

**Office checklist.**

- Review the sick leave policy at onboarding and each policy change. For each request, have the owner approve the applicable leave category.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
