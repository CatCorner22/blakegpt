# Regular paydays and payday notices

**Rule.** A covered private employer must pay wages at least monthly on a regular payday, meet the applicable payroll deadlines, and post its regular payday in conspicuous places. The employer may pay more often.

**Exact text.**

> “not less frequently than once per month” (T.C.A. § 50-2-103(a)(1) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “in at least two (2) conspicuous places” (T.C.A. § 50-2-103(d) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “by the last day of the succeeding month” (2026 Tenn. Pub. Acts ch. 617, § 1)

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Coverage for subsection (a) starts at 5 employees (T.C.A. § 50-2-103(b)). Post in at least 2 conspicuous places where employees can see the notice going to and from work (subsection (d)).
- Monthly payroll: pay prior-month wages by day 5 of the succeeding month (subsection (a)(2), as amended).
- When paying in 2 or more periods per month, wages earned and unpaid before day 1 are due by day 20 of the month following the month earned. Wages earned and unpaid before day 16 are due by day 5 of the succeeding month (subsection (a)(3)(A)–(B)).
- Public Chapter 617 applies to wages or compensation earned on or after July 1, 2026 (§ 3).

**Exceptions.**

- On a monthly payroll, pay covered piece-work or commission compensation by the last day of the succeeding month (2026 Tenn. Pub. Acts ch. 617, § 1).

**Office checklist.**

- At setup and each schedule change, have the owner approve the payroll calendar and update both notices.
- Before submitting each payroll, reconcile the earnings period, pay date, and any commission or production-based pay.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
