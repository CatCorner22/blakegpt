# Paid time to vote on election day

**Rule.** An employer must allow eligible employees a reasonable period of election-day leave to vote during polling hours, up to 3 hours, without penalty or reduced pay. The employer may set the hours of the absence.

**Exact text.**

> “not to exceed three (3) hours” (T.C.A. § 2-1-106(a) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “any penalty or reduction in pay” (T.C.A. § 2-1-106(b) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “before twelve o’clock (12:00) noon” (T.C.A. § 2-1-106(d) — unofficial Justia mirror)

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- The employee requests leave before 12:00 noon on the day before the election, and uses up to 3 hours necessary to vote during polling hours in the employee’s county of residence (T.C.A. § 2-1-106(a), (d)).

**Exceptions.**

- No statutory leave if the work shift starts at least 3 hours after polls open or ends at least 3 hours before polls close in the employee’s county of residence (T.C.A. § 2-1-106(c)).

**Office checklist.**

- For each election request, check the county polling hours and shift. Have the scheduling lead approve the absence and payroll confirm full pay.
