# Federal family and medical leave coverage

**Rule.** A covered employer must provide eligible employees with federal family and medical leave for qualifying reasons, including birth, adoption or foster placement, and a qualifying serious health condition.

**Exact text.**

> “50 or more employees for each working day during each of 20 or more calendar workweeks” (29 U.S.C. § 2611(4)(A)(i) — Cornell LII mirror)

> “12 workweeks of leave during any 12-month period” (29 U.S.C. § 2612(a)(1) — Cornell LII mirror)

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Private-employer coverage starts at 50 employees on each working day in at least 20 calendar workweeks in the current or preceding calendar year (29 U.S.C. § 2611(4)(A)(i)).
- Generally, eligibility requires at least 12 months of employment, 1,250 hours in the prior 12 months, and at least 50 employees within 75 miles of the worksite (29 U.S.C. § 2611(2)(A), (B)(ii)).
- Ordinary qualifying leave is up to 12 workweeks in 12 months. Birth and placement leave expires 12 months after birth or placement. Covered military caregiver leave can run up to 26 workweeks in a single 12-month period (29 U.S.C. § 2612(a)(1)–(3)).

**Exceptions.**

- The statute permits unpaid leave and addresses substitution of accrued paid leave (29 U.S.C. § 2612(c)–(d)).

**Office checklist.**

- At each request, check the entity and worksite counts if the office is part of a larger group. Have the owner approve the coverage determination before communicating it.
