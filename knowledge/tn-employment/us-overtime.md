# Overtime and the practicing-dentist exemption

**Rule.** A covered employer must pay nonexempt employees overtime at least 1.5 times their regular rate for hours over 40 in a workweek. A practice may classify a dentist who holds a valid license and actually practices dentistry as exempt under the practice-of-medicine rule.

**Exact text.**

> “one and one-half times the regular rate” (29 U.S.C. § 207(a)(1) — Cornell LII mirror)

> “dentists” (29 CFR 541.304(b))

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Overtime starts after 40 hours in a workweek at 1.5 times the regular rate (29 U.S.C. § 207(a)(1)). The salary requirements of Subpart G do not apply to the licensed practicing-dentist exemption (29 CFR 541.304(a)(1), (b), (d)).

**Exceptions.**

- Job title alone does not qualify dental assistants, hygienists, or office staff under the practicing-dentist rule (29 CFR 541.304(a)–(b)).

**Office checklist.**

- At each hire or duties change, have the owner review exemption status against actual duties.
- Each pay period, total covered hours by workweek and have the payroll lead sign off on overtime.
