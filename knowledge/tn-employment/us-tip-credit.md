# Tips, tip pools, and the federal tip credit

**Rule.** A covered employer must not keep employee tips or let managers or supervisors share in pooled tips. An employer may claim a federal tip credit only for qualifying tipped work after notifying the employee and must make up any shortfall to minimum wage.

**Exact text.**

> “An employer may not keep tips received by its employees for any purposes” (29 U.S.C. § 203(m)(2)(B) — Cornell LII mirror)

> “customarily and regularly receives more than $30 a month in tips” (29 U.S.C. § 203(t) — Cornell LII mirror)

> “$2.13 per hour” (U.S. Department of Labor, Fact Sheet #15, Tip Credit (agency guidance))

> “directly and solely provides” (29 CFR 531.52(b)(2))

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- A tipped occupation requires more than $30 per month in tips customarily and regularly (29 U.S.C. § 203(t)).
- The minimum direct wage is $2.13 per hour and the maximum credit is $5.12 per hour against $7.25. Test the total for each workweek and pay any shortfall (29 U.S.C. §§ 203(m)(2)(A), 206(a)(1)(C), DOL Fact Sheet #15).
- DOL's state wage table identifies no Tennessee minimum-wage law. The Tennessee meal-period waiver for food and beverage service is a separate rule. The Labor Department reports that the 2021 federal dual-jobs revision was vacated in 2024 and the original regulation was restored (DOL, Tip Regulations under the FLSA, agency guidance).

**Exceptions.**

- A manager or supervisor may keep tips for services they directly and solely provide (29 CFR 531.52(b)(2)).
- A required pool with a tip credit is limited to employees who customarily and regularly receive tips. A pool without a tip credit can include other workers if the employer pays full minimum wage. Managers and supervisors cannot receive pooled tips (29 U.S.C. § 203(m)(2), DOL Fact Sheet #15).

**Office checklist.**

- When the office receives a patient gratuity, document who received it and flag any recurring pattern for the owner and payroll lead.
- Before applying a tip credit, have the owner confirm qualifying work, notice, and pooling rules. Reconcile actual wages and tips each workweek.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
