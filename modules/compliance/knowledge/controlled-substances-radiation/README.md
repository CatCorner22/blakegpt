## Not covered, and why

- Tennessee Public Chapters 2023-2026: capitol.tn.gov was not searched in this session, so no card confirms the absence of a recent amendment.
- Official Tennessee texts: publications.tnsosfiles.com returned 403 to curl and the browser; LexisNexis public access was not used. Mirrors were used instead.
- Court decisions: no search of case law was done.
- MATE Act statute text: the 21 U.S.C. 823 subsection was not pinned (uscode.house.gov 404); the card uses DEA guidance.
- Federal EPCS rules (21 CFR 1311) and DEA Form 222 ordering (21 CFR 1305): fetched but not carded.
- Shielding plan review and numeric occupational dose limits (0400-20-05-.50) and filtration tables: not carded in detail.
- Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 1140-11-.07 conflicts with T.C.A. 53-10-310 (annual vs 6-month check; 7-day vs 3-day exemption); noted in tn-53-10-310-csmd-check-before-prescribing, not carded separately.
