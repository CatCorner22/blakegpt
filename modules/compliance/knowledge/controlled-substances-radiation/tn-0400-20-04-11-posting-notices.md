# Posting radiation notices and violation notices (added item)

**Rule.** The office must post any written Division notice of violation within 2 working days after receipt and keep it posted for at least 5 working days or until the violation is corrected, whichever is later. The office may post a notice saying where other required documents may be examined.

**Exact text.**

> "shall be posted within two working days after receipt of the documents from the Division" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-04-.11(1)(f), Cornell LII mirror).

> "These documents shall remain posted for a minimum of five working days or until action correcting the violation has been completed, whichever is later" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-04-.11(1)(f), Cornell LII mirror).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Post: 2 working days.
- Keep posted: at least 5 working days or until corrected.

**Exceptions.**

None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- Post at the staff entrance to the x-ray area.

**Records to keep.**

No retention rule in this source.
