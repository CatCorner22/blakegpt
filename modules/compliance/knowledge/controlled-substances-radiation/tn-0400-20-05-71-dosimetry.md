# When x-ray staff must wear dosimeters, and dose record retention

**Rule.** The office must supply and require individual monitoring devices for each adult likely to receive more than 10% of the annual occupational limit in 1 year, and must keep monitoring records until the Division terminates the registration.

**Exact text.**

> "Adults likely to receive, in 1 year from sources external to the body, a dose in excess of 10 percent of the limits in Rule 0400-20-05-.50;" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-05-.71(1)(a)1., Cornell LII mirror).

> "Minors likely to receive, in 1 year from radiation sources external to the body, a deep dose equivalent in excess of 0.1 rem (1 mSv)" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-05-.71(1)(a)2., Cornell LII mirror).

> "The licensee or registrant shall retain each required form or record until the Division terminates each pertinent license or registration requiring the record." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-05-.135(7), Cornell LII mirror).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Adults: more than 10% of 0400-20-05-.50 limits in 1 year.
- Minors: more than 0.1 rem (1 mSv) deep dose in 1 year (0400-20-05-.71(1)(a)2.).

**Exceptions.**

None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- Document the dose estimate that shows whether badges are needed; hand-held operators always need badges (0400-20-06-.05(3)(r)).

**Records to keep.**

Individual monitoring records: until the Division terminates the registration (0400-20-05-.135(7)).
