# Dental x-ray inspections every 4 years and reduced-fee conditions

**Rule.** The office must have each dental x-ray machine (Class I) inspected once every 4 years. To qualify for the 18 percent registration fee, the office must have each newly acquired tube inspected within 3 months, file the state evaluation forms within 60 days after inspection, and correct and resubmit an unsatisfactory inspection within 30 days after Division notice.

**Exact text.**

> "CLASS I - once every 4 years" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-10-.27(3)(a), Cornell LII mirror).

> "Dental Radiation Machines:" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-10-.24(3)(a) Class I, Cornell LII mirror).

> "A registrant may qualify to pay a registration fee equal to 18 percent of that listed in this paragraph, subject to the following conditions:" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-10-.24(3)(d), Cornell LII mirror).

> "Each newly acquired tube subject to registration is inspected within 3 months of ownership or possession." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-10-.24(3)(d)2., Cornell LII mirror).

> "Copies of the appropriate State evaluation forms within 60 days after the inspection." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-10-.24(3)(d)4.(i), Cornell LII mirror).

> "The registrant shall correct and re-submit the report(s) and documentation of an inspection found to be unsatisfactory within 30 days of the date of notification by the Division." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0400-20-10-.24(3)(d), Cornell LII mirror).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Class I (dental): inspection once every 4 years (0400-20-10-.27(3)(a)).
- Reduced fee: 18 percent of the listed fee if all conditions are met.
- New tube: inspected within 3 months.
- Forms, service reports and signed certification: 60 days after inspection.
- Unsatisfactory inspection: correct and resubmit within 30 days of notice.
- Repeat inspections: same month as the prior one or the next month.

**Exceptions.**

None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- Calendar each tube inspection for the same month as the last one; hire an inspector who meets 0400-20-10-.27(4); file within 60 days.

**Records to keep.**

No retention rule in this source; keep inspection reports with the unit file.
