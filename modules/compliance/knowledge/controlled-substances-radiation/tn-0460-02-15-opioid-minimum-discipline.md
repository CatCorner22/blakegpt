# Board minimum discipline for opioid prescribing violations

**Rule.** When the Board finds a significant opioid-prescribing deviation, the minimum discipline includes a reprimand and a restriction against prescribing opioids for at least 6 months and until required continuing education is completed.

**Exact text.**

> "Reprimand;" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.15(2)(a), Cornell LII mirror).

> "A restriction against prescribing opioids for at least six (6) months, and until successful completion of the required continuing education;" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.15(2)(c), Cornell LII mirror).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Opioid restriction: at least 6 months.
- Repeat violations carry higher minimum discipline (0460-02-.15(3)).

**Exceptions.**

None stated in the source.
