# Registered dental assistant radiology certification (added item)

**Rule.** A registered dental assistant must not expose dental radiographs until the Board issues radiology certification, and must display current registration at the workplace. Certification is valid only while the assistant's registration is current.

**Exact text.**

> "Once eligible for certification, the registered dental assistant shall not expose dental radiographs until certification has been issued by the Board." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.11(2), Cornell LII mirror).

> "Certification in dental radiology is only valid as long as the registered dental assistant has a current registration." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.11(5), Cornell LII mirror).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

None stated in the source.

**Exceptions.**

None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- Check certification before an assistant takes any x-ray; post registrations; recheck every 90 days.
