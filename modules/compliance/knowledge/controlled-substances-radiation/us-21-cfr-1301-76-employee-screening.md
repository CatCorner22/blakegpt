# Staff with access to controlled substances: disqualifying history

**Rule.** The registrant must not employ, as an agent or employee with access to controlled substances, a person convicted of a felony relating to controlled substances or a person whose DEA application was denied, whose DEA registration was revoked, or who surrendered a DEA registration for cause.

**Exact text.**

> "The registrant shall not employ, as an agent or employee who has access to controlled substances, any person who has been convicted of a felony offense relating to controlled substances or who, at any time, had an application for registration with the DEA denied, had a DEA registration revoked or has surrendered a DEA registration for cause." (21 CFR 1301.76(a)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- "At any time" - no look-back limit on the DEA history.

**Exceptions.**

- 21 CFR 1301.76(a) refers to a waiver process under 1307.03; not summarized here.

**Office checklist.**

- Ask the screening question at hire before granting cabinet access; dentist-registrant approves access.
