# Practitioner controlled-substance records and 2-year retention

**Rule.** A dentist must keep records of Schedule II-V substances dispensed other than by prescribing or administering in the lawful course of practice, and need not keep DEA records for substances merely prescribed. The registrant must keep every required inventory and record available for DEA inspection for at least 2 years.

**Exact text.**

> "A registered individual practitioner is required to keep records, as described in § 1304.04, of controlled substances in Schedules II, III, IV, and V which are dispensed, other than by prescribing or administering in the lawful course of professional practice." (21 CFR 1304.03(b)).

> "a registered individual practitioner is not required to keep records of controlled substances in Schedules II, III, IV, and V that are prescribed in the lawful course of professional practice" (21 CFR 1304.03(c)).

> "for at least 2 years from the date of such inventory or record." (21 CFR 1304.04(a)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Retention: at least 2 years from the record date (21 CFR 1304.04(a)).
- Central storage of financial and shipping records requires notice to DEA (21 CFR 1304.04(a)(1)); inventories stay at the registered location (21 CFR 1304.04(b)(1)).

**Exceptions.**

- Records ARE required for prescribing in maintenance or detoxification treatment (21 CFR 1304.03(c)); 21 CFR 1304.03(d) adds administering situations not summarized here.

**Office checklist.**

- Keep a dispensing/administration log per substance; reconcile monthly; dentist signs.

**Records to keep.**

All required records and inventories: at least 2 years (21 CFR 1304.04(a)).
