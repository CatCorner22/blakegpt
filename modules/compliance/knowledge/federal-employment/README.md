## Not covered and why

- Tennessee state-law counterparts—THRA, Tennessee E-Verify, the Tennessee new-hire directory, Tennessee lactation/meal-break rules, and state COBRA continuation—are outside this federal section and were not fetched for substantive verification.
- Enacted Tennessee Pub. Ch. 471 (2025) text was not obtained. The fetched HB0910 bill is as introduced; it does not itself establish dissolution of the Tennessee Human Rights Commission or transfer of enforcement. Whether Tennessee's FEPA changed in 2025 remains unverified.
- NLRB GC Memo 26-04 full text was not obtained for verification in this section; only the news release was used. The handbook card identifies the memo-specific statements that the release does not establish.
- FLSA retaliation is now covered in `us-retaliation-prohibited`, including 29 U.S.C. 215(a)(3); it is no longer an omitted topic.
