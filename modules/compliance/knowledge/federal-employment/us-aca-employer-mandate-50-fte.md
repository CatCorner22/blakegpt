# ACA applicable large employer test

**Rule.** The owner dentist must assess applicable large employer status using full-time employees and full-time equivalents, not headcount alone. An applicable large employer must evaluate coverage obligations and potential employer shared-responsibility payments.

**Exact text.**
> "an employer who employed an average of at least 50 full-time employees on business days during the preceding calendar year" (26 U.S.C. 4980H(c)(2)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Average at least 50 full-time employees including equivalents during the preceding calendar year; add monthly totals and divide by 12, then round down (26 U.S.C. 4980H(c)(2); 26 CFR 54.4980H-2(b)(1)).
- The statutory full-time test averages at least 30 hours of service weekly; for employer-size purposes, divide non-full-time employees' aggregate monthly service hours by 120 to calculate equivalents, subject to the implementing rules (26 U.S.C. 4980H(c)(2)(E), (4)(A); 26 CFR 54.4980H-2(c)).

**Exceptions.**
- The seasonal-worker exception can apply when the count exceeds 50 for 120 days or less and the excess workers are seasonal. Related employers require aggregation (26 CFR 54.4980H-2(b)(2), (c)).

**Office checklist.**
- At year-end ask payroll and the benefits adviser to calculate the count, including related entities; owner dentist signs off.
- The IRS applicable-large-employer page is guidance, not law.
- Keep the coverage analysis and supporting payroll or request records as office practice, subject to applicable retention and litigation holds.
