# EEO-1 reporting and the proposed rescission

**Rule.** An employer meeting the regulation's coverage test must file the required employer information report according to the form instructions. The practice must not treat a proposed rescission as a final repeal.

**Exact text.**
> "100 or more employees" (29 CFR 1602.7).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- The regulation applies to covered employers with at least 100 employees; a practice below 100 does not meet this regulation's size test (29 CFR 1602.7).
- The Federal Register document published July 23, 2026 proposes rescission of reporting requirements; it is a proposed rule, not a final rule (91 FR 46332, action and summary).

**Exceptions.**
- Other reporting obligations require separate review. The EEOC data-collection page is agency guidance; filing dates require current form instructions (29 CFR 1602.7).

**Office checklist.**
- At year-end and expansion, review employee count; owner dentist signs off on coverage and any required filing. Check for a final rule before changing reporting procedures.

**Records to keep.** Keep a copy of the most recently filed report at each reporting unit or headquarters as the regulation permits (29 CFR 1602.7).
