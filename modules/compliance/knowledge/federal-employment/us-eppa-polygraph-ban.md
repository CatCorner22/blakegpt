# Employee polygraph restrictions

**Rule.** The practice must not request or require employee or applicant lie-detector testing unless a specific statutory exception applies. The practice must not use test results or punish protected refusal contrary to the Act.

**Exact text.**
> "require, request, suggest, or cause any employee or prospective employee to take or submit to any lie detector test" (29 U.S.C. 2002(1)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- The prohibition covers requests and suggestions, not just mandatory testing; a theft suspicion alone does not establish the statutory investigation exception (29 U.S.C. 2002; 2006(d)).

**Exceptions.**
- The statute includes narrow government, security, controlled-substance, and ongoing-investigation exceptions. The ongoing-investigation exception requires the specified loss, access, reasonable suspicion, and written statement; do not treat possession of dental-office drugs as a blanket exception (29 U.S.C. 2006).

**Office checklist.**
- Before any investigation involving testing, stop and obtain counsel's exception analysis; owner dentist signs off. DOL EPPA explanations are guidance, not law.
