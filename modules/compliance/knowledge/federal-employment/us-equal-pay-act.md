# Equal pay for equal work

**Rule.** Covered employers must not pay different wage rates based on sex for equal work within an establishment unless a statutory defense applies. The practice must not reduce another employee's wage rate to correct an unlawful differential.

**Exact text.**
> "equal work on jobs the performance of which requires equal skill, effort, and responsibility, and which are performed under similar working conditions" (29 U.S.C. 206(d)(1)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Coverage follows the Equal Pay Act and its commerce rules, not a separate Title VII employee-count threshold. The regulation protects men and women (29 CFR 1620.1(b)-(c)).

**Exceptions.**
- Permitted defenses are a seniority system, a merit system, a system measuring earnings by quantity or quality of production, or another factor other than sex (29 U.S.C. 206(d)(1)).

**Office checklist.**
- At hiring, pay changes, and pay reviews, compare actual skill, effort, responsibility, and conditions rather than titles alone; document the reason for differences; owner dentist signs off.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
