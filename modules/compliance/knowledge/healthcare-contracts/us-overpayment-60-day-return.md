# Report and return Medicare and Medicaid overpayments within 60 days of identification

**Rule.** The practice must report and return an overpayment to the State (TennCare) or Medicare contractor, with a written reason, by the later of 60 days after identifying it or the cost-report due date. An overpayment kept past the deadline becomes an obligation under the False Claims Act.

**Exact text.**

> "An overpayment must be reported and returned under paragraph (1) by the later of— (A) the date which is 60 days after the date on which the overpayment was identified; or (B) the date any corresponding cost report is due, if applicable." (42 U.S.C. 1320a-7k(d)(2)).

> "notify the Secretary, State, intermediary, carrier, or contractor to whom the overpayment was returned in writing of the reason for the overpayment." (42 U.S.C. 1320a-7k(d)(1)(B)).

> "Any overpayment retained by a person after the deadline for reporting and returning the overpayment under paragraph (2) is an obligation (as defined in section 3729(b)(3) of title 31) for purposes of section 3729 of such title." (42 U.S.C. 1320a-7k(d)(3)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- 60 days after the overpayment is identified (42 U.S.C. 1320a-7k(d)(2)(A)).

**Exceptions.**

- Cost-report due date if later (42 U.S.C. 1320a-7k(d)(2)(B)); dental offices rarely file cost reports.

**Office checklist.**

- When a billing error is found, log the identification date and calendar day 60.
- Owner dentist approves the refund letter before day 60.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
