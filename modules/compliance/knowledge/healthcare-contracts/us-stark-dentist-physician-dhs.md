# Stark law: dentists are physicians, but only Medicare-payable designated health services count

**Rule.** A dentist must treat himself or herself as a "physician" for Stark purposes. A dentist must not refer a Medicare patient for a designated health service (for example clinical laboratory or radiology services) to an entity in which the dentist has a financial relationship unless a Stark exception applies.

**Exact text.**

> "(2) a doctor of dental surgery or of dental medicine who is legally authorized to practice dentistry by the State in which he performs such function and who is acting within the scope of his license when he performs such functions" (42 U.S.C. 1395x(r)(2)).

> "The term \"designated health services\" means any of the following items or services: (A) Clinical laboratory services. (B) Physical therapy services. (C) Occupational therapy services. (D) Radiology services, including magnetic resonance imaging, computerized axial tomography scans, and ultrasound services." (42 U.S.C. 1395nn(h)(6)).

> "Designated health services (DHS) means any of the following services (other than those provided as emergency physician services furnished outside of the U.S.), as they are defined in this section" (42 CFR 411.351).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- DHS categories most likely to touch a dental office: clinical laboratory services (for example biopsy pathology), radiology and imaging (for example CBCT), outpatient prescription drugs, and durable medical equipment (for example sleep-apnea oral appliances billed to Medicare) (42 U.S.C. 1395nn(h)(6); 42 CFR 411.351).

**Exceptions.**

- Stark exceptions in 42 U.S.C. 1395nn(b)-(e) and 42 CFR 411.355-411.357 (not fetched or summarized here).
- Stark applies to Medicare DHS referrals; TennCare dental-only claims are not Medicare DHS, but the AKS still applies (us-aks-kickback-prohibition).

**Office checklist.**

- List every Medicare-billed lab, imaging or appliance service the practice orders and the entity that furnishes it.
- Flag any ownership, lease or compensation tie between a dentist and that entity for counsel review.
- Review once a year; owner dentist signs off.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
