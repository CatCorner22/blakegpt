# Biological (spore) testing of heat sterilizers, failed tests and 2-year logs

**Rule.** The office must test every heat sterilizer with a biological monitoring system that includes a control. After a positive spore test the dentist must follow the manufacturer guidelines and run a 2nd spore test; after a 2nd positive test the office must remove the device from service until repaired. The office must keep a test log for at least 2 years.

**Exact text.**

> "All heat sterilizing devices must be tested for proper function by means of a biological monitoring system that indicates microorganism kill." (0460-01-.11(3)).

> "The biological monitoring system used must include a control to verify proper microbial incubation." (0460-01-.11(3)).

> "Immediate action is defined as following manufacturer guidelines and performing a second (2 nd ) biological spore test." (0460-01-.11(3)).

> "In the event a second (2 nd ) positive biological spore test occurs, the device must be removed from service until repaired. Proof of such repair must be maintained with the testing documentation." (0460-01-.11(3)).

> "The documentation shall be maintained for a period of at least two (2) years" (0460-01-.11(4)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Testing frequency: none stated in the rule. CDC guidance says at least weekly (see us-cdc-sterilization-monitoring).
- 1st positive test: immediate action = manufacturer guidelines + a 2nd spore test (0460-01-.11(3)).
- 2nd positive test: remove from service until repaired; keep proof of repair with the test records (0460-01-.11(3)).
- Logs: at least 2 years, kept in the office, available immediately to the Tennessee Department of Health on request (0460-01-.11(4)).

**Exceptions.**

- The rule covers "heat sterilizing devices" only; it states no spore-test rule for liquid chemical sterilants.

**Office checklist.**

- Run a spore test with a matching control at least weekly (CDC guidance) on each heat sterilizer.
- Log date, sterilizer, person testing and result, or file the mail-in lab report.
- On a positive result: call the manufacturer guidance, retest at once, tell the lead dentist the same day.
- On a 2nd positive: tag the unit out of service; file the repair invoice with the log.
- Lead dentist reviews and initials the log monthly.

**Records to keep.** Spore-test log (dates and person(s) testing) or independent lab reports, plus proof of repair, for at least 2 years, in the office (0460-01-.11(3)-(4)).
