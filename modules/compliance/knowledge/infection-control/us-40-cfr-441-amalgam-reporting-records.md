# EPA dental amalgam rule: One-Time Compliance Report and 3-year records

**Rule.** The office must file a One-Time Compliance Report with its Control Authority and must file a new one within 90 days after an ownership transfer. The office must keep the report while it operates and must keep separator inspection, container replacement, amalgam pickup and repair records for at least 3 years.

**Exact text.**

> "For existing sources, a One-Time Compliance Report must be submitted to the Control Authority no later than October 12, 2020, or 90 days after a transfer of ownership." (40 CFR 441.50(a)(1)).

> "For new sources, a One-Time Compliance Report must be submitted to the Control Authority no later than 90 days following the introduction of wastewater into a POTW." (40 CFR 441.50(a)(1)).

> "must maintain and make available for inspection in either physical or electronic form, for a minimum of three years" (40 CFR 441.50(b)).

> "Documentation of the date, person(s) conducting the inspection, and results of each inspection of the amalgam separator(s) or equivalent device(s), and a summary of follow-up actions, if needed." (40 CFR 441.50(b)(1)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Existing sources: report by October 12, 2020. New sources: within 90 days of first discharge. New owner: within 90 days of transfer (441.50(a)(1), (a)(4)).
- Records: at least 3 years (441.50(b)).
- Report: keep as long as in operation or until ownership transfers (441.50(a)(5)).

**Exceptions.**

- No-amalgam offices file a shorter report with a certification (441.50(a)(3)(i)).

**Office checklist.**

- Keep a copy of the filed report and the Control Authority receipt.
- Log each inspection, cartridge change, pickup (with receiving facility name) and repair.
- Keep the current separator operating manual on file (441.50(b)(5)).

**Records to keep.** One-Time Compliance Report: life of operation or until ownership transfer (441.50(a)(5)). Inspection, replacement, pickup and repair records plus the manual: at least 3 years (441.50(b)).
