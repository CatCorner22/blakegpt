# Hand hygiene, PPE and respiratory hygiene (CDC guidance)

**Rule.** Staff must perform hand hygiene before and after each patient and before gloving, and must not wear 1 pair of gloves for more than 1 patient. The office may post cough signs, offer masks and seat symptomatic patients apart.

**Exact text.**

> "Before and after treating each patient." (2016 Summary, Hand Hygiene item 1.c).

> "Do not wear the same pair of gloves for the care of more than one patient." (2016 Summary, PPE item 3.a).

> "Wear mouth, nose, and eye protection during procedures that are likely to generate splashes or spattering of blood or other body fluids." (2016 Summary, PPE item 5).

> "Remove PPE before leaving the work area." (2016 Summary, PPE item 6).

> "Offer masks to coughing patients and other symptomatic persons when they enter the dental setting." (2016 Summary, Respiratory Hygiene item 1.d).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Hand hygiene: before and after each patient; before gloves; after removing gloves.
- Gloves: 1 pair per patient.

**Exceptions.**

- None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- Post cough-etiquette signs at the entrance; stock tissues, no-touch bins, masks and rub in the waiting room.
- Audit PPE removal at the operatory door monthly.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
