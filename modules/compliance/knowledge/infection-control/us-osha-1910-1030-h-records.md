# OSHA: exposure medical records (employment plus 30 years) and training records (3 years) (added item)

**Rule.** The employer must keep a confidential medical record for each exposed employee, including hepatitis B vaccination dates, for employment plus 30 years. The employer must keep bloodborne training records for 3 years.

**Exact text.**

> "A copy of the employee's hepatitis B vaccination status including the dates of all the hepatitis B vaccinations" (29 CFR 1910.1030(h)(1)(ii)(B)).

> "The employer shall maintain the records required by paragraph (h) for at least the duration of employment plus 30 years in accordance with 29 CFR 1910.1020 ." (29 CFR 1910.1030(h)(1)(iv)).

> "Training records shall be maintained for 3 years from the date on which the training occurred." (29 CFR 1910.1030(h)(2)(ii)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Medical: employment plus 30 years.
- Training: 3 years from the training date.

**Exceptions.**

- None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- Locked file for exposure medical records, separate from personnel files.
- Training sign-in: date, content summary, trainer name and qualifications, attendee names and job titles ((h)(2)(i)).

**Records to keep.** As above: medical records employment plus 30 years ((h)(1)(iv)); training records 3 years ((h)(2)(ii)).
