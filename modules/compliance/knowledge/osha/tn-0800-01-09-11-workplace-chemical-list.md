# Workplace chemical list and fire-chief information

**Rule.** The employer must maintain and update the workplace chemical list and file it upon an authorized request. The employer must provide the fire chief the required chemical information and significant changes when the fire-chief provisions apply.

**Exact text.**
> "Employers shall maintain a copy of each WCL in the workplace to which it pertains." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-09-.11(3)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- File the list within 96 hours of an authorized request; delivery must arrive within that time (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-09-.11(2)(a)-(b)).
- Nonmanufacturing list thresholds are amounts in excess of 55 gallons for liquids or 500 pounds for solids; gases have separate tests, not a blanket exemption (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-09-.11(2)(c)1-3).
- Retain each list for 30 years after its effective period; send all lists to the commissioner within 90 days after ceasing Tennessee business (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-09-.12(3)).

**Exceptions.**
- Gas tests include airborne-limit comparisons, compressed-cylinder quantities, and flammable-gas quantity; consult -.11(2)(c)3 for the actual gas. Fire-chief rule coverage and exemption provisions are separate in -.10(1), (11)-(12); liquid/solid list cutoffs are not universal HazCom exemptions.

**Office checklist.**
- Practice workflow: Amanda: maintain employer/location/NAICS/identification information, product identifiers, and work areas; alert new or reassigned staff before hazardous-area work. Provide fire-chief contacts, list, changes, and requested SDSs; review applicable building signage. Owner dentist: sign off on gas tests, fire-chief applicability, and any exemptions.

**Records to keep.** Keep lists for the period above. Keep fire-chief exemption correspondence while effective plus 1 year (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-09-.12(2)-(3)).
