# Tennessee HazCom training and right to know

**Rule.** The employer must provide covered employees initial and new-hazard chemical training and annual refresher training, subject to the statutory exemption process. The employer must keep training records.

**Exact text.**
> "Refresher training shall be provided at least annually thereafter." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-09-.07).

> "Training records shall be maintained for the period an employee is employed plus five years." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-09-.12(1)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Train at initial assignment, whenever a new hazard enters the work area, and at least annually thereafter (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-09-.07).
- Keep training records for employment plus 5 years (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-09-.12(1)).

**Exceptions.**
- The commissioner may grant an annual-refresher exemption based on assignment, exposure, or chemical nature; it is not automatic. Office workers are generally excluded unless duties routinely involve potential chemical exposure (T.C.A. 50-3-2001(1)(B); Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-09-.02(3)).

**Office checklist.**
- Practice workflow: Amanda: maintain a covered-worker roster, schedule training, and retain employee identification, training dates, and description. Owner dentist: approve coverage and confirm any claimed exemption is actually granted.

**Records to keep.** Training records must identify the employee, dates, and brief training description; retain employment plus 5 years (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-01-09-.07(4), -.12(1)).
