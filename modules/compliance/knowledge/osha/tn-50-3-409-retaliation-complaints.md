# Safety retaliation and employee complaints

**Rule.** The employer must not discharge or discriminate against an employee for protected safety complaints, proceedings, testimony, or exercise of statutory rights. An affected employee may file a retaliation complaint with the commissioner.

**Exact text.**
> "within thirty (30) days after such violation occurs" (T.C.A. 50-3-409(b)(1)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Employee complaint: within 30 days after violation (T.C.A. 50-3-409(b)(1)).
- Commissioner determination notice: within 90 days after receipt of complaint (T.C.A. 50-3-409(c)).

**Exceptions.**
- None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**
- Practice workflow: Amanda: route safety concerns without adverse treatment and preserve relevant communications. Owner dentist: review proposed employment action for protected-activity concerns; do not obstruct complaints.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
