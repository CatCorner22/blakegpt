# Dental-office partial log exemption

**Rule.** The employer may omit routine injury and illness logs for a dental-office establishment listed in the partial-exemption appendix unless the government requests records. The employer must still report severe incidents and comply with an applicable written collection notice.

**Exact text.**
> "you do not need to keep OSHA injury and illness records unless the government asks you to keep the records under § 1904.41 or § 1904.42." (29 CFR 1904.2(a)(1)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Dental listing: NAICS 6212, Offices of Dentists (29 CFR Part 1904 Subpart B, Appendix A).
- A partially exempt establishment need not routinely submit records electronically but must keep and submit requested information if OSHA notifies it in writing; follow the notice's timing (29 CFR 1904.41(b)(4), (6)).

**Exceptions.**
- Industry exemption is establishment-specific. Non-exempt establishments may need logs even when another establishment is exempt. The source references BLS requests, but no separate procedural card for that section is provided (29 CFR 1904.2(a)-(b)).
