# Federal severe-injury reporting

**Rule.** The employer must report qualifying work-related fatalities, inpatient hospitalizations, amputations, and eye losses to federal OSHA where federal reporting jurisdiction applies. The employer must use the prescribed reporting channels; Tennessee practices must consult the TOSHA card.

**Exact text.**
> "OSHA defines in-patient hospitalization as a formal admission to the in-patient service of a hospital or clinic for care or treatment." (29 CFR 1904.39(b)(9)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Fatality: report within 8 hours and only if death occurs within 30 days of the incident (29 CFR 1904.39(a)(1), (b)(6)).
- Inpatient hospitalization of 1 or more employees, amputation, or eye loss: report within 24 hours and only if the event occurs within 24 hours of the incident (29 CFR 1904.39(a)(2), (b)(6)).
- Late knowledge triggers the same 8-hour or 24-hour periods after learning of the event or its work relationship (29 CFR 1904.39(b)(7)-(8)).
- Federal central number: 1-800-321-6742 (29 CFR 1904.39(a)(3)(ii)).

**Exceptions.**
- Observation/testing-only admissions are excluded. Public-road vehicle events outside construction zones and commercial/public transportation events have reporting exceptions; logging may still apply. Use the central number or reporting application if the office is closed, not voicemail, fax, or email (29 CFR 1904.39(b)(1), (3)-(4), (10)).
