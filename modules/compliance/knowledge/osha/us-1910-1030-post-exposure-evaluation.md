# Post-exposure evaluation and follow-up

**Rule.** The employer must immediately make confidential medical evaluation and follow-up available after a reported exposure incident at no employee cost. The employer must document the exposure, provide required information to the healthcare professional, and provide the employee the limited written opinion.

**Exact text.**
> "The exposed employee's blood shall be collected as soon as feasible and tested after consent is obtained." (29 CFR 1910.1030(f)(3)(iii)(A)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Preserve a baseline sample at least 90 days when collection is accepted but HIV testing is declined; test as soon as feasible if elected within 90 days (29 CFR 1910.1030(f)(3)(iii)(B)).
- Obtain and give the written opinion within 15 days after evaluation completion (29 CFR 1910.1030(f)(5)).

**Exceptions.**
- Source identification may be infeasible or prohibited by law; source testing follows consent rules and known infection need not be retested. Keep other findings confidential (29 CFR 1910.1030(f)(3)(ii), (f)(5)(ii)).

**Office checklist.**
- Practice workflow: Amanda: activate the exposure referral immediately and transmit required records securely. Owner dentist: confirm follow-up access, counseling, and medically indicated prophylaxis with the licensed professional.

**Records to keep.** Keep confidential medical records for employment plus 30 years (29 CFR 1910.1030(f)(6), (h)(1)(iv)).
