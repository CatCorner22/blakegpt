# First aid and corrosive-exposure flushing

**Rule.** The employer must ensure ready availability of medical personnel for advice and consultation and readily available adequate first-aid supplies. The employer must provide trained first-aid personnel when nearby treatment facilities are absent and immediate emergency drenching or flushing facilities where injurious corrosives may contact eyes or body.

**Exact text.**
> "Adequate first aid supplies shall be readily available." (29 CFR 1910.151(b)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Where injurious corrosive exposure is possible, provide suitable drenching/flushing facilities within the work area for immediate emergency use (29 CFR 1910.151(c)).

**Exceptions.**
- The first-aid trained-person requirement depends on absence of an infirmary, clinic, or hospital in near proximity used for treatment of all injured employees. The source gives no numeric travel-time or eyewash-flow requirement (29 CFR 1910.151(b)-(c)).

**Office checklist.**
- Practice workflow: Amanda: check supplies and corrosive hazards when inventory changes; keep flushing access unobstructed. Owner dentist: confirm nearby-treatment arrangements or trained-person coverage and suitable flushing facilities.
