# Safe and unobstructed exit routes

**Rule.** The employer must provide adequate exit routes, keep them unobstructed, and allow employees to open exit doors from inside without keys, tools, or special knowledge. The employer must maintain effective lighting, marking, and safeguards.

**Exact text.**
> "Exit routes must be free and unobstructed." (29 CFR 1910.37(a)(3)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Normally provide at least 2 separated exit routes; more may be needed and a single route is allowed only when safe evacuation is possible under the stated factors (29 CFR 1910.36(b)(1)-(3)).
- Exit access must be at least 28 inches wide, with greater capacity where required for occupant load (29 CFR 1910.36(g)(2)-(3)).

**Exceptions.**
- A single route requires the safe-evacuation conditions in 1910.36(b)(3). The inside-lock exception is confined to specified institutional facilities with continuous supervision and an evacuation plan, not an ordinary dental office (29 CFR 1910.36(d)(3)).

**Office checklist.**
- Practice workflow: Amanda: walk routes during office setup and after deliveries; remove temporary storage and check visible Exit signs. Owner dentist: approve route capacity and any single-route determination.
