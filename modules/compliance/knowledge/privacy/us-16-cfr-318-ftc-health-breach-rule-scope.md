# FTC Health Breach Rule scope

**Rule.** A HIPAA-covered dental practice may treat itself as outside Part 318 under its express covered-entity exclusion, not as subject to duplicate FTC breach notices under this rule. A separate non-HIPAA personal-health-record business must assess the vendor, PHR-related entity, and service-provider definitions before handling an incident.

**Exact text.**
> "This part does not apply to HIPAA-covered entities, or to any other entity to the extent that it engages in activities as a business associate of a HIPAA-covered entity." (16 CFR 318.1(a)).

> "Vendor of personal health records means an entity, other than a HIPAA-covered entity or an entity to the extent that it engages in activities as a business associate of a HIPAA-covered entity, that offers or maintains a personal health record." (16 CFR 318.2).

> "Personal health record (PHR) means an electronic record of PHR identifiable health information on an individual that has the technical capacity to draw information from multiple sources and that is managed, shared, and controlled by or primarily for the individual." (16 CFR 318.2).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Scope covers foreign/domestic qualifying entities maintaining information of U.S. citizens/residents, irrespective of FTC Act jurisdictional tests (318.1(a)).
- PHR-related entities include qualifying businesses offering products through PHR/HIPAA-entity sites or accessing/sending unsecured PHR information; third-party service providers must meet both service and information-handling elements (318.2).
- For a nonexcluded entity, individual/media/service-provider notices are due without unreasonable delay and within 60 calendar days after discovery; FTC notice for 500 or more individuals is contemporaneous, and fewer-than-500 logs may be annual within 60 calendar days after year end (318.4(a)–(b)).
- FTC media trigger is 500 or more residents of a state/jurisdiction (318.3(a)(3)); do not import HIPAA's more-than-500 media threshold.

**Exceptions.**
- The BA exclusion applies only to BA activities; a separate non-BA consumer product needs its own analysis (318.1(a), 318.2).
- The FTC timing provision includes a law-enforcement exception with its own incorporated conditions (318.4(d)).

**Office checklist.**
- Before launching or buying a consumer-facing record product, Amanda documents whether the operator is the HIPAA practice, its BA, or a separate business.
- The privacy official approves the scope decision; route any non-HIPAA PHR incident for separate FTC-rule review.

**Records to keep.** 318.4(c) requires proof that notices and any delay comply but gives no fixed retention period. No fixed retention period appears in the cited scope/definition provisions.
