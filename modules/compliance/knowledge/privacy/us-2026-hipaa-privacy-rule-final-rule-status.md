# Pending HIPAA Privacy final rule status

**Rule.** A covered practice must continue to meet operative current requirements while preparing for the issued public-inspection final rule. Staff must not describe this rule as already effective on 2026-11-18 or as merely a proposal.

**Exact text.**
> "The final rule is effective on [INSERT DATE 60 DAYS AFTER DATE OF PUBLICATION IN THE FEDERAL REGISTER]." (2026-23429, DATES p. 1).

> "January 18, 2028." (2026-23429, revised 45 CFR 164.532(e)(2)(ii)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Final rule 2026-23429 is scheduled for publication 2026-11-19; effective 60 days after publication (derived 2027-01-18), compliance 240 days after publication (derived 2027-07-17). These calendar dates are calculated, not quoted (final rule DATES, p. 1).
- Access changes from current 30 days to as soon as practicable within 15 calendar days; 1 extension up to 30 calendar days; shorter applicable law is deemed practicable (revised 164.524(b)(2)).
- Revised 164.524 adds access-to-direct for electronic health records, changes fees, and new 164.525 requires fee schedules. Revised 164.520 changes the notice header/contact email and removes acknowledgment efforts.
- Qualifying BA contracts in place before publication may remain deemed compliant until the earlier of renewal/modification on or after the compliance date or 2028-01-18. They must meet prior rules and not be renewed/modified during the effective-to-compliance window (164.532(e)(1)–(2)).

**Exceptions.** 
- The BA transition is limited deemed compliance, not a delay of patient access, amendment, accounting, or mitigation duties (164.532(e)(3)).

**Office checklist.**
- At publication, Amanda checks the final published text and confirms calculated dates; the privacy official approves the implementation calendar.
- Before compliance, the privacy official signs updated access, fee, notice, vendor, and training procedures; Amanda inventories BA renewal dates.

**Records to keep.** Keep revised policies and required records under 164.530(j) for 6 years from creation or last effective date, whichever is later.
