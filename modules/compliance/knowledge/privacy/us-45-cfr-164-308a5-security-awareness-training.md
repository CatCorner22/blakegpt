# Security awareness and training

**Rule.** A covered practice or business associate must run a security awareness and training program for its entire workforce, including management. The entity must assess and implement the addressable safeguards when reasonable and appropriate, rather than treat them as optional without analysis.

**Exact text.**
> "Implement a security awareness and training program for all members of its workforce (including management)." (45 CFR 164.308(a)(5)(i)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**
- Security reminders, malicious-software protection, login monitoring, and password management are addressable specifications (164.308(a)(5)(ii)).
- Security reminders are periodic; no fixed annual course interval appears in this source.

**Exceptions.** 
- If an addressable measure is not reasonable and appropriate, document why and implement an equivalent alternative when reasonable and appropriate (164.306(d)(3)).

**Office checklist.**
- At onboarding and when risks or systems change, Amanda arranges role-specific security instruction; this is an office workflow, not a fixed statutory course schedule.
- The security official approves content and the periodic reminder schedule and signs the office training log.

**Records to keep.** Keep security policies and required documented actions/assessments for 6 years from creation or last effective date, whichever is later; make them available to implementers and update as needed (45 CFR 164.316(b)(1)–(2)).
