# Tennessee Board of Dentistry rules for the office

Scope: the Tennessee Board of Dentistry rules (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. ch. 0460-01 to 0460-04) and the T.C.A. Title 63, Chapter 5 sections that a Tennessee dental office manager uses to staff, supervise, train and run the office: assistant levels and certifications, CE and CPR, infection control, conduct, patient rights, records, advertising, sedation permits, ownership and hygienist scope.

## Items added beyond the assigned scope

- tn-63-5-115-supervision-definitions: statutory definitions of direct and general supervision, which the scope rule 0460-04-.08 relies on.
- tn-0460-01-12-26-patient-termination-notice: 14-day mailed notice before dismissing a patient.
- tn-63-2-101-patient-record-copies: 10-working-day copy deadline and copy fee caps (T.C.A. §§ 63-2-101, 63-2-102).
- tn-63-5-116-discipline-for-unauthorized-duties: Board discipline of the dentist and the assistant for out-of-scope duties.

## Recent and pending changes (checked 2026-11-18)

- Proposed, not effective: Notice ID 4269 (0460-04 wording changes; hearing 07/09/2026); filings 11-17-26, 11-18-26 and 11-19-26 (hearing 01/07/2027) that would rewrite 0460-01-.01, .05 (CE and CPR), .11, .12, .13, amend .16(7), amend 0460-03-.09 and .13, and add an assistant duty to 0460-04-.08(3) for helping deliver medications and anesthetic agents under direct visual supervision.
