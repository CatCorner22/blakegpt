# Infection control in the dental office

**Rule.** The dentist must ensure daily sterilization by an approved method, sterilize listed instruments between patients, test every heat sterilizer with a biological monitor, and keep the test log in the office for at least 2 years. All dentists, hygienists and assistants must wear gloves, masks and eye protection when contact with blood or saliva is imminent, and must change gloves between patients.

**Exact text.**

> "The dentist shall ensure that at least one (1) of the following sterilization procedures is utilized daily for instruments and equipment" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.11(1)).

> "All heat sterilizing devices must be tested for proper function by means of a biological monitoring system that indicates microorganism kill." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.11(3)).

> "In the event a second (2nd) positive biological spore test occurs, the device must be removed from service until repaired." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.11(3)).

> "The documentation shall be maintained for a period of at least two (2) years, and shall be maintained in the dental office and be made immediately available upon request by an authorized agent of the Tennessee Department of Health." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.11(4)).

> "bleach used in a dilution ratio of one (1) to ten (10) or one hundred (100) [1:10 or 1:100]" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.11(6)).

> "Gloves are never to be washed and reused." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.11(11)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Sterilization by steam autoclave, dry heat, chemical vapor or EPA-approved chemical sterilant, daily (0460-01-.11(1)).
- 12 instrument classes sterilized between patients unless disposable (0460-01-.11(2)(a)-(l)).
- Positive spore test: follow manufacturer guidance and run a 2nd test; 2nd positive = remove from service until repaired (0460-01-.11(3)).
- Sterilizer test log kept at least 2 years in the office (0460-01-.11(4)).
- Bleach dilution 1:10 or 1:100 (0460-01-.11(6)).
- Barriers replaced between patients (0460-01-.11(7)).

**Exceptions.**

- A worker with exudative lesions or weeping dermatitis may give patient care if "barrier techniques would prevent patient contact" (0460-01-.11(16)).

**Office checklist.**

- Run and log a biological spore test on each heat sterilizer; the rule sets no frequency, so follow the manufacturer and CDC guidance (office practice).
- Log each spore result with the date and the person who ran it.
- On a positive result, retest at once; on a 2nd positive, tag the unit out and file the repair proof with the log.
- Each month, the dentist reviews and initials the log.
- Lab cases: disinfect, seal and label "Biohazard" before shipping (0460-01-.11(9)).

**Records to keep.**

Keep heat-sterilizer test logs or independent testing reports, and repair proof, in the office for at least 2 years (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.11(3)-(4)).
