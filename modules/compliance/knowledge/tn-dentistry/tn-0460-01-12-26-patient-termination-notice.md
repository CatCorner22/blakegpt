# Ending a dentist/patient relationship: 14-day mailed notice

**Rule.** The dentist who owns or keeps the records must mail a termination notice to a patient at least 14 days before ending the relationship, including dismissal for non-payment or an office closing for more than 30 days. The notice must state the effective date and, if applicable, when care may resume.

**Exact text.**

> "a "dentist/patient" relationship exists where a dentist has provided dental treatment to a patient on at least one occasion within the preceding year" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.12(26)).

> "The office where the patient has received dental care has been closed permanently or for a period in excess of thirty (30) days" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.12(26)(a)1.).

> "The dentist discontinues treatment of a particular patient for any reason, including non-payment of fees for dental services" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.12(26)(a)2.).

> "The notice shall be mailed at least fourteen (14) days prior to the date of termination of the dentist/patient relationship, unless the termination results from an unforeseen emergency (such as sudden injury or illness), in which case the notice shall be mailed as soon as practicable under the circumstances." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.12(26)(c)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Relationship exists if treated at least once in the preceding year (0460-01-.12(26)).
- Office closure trigger: permanent or more than 30 days (0460-01-.12(26)(a)1.).
- Mail notice at least 14 days before termination (0460-01-.12(26)(c)).

**Exceptions.**

- Unforeseen emergency: mail "as soon as practicable" (0460-01-.12(26)(c)).

**Office checklist.**

- Use a dismissal letter template with the effective date and any resume date; mail it at least 14 days ahead.
- Keep a copy of the letter and proof of mailing in the chart.
- The treating dentist signs every dismissal letter.
- Records must not be withheld for non-payment (0460-02-.12(4)(b)).

**Records to keep.**

No retention rule in this source. Keep the letter in the dental record, which is kept at least 7 years (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.12(4)(g)).
