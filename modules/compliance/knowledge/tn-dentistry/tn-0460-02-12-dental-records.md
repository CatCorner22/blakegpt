# Dental records: required content, transfer, retention and destruction

**Rule.** The dentist must create and keep a record for every patient that includes, at a minimum, tooth charting, a concise description and date of each service, a concise medical history, drugs prescribed or dispensed, and readable x-rays. The office must keep each record at least 7 years after the last professional contact, longer for minors and indefinitely for incompetent patients, and must not withhold records for non-payment.

**Exact text.**

> "A dentist shall not withhold records for non-payment of current or prior dental services." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.12(4)(b)).

> "that notice shall be required to be issued within thirty (30) days of the date of the event that triggers the notice requirement, and may be accomplished by public notice" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.12(4)(c)).

> "X-rays and X-ray interpretations are considered to be part of the dental records." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.12(4)(e)3.).

> "Notation of dates, types, and amounts of pharmaceuticals prescribed or dispensed." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.12(4)(e)4.(iv)).

> "patients seen by the dentist in his/her office during the immediately preceding thirty-six (36) months shall be notified" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.12(4)(f)1.).

> "Dental records shall be retained for a period of not less than seven (7) years from the dentist's or his supervisees' last professional contact with the patient" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.12(4)(g)).

> "Dental records of minors shall be retained for a period of not less than one (1) year after the minor reaches the age of majority or seven (7) years from the date of the dentist's or his supervisees' last professional contact with the patient, whichever is longer." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.12(4)(g)2.).

> "When records are destroyed, the time, date and circumstances of the destruction shall be recorded and maintained for future reference." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.12(4)(h)4.).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Minimum content: 5 items (0460-02-.12(4)(e)4.(i)-(v)), plus T.C.A. § 63-2-101(c) content (0460-02-.12(4)(e)1.).
- Patient notices required by the rule: within 30 days of the triggering event (0460-02-.12(4)(c)).
- Retirement, death, departure or sale: notify patients seen in the preceding 36 months (0460-02-.12(4)(f)1.-3.).
- Retention: at least 7 years from last contact; minors, the longer of 1 year after majority or 7 years; incompetent patients, indefinitely; disputed services, until resolved (0460-02-.12(4)(g)).

**Exceptions.**

- Records created in a hospital, hospital emergency room or hospital outpatient facility are governed by T.C.A. Title 68 instead (0460-02-.12(3), (4)(d)).
- Death of a dentist is not abandonment (0460-02-.12(4)(f)4.).

**Office checklist.**

- Each quarter, audit a sample of charts for the 5 minimum items (office practice); the dentist signs the audit.
- Keep a written destruction procedure; destroy only in the ordinary course by shredding or another secure method.
- Keep a destruction log (date, time, method, record group) permanently (office practice; the rule says "for future reference").
- Put a legal hold on any chart with a disputed service.

**Records to keep.**

Keep each dental record at least 7 years after last professional contact; minors, the longer of 1 year past majority or 7 years; incompetent patients, indefinitely; disputed services, until the dispute ends (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-02-.12(4)(g)). Keep a destruction record that identifies each destroyed group (0460-02-.12(4)(h)4.).
