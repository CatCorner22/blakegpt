# Dental assistant registration renewal and reinstatement

**Rule.** A registered dental assistant must renew the registration on or before the expiration date, which is the last day of the assistant's birth month. An assistant whose registration has expired must not practice as a registered dental assistant until the Board reinstates it.

**Exact text.**

> "The due date for registration renewal is the last day of the month in which a registrant's birthday falls" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.06(1)(a)).

> "Failure to receive such notification does not relieve the registrant from the responsibility of meeting all requirements for renewal." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.06(1)(b)2.).

> "Provide documentation of successfully completing continuing education requirements for the entire time the registration was expired, pursuant to Rule 0460-01-.05" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.06(2)(b)).

> "Any registrant who fails to renew registration prior to the expiration of the second (2nd) year after which renewal is due may be required to meet other conditions as the Board may deem necessary to protect the public." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.06(2)(d)).

> "Practicing with an expired, retired, suspended or revoked license, permit, or registration." (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.12(18)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Renewal due: last day of the registrant's birth month (0460-04-.06(1)(a)).
- Renewal is biennial; CE and CPR attestations are made on the "biennial renewal application" (0460-01-.05(2)(b), (4)(a)).
- Renewal requires proof of the Ethics and Jurisprudence examination (0460-04-.06(1)(c)3.).
- Expired more than 2 years: the Board may impose other conditions (0460-04-.06(2)(d)).

**Exceptions.**

- Renewal may be done online or on paper (0460-04-.06(1)(b)).

**Office checklist.**

- Keep a renewal calendar with each assistant's birth month; send each assistant a reminder well before the due date (office practice).
- On the first business day after each due date, verify each renewal on the Board site.
- Remove an assistant from registered-level duties the day a registration lapses; the owner dentist signs the removal.

**Records to keep.**

No retention rule in this source.
