# Dental assistant registration retirement and reactivation

**Rule.** A registered dental assistant who stops practicing may retire the registration by filing an affidavit of retirement. A retired assistant must reactivate the registration, including the required CE, CPR and Ethics and Jurisprudence examination, before practicing again.

**Exact text.**

> "Registrants who wish to retain their registration but not actively practice may avoid compliance with the registration renewal process, continuing education and CPR requirements by doing the following:" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.07(1)).

> "appear before the Board, a designated Board member, or the Board consultant, for an interview regarding continued competence in the event of retirement in excess of two (2) years" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-04-.07(2)(c)).

> "one-half (1/2) the number of hours of approved dental-related continuing education required by subparagraph (1)(c) of this rule, all of which must have been earned in the twelve (12) months immediately preceding application for reactivation" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.05(6)(b)1.).

> "twelve (12) hours of continuing education" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.05(6)(b)2.).

> "twenty-four (24) hours of continuing education" (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0460-01-.05(6)(b)3.).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Reactivation within 1 year of retirement under 0460-04-.06(5): the Board may require the late renewal fee and past-due fees (0460-04-.07(2)(b)).
- Retired more than 2 years: possible competence interview (0460-04-.07(2)(c)).
- CE for reactivation, all earned in the 12 months before applying: retired under 2 years = one-half of 24 hours; 2 to under 5 years = 12 hours; 5 years or more = 24 hours (0460-01-.05(6)(b)1.-3.).
- Reactivation also requires current CPR (0460-01-.05(6)(c)).
- Reactivation CE hours do not count toward the next renewal (0460-01-.05(6)).

**Exceptions.**

- The Board may waive or condition CE or CPR requirements for reactivation in emergency situations (0460-01-.05(6)(d)).

**Office checklist.**

- When rehiring a former assistant, verify on the Board site that the registration is active, not retired.
- A retired assistant works only at the practical level until reactivation posts; the owner dentist signs off.
