# Discipline when an assistant or hygienist works outside the law

**Rule.** The Board may discipline or fine the supervising dentist who lets a hygienist or assistant perform unauthorized acts, and may separately discipline or fine the hygienist or assistant. Each separate violation can carry its own civil penalty.

**Exact text.**

> "The board may revoke or suspend the license of, assess a civil penalty for each separate violation against or otherwise lawfully discipline any dentist who permits any licensed and registered dental hygienist, registered dental assistant and/or practical dental assistant operating under that dentist's supervision to perform any acts or services other than those authorized by this chapter." (T.C.A. § 63-5-116(a) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

> "The board may also revoke or suspend the license or registration of, assess a civil penalty for each separate violation against or otherwise lawfully discipline any dental hygienist or dental assistant violating any of the provisions of this chapter." (T.C.A. § 63-5-116(b) (Justia unofficial mirror)).

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Civil penalty may be assessed "for each separate violation" (T.C.A. § 63-5-116(a)-(b)).

**Exceptions.**

- None stated in the source.

**Office checklist.**

- Treat every out-of-scope duty as a reportable internal incident; the supervising dentist reviews it promptly (office practice).
- Keep the signed duty matrix for each assistant on file.
