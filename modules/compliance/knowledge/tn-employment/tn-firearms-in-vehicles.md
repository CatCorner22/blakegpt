# Employment protection for lawful firearm storage in parked vehicles

**Rule.** An employer must not discharge or take adverse action against a covered employee solely for parking-area transport or storage of a firearm or ammunition that complies with Tennessee’s vehicle-storage statute. An employer may prohibit firearms elsewhere on the premises within the limits of the statute.

**Exact text.**

> “solely for transporting or storing a firearm or firearm ammunition in an employer parking area” (T.C.A. § 50-1-312(b)(1)(A) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “possesses a valid handgun carry permit recognized in this state” (T.C.A. § 50-1-312(a)(1) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “parked in a location where the motor vehicle is permitted to be” (T.C.A. § 39-17-1313(a)(1) — unofficial Justia mirror)

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- The parking law applies in a permitted parking location and sets visibility and security conditions. It recognizes specified permit holders and qualifying lawful carry under T.C.A. § 39-17-1307(g). The employment remedy separately defines the protected employee as holding a recognized valid handgun carry permit (T.C.A. §§ 39-17-1313(a), 50-1-312(a)(1), (b)(1)(A)).
- An employee has 1 year from discharge or adverse action to file the statutory action (T.C.A. § 50-1-312(b)(3)).

**Exceptions.**

- The parking statute yields to express federal prohibitions and excludes certain employer-provided vehicles when a written prohibition applies and certain single-family residences (T.C.A. § 39-17-1313(a), (c)).
- The employment statute permits restrictions on other premises subject to the parking law (T.C.A. § 50-1-312(d)).

**Office checklist.**

- At each policy revision, have the owner distinguish parking areas from clinical premises. Before any employment action involving a vehicle, have the owner document the relevant facts and review the parking statute.
