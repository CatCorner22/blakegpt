# Tennessee new-hire and rehire reporting

**Rule.** An employer must report newly hired and qualifying rehired employees to the Tennessee new-hire directory. An employer may use the statutory electronic batch schedule.

**Exact text.**

> “not later than twenty (20) days after the date the employer hires the employee” (T.C.A. § 36-5-1104 — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “not less than twelve (12) nor more than sixteen (16) days apart” (T.C.A. § 36-5-1104 — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “at least 60 consecutive days” (42 U.S.C. § 653a(a)(2)(C)(ii) — Cornell LII mirror)

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Submit by 20 days after hire. An employer reporting electronically or magnetically may use 2 monthly transmissions if needed, 12 to 16 days apart (T.C.A. § 36-5-1104).
- Treat a rehire after at least 60 consecutive days of separation as newly hired under the federal definition. Tennessee Labor and Workforce Development's new-hire guidance directs employers to report hires and rehires (42 U.S.C. § 653a(a)(2)(C)).
- Report the employee name, address, hire date, and Social Security number and the employer name, address, and federal identifying number (T.C.A. § 36-5-1102).

**Exceptions.**

- An eligible multistate employer reporting electronically may designate a single employee-work state and notify the federal Secretary (42 U.S.C. § 653a(b)(1)(B)). The Tennessee provision defines employee by federal wage withholding status (T.C.A. § 36-5-1101(5)).

**Office checklist.**

- At each hire or rehire, use the secure reporting portal and save confirmation. Have the payroll lead reconcile submitted reports against the hire roster each batch.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
