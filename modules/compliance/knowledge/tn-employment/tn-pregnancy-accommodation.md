# Tennessee pregnancy accommodations

**Rule.** A covered employer must provide reasonable accommodations for medical needs from pregnancy, childbirth, or related medical conditions unless the employer demonstrates undue hardship. A covered employer must not force leave when it can provide another reasonable accommodation or take adverse action for requesting or using a reasonable accommodation.

**Exact text.**

> “fifteen (15) or more employees” (T.C.A. § 50-10-102(2) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “medical needs arising from pregnancy, childbirth, or related medical conditions” (T.C.A. § 50-10-103(b)(1) — unofficial Justia mirror)

> “no later than one (1) year” (T.C.A. § 50-10-104(c) — unofficial Justia mirror)

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Coverage starts at 15 employees (T.C.A. § 50-10-102(2)). Undue hardship means significant difficulty or expense (subsection (4)).
- Options include accessible facilities, flexible breaks, private pumping space outside a bathroom stall, food and drink changes, seating, lifting limits or help, a temporary vacant-position transfer, available job restructuring or light duty, equipment changes, modified schedules, and flexible prenatal visits (T.C.A. § 50-10-102(3)(A)–(K)).
- The private civil action must start within 1 year from termination or the adverse employment action (T.C.A. § 50-10-104(c)).

**Exceptions.**

- Where comparable employees would not receive them, the office need not hire extra workers, displace another worker, create a position, pay for additional break time that is otherwise unpaid, or build a dedicated permanent pumping space (T.C.A. § 50-10-103(a)). Check federal rules separately.
- If the office requires comparable certification from other employees with medical conditions, it may request certification for the specified transfer, restructuring, light duty, or time-away requests. Begin the good-faith interactive process during the employee's good-faith effort to obtain it, and do not take adverse action related to the request in that period (T.C.A. § 50-10-103(c)).

**Office checklist.**

- At each request, record the work limitation, discuss workable options, and send the plan to the owner for approval.
- At the agreed review date and when duties change, ask whether the accommodation still works.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
