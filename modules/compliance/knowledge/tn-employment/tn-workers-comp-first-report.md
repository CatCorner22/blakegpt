# First report of a work injury or illness

**Rule.** A covered insured employer must report a known or reported work injury or illness to its adjuster under the Bureau’s reporting guidance. The adjusting entity must submit the first report to the Bureau for injuries that meet the reporting rule. The employer must provide the medical panel when the worker expresses a need for care.

**Exact text.**

> “within one (1) working day of the employer’s knowledge of the injury” (Tennessee Bureau of Workers’ Compensation, Reporting a Claim, Employers’ Responsibilities (agency guidance))

> “no later than fourteen (14) calendar days after the report by an employer” (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-02-14-.04(4)(b) — Cornell LII mirror)

> “on or before the fifteenth (15th) day of the month following the month in which the injury occurred” (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-02-14-.04(4)(a) — Cornell LII mirror)

> “no later than three (3) business days after receipt of such request” (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-02-01-.06(1) — Cornell LII mirror)

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- Employer to adjuster: send Form C-20 within 1 working day of learning of the injury. The Bureau also directs employers to report immediately (Bureau Reporting a Claim, agency guidance).
- Adjuster to Bureau: report as soon as possible if there is medical treatment, restricted work, inability to work, or death. With 7 calendar days of disability or fewer, report by day 15 of the next month. For death or no return to work within 7 calendar days of injury, report within 14 calendar days after the employer's report (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-02-14-.04(4)(a)–(b)).
- The employee's written notice when the employer lacks actual notice generally must be given promptly and within 15 days of injury for compensation. A separate 15-day trigger applies to gradual injury (T.C.A. § 50-6-201(a)–(b)).
- Give the worker a medical panel as soon as practicable, within 3 business days after the combination of injury notice and a request for care. Provide immediate help in an emergency (Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-02-01-.06(1)–(4)).
- Public Chapters 705 and 845 address the Bureau system and claims proceedings, including fee awards. The employer and adjuster reporting periods above come from agency instructions and the reporting rule (2026 Tenn. Pub. Acts chs. 705, 845).

**Exceptions.**

- Minor first-aid-only injuries need not be reported to the Bureau under Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 0800-02-14-.04(4)(c). Follow the Bureau’s separate employer-to-carrier reporting guidance.
- A self-insured employer directs the claim to its administrator or claims program (Bureau Reporting a Claim, agency guidance).

**Office checklist.**

- At each injury, arrange emergency care first if needed, then record the time the office learned of the injury and contact the carrier immediately. Have the owner verify the C-20 submission, treatment panel, and adjuster receipt.

**Records to keep.** No retention rule in this source.
