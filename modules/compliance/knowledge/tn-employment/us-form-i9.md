# Federal Form I-9 for newly hired employees

**Rule.** An employer must complete Form I-9 for covered new hires and verify identity and employment authorization. An employer must accept qualifying documentation under the federal process and must retain the completed form for the prescribed period.

**Exact text.**

> “within three business days of the hire” (8 CFR 274a.2(b)(1)(ii))

> “whichever is later” (8 U.S.C. § 1324a(b)(3)(B) — Cornell LII mirror)

**Thresholds and deadlines.**

- The employee completes the required attestation at hire. The employer generally completes verification within 3 business days of hire (8 CFR 274a.2(b)(1)(i)–(ii)).
- If the job lasts fewer than 3 business days, complete employer verification at hire (8 CFR 274a.2(b)(1)(iii)).

**Exceptions.**

- Use the federal rules for qualifying receipts and DHS-authorized alternative examination procedures if applicable (8 CFR 274a.2(b)(1)(ii), (vi)).
- State document and E-Verify requirements may also apply.

**Office checklist.**

- At each hire, start Form I-9 and have the designated verifier sign and date the employer section. At separation, have the owner or payroll lead calculate the destruction date.

**Records to keep.** Keep Form I-9 until the later of 3 years after hire or 1 year after termination (8 U.S.C. § 1324a(b)(3)(B)).
